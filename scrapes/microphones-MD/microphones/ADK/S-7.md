## CardioidADK S-7

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The S-7 is a fixed-cardioid mic designed for high-SPL applications, from rock vocal to kick drum. The output circuit is transformerless.

A three-way onboard pad offers up to 18dB attenuation, for a max SPL of 150dB. A three-way HPF can cut the low end at 100Hz or 150Hz.

The mic ships in a black wooden jewel case with a metal “ring mount.” An elastic shockmount is available separately (p/n ADKMountBaskBlk).

ADK subsequently released “mellower” version of this mic, called the S-7B. The capsule and housing are the same; the ‘B’ version has a different EQ circuit built into the preamplifier.
