## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The ADK S-7B is a large-diaphragm transformerless FET condenser microphone derived from the S-7, a high-SPL instrument microphone that shares the 7B’s capsule and circuitry.

The S-7B differs from the S-7 in tone, because the S-7B has an EQ circuit hardwired into the amplifier to produce a smoother, “mellower” tone that has been compared to the sound of ribbon microphones. It was originally intended for brass recordings where high SPL capability and mellow tone would be appropriate, and was produced as a “[one-off](http://recforums.prosoundweb.com/index.php/mv/msg/20292/0/0/18521/#msg_num_11)” within ADK’s Custom Shop. The S-7B was officially added to the product line in 2008.

The S-7B ships with a black wooden jeweler’s case and a metal “ring mount.” Available separately is ADK’s “Pro Kit,” which includes a Neutrik XLR cable, a pop filter, a foam windscreen, an elastic shockmount, and a metal flightcase (with room for the wooden mic case).
