## Wide Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

A small pencil condenser, the SC-1 provides a fixed cardioid (characterized by the vendor as “wide cardioid”) pickup pattern.

Available configurations:

* **SC-1 DLX** - Microphone / Mic Clip / Vinyl Pouch / in Box
* **SC-1 PRO** - Microphone / Mic Clip / Vinyl Pouch / in Small Aluminum Flight Case

The [ADK SC-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ADK/SC-1) is also known as: SC1.
