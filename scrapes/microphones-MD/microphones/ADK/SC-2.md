## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The SC-2 is a true condenser mic with a fixed 20mm cardioid capsule, applicable for all the classic small-diaphragm condenser applications such as drum overheads, hi-hat, acoustic guitar or other fretted instruments.

The onboard switched -10/-20dB pad allows for high SPL applications. Similarly, the onboard switched HPF (75Hz or 150 Hz) allows for versatility in the studio.

This mic appears to be a rebadged Feilo CR1-14.

The [ADK SC-2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ADK/SC-2) is also known as: SC2.
