## X/Y StereoNady SCM-2090

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The SCM-2090 is an XY-stereo FET condenser microphone, with two 22mm (not quite “large”) diaphragm condenser capsules mounted in a 90° array.

The mic has a 5-pin XLRM connector, and is supplied with a Y-cable providing two standard XLR jacks. Also included is a soft pouch. Optional accessories, available separately, include the SSM-3 spider shockmount, MBM-7 mic mount, SMCC-2 flight case, FW-2 windscreen, and more.

The [Nady SCM-2090](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/SCM-2090) is also known as: SCM2090, 2090.

The mic was released in 2008.
