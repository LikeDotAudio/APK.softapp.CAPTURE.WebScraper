## Bidirectional Active Ribbon Microphone

The RSM-8A is a large-geometry Chinese-made ribbon mic in the style of Nady’s [RSM-2](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/RSM-2), but with a phantom-powered preamp circuit built into the mic body — as well as a switched -10dB pad (on a ribbon!) and HPF.

THe 50mm long ribbon qualifies this mic as a [long ribbon, short path](http://recordinghacks.com/2008/11/01/chinese-ribbon-microphone-designs/) ribbon architecture.

The [Nady RSM-8A](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/RSM-8A) is also known as: RSM8, RSM-8, RSM8A.

The mic was released in 2008.
