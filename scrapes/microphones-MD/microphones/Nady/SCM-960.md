## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Nady SCM-960 is an inexpensive large-diaphragm FET condenser. Onboard switches provide access to a 3-way pad (0dB, -10dB, -20dB) and a two-way pattern-selector (omni, cardioid).

[![SCM-960 Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/nady/scm960-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/nady/scm960-pcb.jpg)The mic’s circuit is transformerless, but is not obviously the common Chinese version of the Schoeps design.

PCB photo by Jonathan Rooke

The [Nady SCM-960](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/SCM-960) is also known as: SCM960.
