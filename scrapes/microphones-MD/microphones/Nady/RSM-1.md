## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The RSM-1 “Ribbon Studio Microphone” is a Chinese-made passive ribbon mic, based on a low-tension, 50mm x 5mm aluminum ribbon measuring 2 microns in thickness. The same basic ribbon assembly can be found in numerous other “long ribbon/short path” mics, such as Alctron’s [HRM-8](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HRM-8B), aka the [Apex 205](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/205); find a full explanation of the [three motors designs found in Chinese ribbon mics](http://recordinghacks.com/2008/11/01/chinese-ribbon-microphone-designs/) elsewhere on this site.

The RSM-1 appears to be a rebadged [Alctron HRM-7A](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HRM-7). It is resold under various brand names (although you’ll find subtle differences in the mount, grill size and shape).

A confusing discrepancy in the mic’s SPL specification has been reproduced by numerous resellers. The HRM-7A specs state the max SPL (at 1% THD @ 1000 Hz) as 148dB, and this is echoed in the user guide of the RSM-1, but Nady’s marketing blurb gives a value of 165 dB.

The mic ships with a soft case and integrated stand mount.

The RSM-1’s retail price has not changed since its [introduction](http://news.harmony-central.com/Newp/2005/RSM-1-3-4-5.html); MSRP remains at about $199, with a street price of $160.

The [Nady RSM-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/RSM-1) is also known as: RSM1.

The mic was released in 2005.
