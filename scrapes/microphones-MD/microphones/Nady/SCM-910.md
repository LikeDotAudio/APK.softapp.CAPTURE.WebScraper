## CardioidNady SCM-910

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The SCM-910 is an SCM-900 with the addition of a switchable high-pass filter (-6dB/octave @ 100Hz) and a -10dB preattenuation pad. Otherwise, the specs match its sibling mic.

The mic ships in a vinyl pouch. Optional accessories include an aluminum flight case (p/n SMCC-2), a shockmount (p/n SSM-3), and a foam windscreen (p/n FM-2).

As with the SCM-900, the early 910s were transformer-coupled, and likely changed without notice to a transformerless design around 2002.

The [Nady SCM-910](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/SCM-910) is also known as: SCM910.
