## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The TCM-1150 is a variable-pattern, dual-diaphragm, large diaphragm tube condenser mic that is a favorite starting point for mic modders.

It is manufactured in China, although as with all outsourced manufacturing it is impossible to state specifically which factory might have produced the mic at any time. Indeed, it is likely the supplier has changed since the mic’s introduction, and maybe for each production run.

The mic uses a 6072/12AT7 tube, a 1-inch diaphragm that is almost certainly a Chinese-made K67-style (center-terminated, dual-diaphragm) capsule, and a 9-way pattern selection switch on the power supply.

See also our [Apex 460 mods page](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/460).

The [Nady TCM1150](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/TCM1150) is also known as: TCM-1150.
