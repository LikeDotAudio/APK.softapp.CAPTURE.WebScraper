## OmnidirectionalNady CM 100

## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The CM 100 is an omnidirectional electret condenser mic in the traditional measurement-mic form factor.

The published frequency graph shows less than 1dB of deviation from 30Hz to 20kHz. The mic’s low-end frequency response is at -2dB @ 20Hz.

![Nady CM 100 polar graph](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/graphs/nady/CM-100-polar-graph.png)The mic’s polar plot shows a decay in off-axis response at high frequencies. For example, at 4kHz the response is nearly omni, whereas at 8kHz the response is down 5dB at 180°. At 16kHz, the off-axis (180°) response is down nearly 15dB.

![Nady CM 100 kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/nady/CM-100-kit.jpg)The mic ships with a plastic mic clip, a foam windscreen (p/n FW-1/100), and a foam-lined plastic carrying case (p/n SMCC-1).

The [Nady CM 100](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/CM-100) is also known as: CM100.

The mic was released in 2003.
