## Chameleon Labs

Chameleon Labs manufactures a small line of highly-regarded pro audio gear, including compressors, preamps, and microphones.

The company’s three microphone models — TS-1, TS-2, and TS-1 Mk II — were designed in the US by Terry Setter. Manufacturing of Chameleon Labs products is done in China.

In 2011, the company is introducing a line of 500-series compatible modules.
