## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The TS-2 is a large-diaphragm multipattern tube mic. Like its SDC brother (Chameleon’s TS-1), the TS-2 was designed by and named for [Terry Setter](http://www.evergreen.edu/faculty/sfaculty.htm#settert), a faculty member of Evergreen State College and occasional writer for TapeOp.

The mic’s one-inch capsule has two diaphragms; a switch on the power supply allows selection of an infinite number of polar patterns ranging from omni to figure-8.

We have received conflicting data about the mic’s capsule, but based on its source we believe it to be a 34mm capsule with 27mm diaphragms. The diaphragm thickness is 3 microns.

Like the TS-1, the TS-2 is manufactured in China, using locally-sourced parts — with the exception of a single “exotic” capacitor, used as the final decoupling cap before the transformer. Chameleon provides this capacitor to the Chinese factory.

Also like the TS-1, the TS-2 includes an over-engineered tube power supply, designed by Terry Setter. It provides a unique feature: the heater voltage is controllable by the user, allowing the mic’s character or warmth to be adjusted to best complement the source — at a cost of potentially overdriving the vacuum tube.

Included:

* TS-2 Microphone
* Dedicated power supply
* 25' 7-pin power cable
* Suspension shockmount
* Aluminum case
* windscreen

The TS-2 can be special ordered with a hand-selected tube (rather than the Chinese 12AT7). Chameleon Labs also offers “circuit changes to enhance [the mic’s] sonic character for discriminating users.”

The [Chameleon Labs TS-2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Chameleon-Labs/TS-2) is also known as: TS2.
