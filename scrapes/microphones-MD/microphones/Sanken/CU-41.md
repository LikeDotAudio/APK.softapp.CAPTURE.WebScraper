## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The CU-41 is a unique dual-capsule condenser microphone designed for accurate and neutral sound reproduction. It was developed by Sanken and NHK, the Japanese Broadcasting Corporation, between 1979 and 1982.

[![CU-41 Capsule Assembly](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/sanken/CU41-capsules-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/sanken/CU41-capsules.jpg)The mic is one of a very few “two-way” condensers ever made. It has two separate capsules, each optimized for different frequency ranges. A small-diameter capsule, mounted near the top of the headbasket, picks up high-frequency information, while a large-diaphragm capsule carries low to mid frequencies. The output of the two capsules is mixed into a single mono output with a flat and extended frequency response. The crossover point is at 1kHz.

Detailed capsule size specifications are not available at this time, although Sanken has published a description of the diaphragms: both are 1-micron titanium. This composition makes the mic less susceptible to sonic changes due to temperature, humidity, or corrosion over time.

Both capsules are edge-terminated.

The mic has a transformer-coupled output circuit. (The CU-44X is essentially a transformerless version of the CU-41.)

The mic’s frequency response is described as “totally flat” by Sanken. The published plot shows approximately 2dB of variation from 20Hz to 22kHz, a smooth dip of -5dB centered at 35Hz, and a slight rise beyond that.

[![](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/sanken/CU-41-shockmount-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/sanken/CU-41-shockmount.jpg)The CU-41 has a satin nickel finish. It ships with an elastic shockmount (Sanken p/n S-41) and a storage pouch. An optional aluminum flightcase (p/n TC-41) holds two CU-41 mics with a pair of S-41 shockmounts.

Note that the CU-41 [cannot be disassembled without a special tool](http://groups.google.com/group/rec.audio.pro/msg/21cb6ab62a50d8d3?dmode=source).

The [Sanken Microphone Company, Ltd. CU-41](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sanken/CU-41) is also known as: CU41.

The mic was released in 1982.
