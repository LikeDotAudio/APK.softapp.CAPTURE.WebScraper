## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The ECM-84 is Gauge’s low-cost pencil condenser microphone. It provides Cardioid and Omnidirectional pickup patterns via a pair of interchangeable capsules.

[![Gauge ECM-84 Shockmount](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/gauge/ECM84-mount-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/gauge/ECM84-mount.jpg)The capsules are true condensers (externally biased), and are believed to have 22mm diaphragms.

Two switches on the microphone body provide access to a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter (slope and cutoff frequency unknown). These switches differentiate the ECM-84 from some competitive models; the [MXL 604](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/604), for example, has a single switch that can engage the pad or filter, but not both, whereas the [Advanced Audio CM-54](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Advanced-Audio-Microphones/CM-54) provides a two-position pad and two-position filter.

The ECM-84 bears a superficial similarity to the [Apex 185](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/185) and Alctron HST-02A, but boasts lower self-noise than both.

[![Gauge ECM-84 Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/gauge/ECM84-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/gauge/ECM84-kit.jpg)The ECM-84 ships with an elastic shockmount, two interchangeable capsules — one cardioid, one omni — and a padded wooden storage box.
