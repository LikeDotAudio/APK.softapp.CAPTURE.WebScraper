## Apex Electronics

Apex Electronics, a division of Canadian audio manufacturer Yorkville Sound, provides a wide array of microphone and audio electronics products. Apex-branded microphones are manufactured in Asia.

The mic products tend to be priced at the low end of the spectrum, and as such are favorites for mic modders. See, as a primary example, the Apex 460 tube mic.
