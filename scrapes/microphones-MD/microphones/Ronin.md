## Ronin Applied Sciences

Founded by Dimitri Wolfwood and Fenton “Kobayashi” Joseph, Ronin Applied Sciences is a new boutique microphone manufacturer whose sights are set on the high end of the microphone market. The company’s first product, the Pegasus tube microphone, incorporated numerous innovative components and methodologies, but never met its initial production quota of 100 units due to the limited availability of critical components.

Subsequent products are designed to be produced in unlimited quantities.
