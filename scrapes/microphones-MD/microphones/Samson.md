## Samson

Samson was founded in 1982 and has introduced a number of innovative audio products since then, such as dbx noise reduction within wireless instrument transmission systems. Samson is the worldwide distributor for Hartke bass amps and cabs and the US distributor for Zoom digital-recorder products.

The company currently sells a wide variety of musical equipment, from mic clips and cables to power amplifiers and outboard effects.
