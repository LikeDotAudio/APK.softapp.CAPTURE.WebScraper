## CardioidAseyer Electric Technology Nanjing Co D36B Pro

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Aseyer D36B is a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone with a transformer-coupled output circuit.

[![D36B Pro Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-capsule-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-capsule.jpg)The capsule is Aseyer’s proprietary large-diaphragm design. It is an edge-terminated, 36mm-diameter capsule with a single diaphragm made of 4-micron aluminum foil. It was designed to withstand the effects of humidity without compromising the mic’s performance.

[![D36B Pro](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-cutaway-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-cutaway.jpg)The published frequency-response graph is nominally flat from 40Hz to 1kHz, with a broad and flat presence rise peaking at +4dB from 5–11kHz.

The polar graph shows only a single trace. The mic’s pickup pattern at this frequency (presumably 1kHz) is a slightly narrow Cardioid.

[![D36B Pro Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/aseyer/D36B-circuit.jpg)The mic’s circuit is a simple design, using only about a dozen components (including the large output transformer).

The mic was released in 2010.
