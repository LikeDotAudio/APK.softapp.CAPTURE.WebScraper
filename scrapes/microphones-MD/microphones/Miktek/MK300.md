## CardioidMiktek MK300

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Miktek MK300 is a multipattern condenser microphone. It is Miktek’s first import product, and the company’s entry-level microphone. The MK300 was specifically designed for project studios.

Like all Miktek microphones, the MK300 has a transformer. In this case, the transformer — called the T-301 — is manufactured overseas.

A switch on the front of the microphone enables selection of 3 polar patterns (cardioid, figure 8, omni). On the rear, two switches provide a low cut shelf of -12db/octave at 100Hz, and a -10db pad.

The MK300 comes with a shock mount (SMS3), a swivel mount, and a vinyl bag.

The mic was released in 2015.
