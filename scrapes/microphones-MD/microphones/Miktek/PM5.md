## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Miktek PM5 is a condenser microphone designed for handheld use on stage. It shares the capsule, electronics, and output transformer as Miktek’s C5 small-diaphragm studio microphone.

The cardioid capsule has a half-inch diaphragm made of 5-micron Mylar, metallized with gold. The capsules are manufactured in China to Miktek’s specifications. The transformer is from AMI (model T5). The mic has a built-in (non-switched) high-pass filter at 80Hz.

The PM5 includes a camera-style carry case, mic bag, and clip.

The mic was released in 2016.
