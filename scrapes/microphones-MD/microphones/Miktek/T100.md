## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

The Miktek T100 is a tom- and snare-specific mic in Miktek’s “T” series. It is a supercardioid dynamic mic that uses Miktek’s proprietary transformer.

The T100 has a two-stage mounting clip that both screws to clamp on a drum rim, and locks down with a lever. The mic can also be removed from the rim clip and used on a conventional stand if desired.

Though details are unavailable, Miktek states that the capsule sits on top of a “unique labyrinth design” that facilitates greater feedback rejection.

The T100 is part of Miktek’s TDK5 drum mic kit, alongside the T200 kick drum mic.

The T100 is sold with a drum rim mount (LRM100) and storage bag.

The [Miktek T100](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Miktek/T100) is also known as: T-100.

The mic was released in 2018.
