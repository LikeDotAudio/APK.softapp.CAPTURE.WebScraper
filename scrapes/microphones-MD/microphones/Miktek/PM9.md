## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PM9 is a stage vocal microphone with a supercardioid polar pattern.

The moving-coil cartridge is a custom design, according to Miktek, and was developed in parallel with the AMI output transformer to produce the desired voicing.

The mic’s body is made of die-cast metal. The grille is a multi-layer steel mesh.

The PM9 ships with a storage case and a mic clip.
