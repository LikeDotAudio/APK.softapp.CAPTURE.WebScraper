## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Stedman LD50 is a large-diaphragm, low-impedance dynamic microphone. The mic is design for use where feedback is a concern. The supercardioid polar pattern exhibits excellent off-axis rejection, to allow for greater stage volume as well as reduced pickup of surrounding instruments and speakers.

The frequency response of the LD50 is very smooth with a slight rise above 5 kHz to give increased presence to the subject.

The output of the LD50 is low impedance and will provide best results when used with a balanced low impedance mixer or preamp. The body of the LD50 is cast from a heavy metal alloy to reduce handling noise and is finished with a non-glare durable hard coating.

This product carries a full two year warranty on parts and labor.
