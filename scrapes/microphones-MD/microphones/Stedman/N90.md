## CardioidStedman N90

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The N90’s sound is achieved with a precision large-diameter mylar diaphragm and powerful Alnico/Iron magnet assembly. This efficient dynamic design yields a warm sound and very smooth frequency response. The N90s transformerless design offers exceptional transient response and is capable of handling sound pressure levels in excess of 155dB without distortion. The N90 does not require phantom power or batteries.

Hand built in the U.S.A. Includes stand adapter clip, foam lined hard case and 2 year warranty.
