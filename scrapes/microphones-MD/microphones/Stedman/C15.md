## CardioidStedman C15

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Stedman C15 is a nearly-large-diaphragm studio condenser microphone with studio optimized cardioid pattern and “super buffered” ultra low noise electronics.

The C15’s cardioid polar pattern exhibits excellent off-axis rejection to allow for easy operation in studio situations where room ambience is not always ideal. The capsule assembly utilizes a lightweight gold-sputtered membrane measuring just under 1 inch in diameter.

The screen assembly is designed to reduce pops on vocals and allow maximum sound passage with minimum coloration. The C15 housing is precision machined aluminum with a brass inner shield designed to minimize hum, maximize vibration isolation, and guaranty an accurate mechanical fit. The C15 operates from standard 48V phantom power and is finished with a non-glare durable hard anodized coating. This product carries a full two year warranty on parts and labor.

Included Accessories: Foam lined hard case and dual knob adjustable adapter clip.
