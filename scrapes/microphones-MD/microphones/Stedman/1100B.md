## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Stedman 1100B is a hand-built large-diaphragm tube condenser microphone with 11 selectable patterns.

The 1100B large diameter dual capsule is a precision German manufactured assembly with ultra light weight (3 to 4 micron) gold-sputtered membrane. The preamp section uses an individually-tested 12AY7 twin triode tube with large high voltage metal film capacitors and 1% precision film resistors.

This preamp design is hand wired with meticulous precision, on point to point Teflon boards to guarantee consistent optimum performance. The 11 selectable patterns range from omni, figure-eight to cardioid and are selectable from the included power supply.

The efficient low EMF toroid power supply transformer will ensure low noise and reliability. Made in U.S.A.

Included: Wooden plush lined case, adjustable nickel-plated shockmount and 25’ Mogami cable. Two year warranty on parts and labor.

The [Stedman 1100B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Stedman/1100B) is also known as: 1100.
