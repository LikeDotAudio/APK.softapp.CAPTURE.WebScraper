## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Stedman LD23 is a special design large diaphragm low impedance dynamic microphone. The performance of the LD23 microphone is ideal for use where feedback is a concern. The super cardioid polar pattern exhibits excellent off axis rejection to allow for greater stage volume as well as reduced pick up of surrounding instruments and speakers. The LD23 employs an effective internal wind filter to eliminate excessive pops during close up vocal applications. The frequency response of the LD23 exhibits a slight rise above 3 kHz to give increased presence to the subject.

The output of the LD23 is low impedance and will provide best results when used with a balanced low impedance mixer or preamp. The body of the LD23 is cast from a heavy metal alloy to reduce handling noise and is finished with a non-glare durable hard coating.

This product carries a full two year warranty on parts and labor.
