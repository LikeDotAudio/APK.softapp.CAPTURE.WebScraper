## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

* Hypercardioid microphone for use in noisy environments
* Small and inconspicuous
* Noise canceling close-talking microphone
* Extremely rugged

A special, hypercardioid close-talking dynamic microphone for talkback use in professional studios.

Small and lightweight, the D 58 has an integral XLR connector for mounting on a gooseneck or mic cable. Finish is satin nickel.

The D 58 is small and inconspicuous, extremely rugged noise-canceling microphone.

The [AKG Acoustics D 58](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-58) is also known as: D58, D 58.
