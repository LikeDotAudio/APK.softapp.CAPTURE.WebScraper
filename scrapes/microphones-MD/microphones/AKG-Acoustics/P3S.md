## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The AKG P3S is a handheld cardioid dynamic microphone for stage vocal use.

The mic features a rugged wire mesh grille with internal windscreen, die-cast metal body and integrated ON/OFF switch.

It appears to be identical to the D 77 S dynamic vocal microphone; the two published frequency-response charts are identical.

The P3 ships with stand adapter and zippered pouch.

The [AKG Acoustics P 3 S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/P3S) is also known as: P3.

The mic was released in 2011.
