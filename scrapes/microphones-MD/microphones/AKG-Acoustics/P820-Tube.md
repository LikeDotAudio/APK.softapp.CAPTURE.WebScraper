## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The P820 is a reincarnation of the Perception 820 Tube microphone, with slightly different specs, a slightly revised circuit, and a black paint job.

The mic has a large-diaphragm K67-type capsule, a tube-based impedance conversion circuit, and a transformer-coupled output.

For more information, see the Perception 820 profile (linked from the sidebar).

The [AKG Acoustics P820 Tube](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/P820-Tube) is also known as: P820.

The mic was released in 2014.
