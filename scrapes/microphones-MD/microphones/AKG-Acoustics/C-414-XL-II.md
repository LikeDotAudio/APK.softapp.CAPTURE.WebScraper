## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The C414 XL II is a multipattern FET condenser microphone built around AKG’s famed CK12 capsule. It is the latest in the TLII/XLII series of studio microphones, which feature a presence boost rather than a neutral frequency response. The primary new feature of the revised mic is that it offers 9 polar patterns, rather than the B-XL II’s five.

[![C414 B-XLII and XLII](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/XLII-old-new-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/akg/XLII-old-new.jpg)Pattern for pattern, the frequency response of the XL II matches that of the B-XL II. The self-noise is the same, 6dBA. The new mic is slightly less sensitive (20mV/Pa) than the B-XL II (23mV/Pa).

The pattern, pad, and filter are selected via pushbutton controls on the body of the microphone. Current selections are indicated by LEDs. As on the B-XL II, the controls can be locked to prevent accidental reconfiguration during handling.

The clip indicator LED introduced on the B-XLS and B-XL II is improved on the 2009 models (XLS and XL II), in that the LED stays lit for 3 seconds.

[![C 414 XLS Cutaway View](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/C414XLS-cutaway-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/akg/C414XLS-cutaway.jpg)Like the B-XL II, the new XL II employs surface-mount components within its circuit boards. (Pictured is the XLS, which shares much of the circuitry of the XL II.)

The amplifier circuit is a low-noise design, making the mic one of the lowest-noise mics we have seen (6dBA in all polar patterns).

[![C 414 XLII Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/XLII-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/akg/XLII-kit.jpg)The C414 XL II ships with a low-profile shockmount (AKG p/n H 85), a foam windscreen (W414), a detachable pop filter (PF80), and an aluminum “Soundtool” flight case.

The C 414 XL II is also available in matched stereo pairs. AKG’s matching criteria requires the two microphones to produce frequency response within 1dB, between 300Hz–8000Hz. The stereo kit, AKG p/n C 414 XL II/ST, includes two shockmounts, two windscreens, and a stereo mounting bar, and ships in an oversized Soundtool flightcase. (No pop filter is included.)
