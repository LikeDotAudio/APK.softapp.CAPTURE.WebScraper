## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Perception 400 is a multipattern large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone from AKG’s low-cost Perception series. It was replaced in AKG’s product line by the Perception 420, which changed the pad value to -20dB.

The 400 has a dual-diaphragm capsule that provides three polar patterns, selectable from a switch on the front of the microphone body: Cardioid, Omnidirectional, Figure of 8.

> AKG
>
> The Perception 220, 420, 820, 100, 200 and 400 share the same capsule platform which is is one-inch, center-terminated and true condenser.

Like the rest of the large-diaphragm Perception microphones, the Perception 400 uses an output transformer.

The mic has a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter; both can be enabled separately via a pair of switches on the rear of the microphone body.

Perception 400’s chassis is made of precision-tooled zinc/aluminum alloy and has a stainless steel grille screen that is dent-resistant. It comes complete with a spider-type shock mount in a metal carrying case.

AKG’s Perception microphones were designed in Vienna, and are manufactured in China “under strict quality control” (according to AKG).

The [AKG Acoustics Perception 400](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/Perception-400) is also known as: Perception400.

The mic was released in 2006.
