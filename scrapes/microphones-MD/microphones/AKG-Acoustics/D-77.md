## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The D 77 S in an inexpensive dynamic microphone intended for live vocal applications. It was part of AKG’s CCS, “Crystal Clear Sound” series.

The mic has a die-cast metal body, a wire mesh cap, and an on/off switch. It shipped in one of two configurations:

* **D 77 S/XLR** - with 5m XLR cable
* **D 77 S/Jack** - with 5m conversion cable, XLR3F-to-1/4'' audio jack

Both packages include a mic clip.

The [AKG Acoustics D 77 S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-77) is also known as: D 77 S, D77S, D77, D 77.
