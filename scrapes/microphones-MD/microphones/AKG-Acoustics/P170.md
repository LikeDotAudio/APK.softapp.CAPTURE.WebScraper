## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The P170 is a simple Cardioid pencil condenser. It is the 3rd-generation pencil mic from AKG’s “Perception” line of project-studio microphones.

Like its immediate predecessor (the Perception 170), the P170 has a half-inch “true condenser” (externally polarized) capsule.

Another feature retained from the Perception 170 is the switchable 20dB pad.

*How does the P170 differ from the Perception 170?*

1. The P170 has higher sensitivity, 15mV/Pa vs 12mV/Pa.
2. The P170 has lower self-noise, 19dBA vs 21dBA.
3. The P170 has a black finish, rather than metallic silver/blue.
4. The P170 MSRP is roughly half the price of the Perception 170: $129 vs. $279.

The P170 ships with a hard mount.
