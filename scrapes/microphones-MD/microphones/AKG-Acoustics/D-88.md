## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The D 88 S is an inexpensive dynamic microphone intended for stage vocal applications.

It has a metal diecast body, a metal-mesh grille, and an on/off switch. Its polar pattern is Supercardioid, which gives it better side rejection than a Cardioid design.

The mic is part of AKG’s CCS series, which is largely discontinued (but reborn as Perception Live).

Two configurations are available:

* **D 88 S/XLR** - includes 5m XLR3F-XLR3M (standard microphone) cable
* **D 88 S/Jack** - includes 5m conversion cable, XLR3F to 1/4'' male audio jack

The [AKG Acoustics D 88 S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-88) is also known as: D 88 S, D88S, D88, D 88.
