## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The D3800 is a handheld dynamic microphone intended for live vocal reproduction. It uses AKG’s “Varimotion” laminate diaphragm technology, and a triangular “TriPower” body.

The mic’s moving-coil capsule is suspended within the mic body to reduce mechanical noise. A hum suppression coil helps reduce EMI/RFI (radio or electronic interference).

> AKG
>
> The VARIMOTION diaphragm is the first microphone diaphragm ever made whose thickness varies across its diameter. This allows the diaphragm itself to be tuned to the desired resonance frequency for a quantum leap in audio performance.

The package includes the microphone, a stand adapter and a hard shell road case.

The [AKG Acoustics D 3800](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-3800) is also known as: D3800, D 3800.
