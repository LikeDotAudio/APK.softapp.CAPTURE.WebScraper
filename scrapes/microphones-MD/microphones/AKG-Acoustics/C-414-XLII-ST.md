## AKG Acoustics C 414 XL II/ST

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The C 414 XL II/ST is one of two stereo pairs of AKG’s current (2012) two models of C414. The XL II is the gold-grille, presence-boosted version.

The two XL II’s in this kit are computer-matched to within 1dB from 300–8kHz in Cardioid mode.

The stereo kit includes the two mics, two universal shockmounts (AKG model H 85 ), an adjustable-width stereo bar (model H 50), a pair of SA 60 mic clips, and two W414 windscreens, all packed into a large-size “Soundtool” padded flightcase.
