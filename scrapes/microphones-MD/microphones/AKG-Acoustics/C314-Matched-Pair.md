## AKG Acoustics C314 Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The C314 Matched Pair includes two of AKG’s C 314 large-diaphragm condenser microphones, selected to provide “virtually identical” sensitivity and the “highest possible correlation” in frequency response.

[![C314 Matched Pair](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/c314-set-sm.jpg)](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/akg/c314-set.jpg)Two H85 shockmounts, two SA60 hard mounts, two W214 windscreens, and one H50 stereo bar are included in the kit. The two mics and all accessories are packed into a foam-lined flightcase.
