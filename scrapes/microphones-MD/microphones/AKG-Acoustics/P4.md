## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The AKG P 4 is a compact cardioid dynamic instrument microphone.

It features an all-metal body, integrated stand mount, and a humbucking coil to reduce EMI/RFI noise.

The P4 appears to be identical to the discontinued AKG D 22. The frequency response and polar pattern charts are identical; however, this appears to be due to a shortcut by the marketing team, as AKG claims the P4 has a different moving-coil cartridge than the D22.

![AKG H440 rim clamp](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/akg_h440_clamp.jpg)The P4 ships with an H440 drum rim mount and carrying case.

The [AKG Acoustics P 4](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/P4) is also known as: P4.
