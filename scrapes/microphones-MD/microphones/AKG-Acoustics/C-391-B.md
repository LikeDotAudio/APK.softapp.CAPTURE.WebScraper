## Detachable-Capsule Condenser Microphone

* Switchable bass cut filter and 10-dB preattenuation pad
* Insensitive to handling noise
* High resistance to RF interference
* Capsules mount directly on SE 300 B powering unit or connects via 10-ft. (3-m) cable

Versatile cardioid condenser microphone comprising an SE 300 B powering/output module and CK 91 cardioid capsule, foam windscreen, and stand adapter.

The [AKG Acoustics C 391 B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/C-391-B) is also known as: C391B, C 391 B.
