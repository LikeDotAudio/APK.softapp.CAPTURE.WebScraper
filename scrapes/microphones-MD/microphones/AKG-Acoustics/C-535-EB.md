## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The C535 EB is a high-end handheld condenser microphone designed for vocal and instrumental reproduction, on stage or in the studio.

Somewhat unusually for a handheld design, the 535EB has onboard pad and filter options. The following four configurations are supported:

* no pad, no filter
* high-pass filter: -12dB @ 100Hz
* -14dB pad, no filter
* -14dB pad, high-pass filter: -4dB @ 500Hz

The mic body is made of die-cast zinc alloy, finished in matte black.

The mic ships with a “quick-release” mic clip (AKG p/n SA 61) and a storage pouch.

The [AKG Acoustics C 535 EB](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/C-535-EB) is also known as: C535EB, C 535 EB.
