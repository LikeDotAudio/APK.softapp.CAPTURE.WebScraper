## AKG Acoustics Perception 170 Stereo

## Stereo Mic Set

The Perception 170 Stereo set is a simple stereo pair of AKG’s low-cost Perception 170 pencil mic. It is a no-frills kit, including just the two microphones (factory matched) and two mic clips. No carrying case nor shockmount is included.

The 170 is a small-diaphragm true condenser design, with a switchable -20dB pad.
