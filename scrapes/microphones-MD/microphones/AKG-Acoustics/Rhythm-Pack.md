## AKG Acoustics Rhythm Pack

## 6-Mic Set

The AKG Rhythm Pack is a 6-mic drum kit package. It includes:

* One D112 for kick drum
* Two C430 overheads, with removable foam windscreens for outdoor use
* Three D 40 dynamics for snare and toms

All mics include basic hard mounts. The kit ships in a padded aluminum flightcase.
