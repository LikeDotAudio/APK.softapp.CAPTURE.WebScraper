## AKG Acoustics C 451 B/ST

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The C 451 B/ST is a stereo pair of AKG’s C 451 B FET pencil condensers. The two microphones are matched to within 1dB for both sensitivity and frequency range (between 200Hz –15kHz) in Cardioid.

The kit includes an adjustable-width stereo bar, suitable for XY or ORTF positions, packed into AKG’s medium-size “Soundtools” flightcase. A pair of SA 60 mic clips are included; a variety of shockmounts (e.g. H 30 or H 85) are available separately.
