## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The AKG P 2 is a dynamic microphone in AKG’s new Perception Live series. The mic is designed for kick drum and bass instruments for stage use.

The P2 features an all-metal body, integrated stand mount, and humbucking coil for EMI/RFI noise reduction.

The P2 appears to be identical to the discontinued [AKG D 11](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-11); the two mics are physically identical, and share sensitivity figures and frequency-response graphs. Nonetheless, AKG says the P2 has a different moving-coil cartridge. (We have noted a discrepancy between the P2 cutsheet and website specifications, which we hope to resolve soon.)

The P2 ships with a carrying case. The body has an integrated hard mount.

The [AKG Acoustics P 2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/P2) is also known as: P2.

The mic was released in 2011.
