## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The AKG P 5 is a supercardioid handheld dynamic mic for live vocal use.

The mic is similar to AKG’s D 5 model but lacks the “Varimotion” capsule. It appears to be identical to the D 88 S from the CCS series.

The mic features a rugged wire mesh grille with internal windscreen, and a die-cast metal body.

Two configurations of the mic are available:

* **P 5** - as described above
* **P 5 S** - with on/off switch

Both versions include a storage pouch and a mic clip (AKG p/n SA 45).

The [AKG Acoustics P 5](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/P5) is also known as: P 5 S.

The mic was released in 2011.
