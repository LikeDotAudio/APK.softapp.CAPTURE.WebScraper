## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The D230 is an omnidirectional dynamic mic intended for field reporting and broadcast use.

Features include a durable metal body, an integrated windscreen, and internal circuitry to reduce sensitivity to electromagnetic interference/EMI/hum.

[![Varimotion Capsule Cutaway](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/varimotion_sm.png)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/akg/varimotion_cutaway.jpg)The mic uses AKG’s “Varimotion” diaphragm technology:

> AKG
>
> Varimotion is the first technique that allows the production of a microphone diaphragm whose thickness varies along its diameter.

Stand adapter included (SA 44).

The [AKG Acoustics D 230](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/D-230) is also known as: D230, D 230.
