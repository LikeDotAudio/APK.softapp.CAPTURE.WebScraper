## CardioidCascade Microphones V57

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The V57 is an inexpensive large-diaphragm condenser microphone, manufactured in China. It has a fixed cardioid pattern and onboard switches controlling a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter.

The 27mm capsule is a “true” condenser design.

The mic is sold as a bundle with a pair of Cascade’s M39 small-diaphragm “pencil” condensers. It ships with a suspension shockmount and a micro-fiber cleaning cloth in an aluminum flight case.

Three color combinations are available:

* Silver V57 body with silver grille, $239
* Silver V57 body with gold grille, $259
* Black V57 body with gold grille, $259

The [Cascade Microphones V57](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Cascade/V57) is also known as: V-57.
