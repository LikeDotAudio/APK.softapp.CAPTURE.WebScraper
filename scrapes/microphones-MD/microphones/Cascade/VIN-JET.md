## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The Cascade VIN-JET is a long-format, passive ribbon microphone manufactured in China and upgraded in the US.

The single ribbon is made of 99% pure aluminum and measures approximately 5mm by 62mm. Suspended between Neodymium magnets, it produces an extended figure-of-8 pickup pattern with high (165dB) SPL capability.

Two models are available:

* VIN-JET - with stock transformer, $199
* VIN-JET-L - with Lundahl LL2913 transformer, $349

Both ship with a suspension shockmount, a padded storage pouch, an aluminum flight case, and a micro-fiber cleaning cloth.

The Cascade Vin-Jet (w/Lundahl upgrade) was part of our [$60,000 Ribbon Mic Shootout](http://recordinghacks.com/2011/07/11/60000-ribbon-mic-shootout/).

The [Cascade Microphones VIN-JET](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Cascade/VIN-JET) is also known as: VINJET.
