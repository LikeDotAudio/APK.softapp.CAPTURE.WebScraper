## Cascade Microphones Fat Head Blumlein Package

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The Fat Head Stereo Pair Blumlein package includes two Cascade Fat Head microphones, shockmounts, and a stereo adapter bar that allows Blumlein (crossed Figure-8) mounting.

The kit ships in a padded aluminum flightcase.

The microphones are available with stock (Chinese), Cinemag, or Lundahl transformers, and in three color combinations. The two upgraded-transformer versions sell for $625; all color combinations are priced the same (either $349 or $625 depending on transformers).
