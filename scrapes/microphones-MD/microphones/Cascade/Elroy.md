## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Cascade Elroy is a 3-pattern, dual-large-diaphragm tube mic based on the 6J1 tube. It appears to be a first cousin to CAD’s Trion 8000, and is possibly a rebadged ShuaiYin SYT-3000.

The dual diaphragms measure 32mm in diameter and support three pickup patterns, switched from the mic body (as on the Trion 8000). Omni, Cardioid, and Figure-of-8 pickup patterns are supported.

A “dual-position” switch on the back of the mic body provides access to either the high pass filter or the -10dB pad.

The mic body incorporates a yoke mount; Cascade claims that internal shockmounting of the capsule makes the use of an external shockmount unnecessary.

The mic ships in a padded bag, with a power supply and cable, microfiber cleaning cloth, and an aluminum flight case.

The mic was released in 2008.
