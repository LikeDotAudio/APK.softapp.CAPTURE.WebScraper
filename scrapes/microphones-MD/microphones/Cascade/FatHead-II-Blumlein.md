## Cascade Microphones Fat Head II Blumlein Package

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The Fat Head II Stereo Pair Blumlein package includes two Cascade Fat Head II microphones, shockmounts, and a stereo adapter bar that allows Blumlein (crossed Figure-8) mounting.

The Fat Head II is primarily a cosmetic upgrade from the original Fat Head; the ribbon motors are identical.

The kit ships in a padded aluminum flightcase.

The microphones are available with stock (Chinese), Cinemag, or Lundahl transformers, and in three color combinations. The two upgraded-transformer versions sell for $699; all color combinations are priced the same (either $399 or $699 depending on transformers).
