## Cascade Microphones V57 Bundle

## 3-Mic Set

The Cascade V57 Bundle includes one V57 large-diaphragm FET condenser and a pair of M39 small-diaphragm pencil condensers. It is intended as a first “mic pack” for new recordists, providing in one pack all the microphones necessary to record vocals and acoustic instruments.

The kit includes three elastic shockmounts and two micro-fiber cleaning cloths. It is packed in a foam-lined plastic carrying case.

Two colors are available: black and silver.
