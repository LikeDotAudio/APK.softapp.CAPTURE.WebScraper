## Elation Mic Lab

Elation Microphone Lab is a private company spun off of the Russian Scientific Research Cinema & Photo Institute, better known as [NIKFI](http://www.holography.ru/nikfieng.htm).

The company currently has no US dealers, but their products can occasionally be found on Ebay, sold direct from the factory.
