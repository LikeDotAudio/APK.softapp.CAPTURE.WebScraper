## MXL Kick & Snare Kit

## Stereo Mic Set

The Kick & Snare Kit is a 2-mic set featuring MXL’s A-55 Kicker bass-drum dynamic mic, and the new MXL 606 Condenser, which incorporates both a 20dB pad (giving it a high max SPL) and low-frequency rolloff (to compensate for proximity effect).
