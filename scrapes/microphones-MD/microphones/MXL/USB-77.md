## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The USB-77 is a USB condenser with a retro pill style body and yoke mount.

The USB-77 is class compliant for OSX and Windows and operates at 16-bit (presumably at 44.1kHz sample rate). A gain control on the rear of the microphone controls input gain (before A-to-D conversion). A stereo headphone jack on the rear allows for low latency monitoring.

The mic ships with a microfiber cleaning cloth, USB cable, desktop stand, and aluminum briefcase.

Few specifications for this microphone have been released; we will update this profile as more data is made available.

The [MXL USB-77](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/USB-77) is also known as: USB77.

The mic was released in 2009.
