## CardioidMXL 870

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL 870 is a large-diaphragm condenser billed as an all-purpose “utility” microphone.

It combines familiar MXL elements: the surface-mount transformerless circuit found in the V250 and 910 (and presumably the 2006 and V63M) with the 32mm K67 capsule found in the V63M and 2006 and numerous other models.

[![MXL 870 Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/mxl-870-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/mxl-870-circuit.jpg)It is a good candidate for modification; see the options in the sidebar.

In the sample we tested, the capsule’s front diaphragm was polarized at 50VDC. The mic is a good mod candidate, with replacement capsule options and also a replacement circuit option; see the sidebar listing.

The mic ships with an elastic shockmount in a cardboard box. Spare elastics for the shockmount are included.
