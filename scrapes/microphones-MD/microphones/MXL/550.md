## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL 550 is a small-diaphragm condenser microphone with a transformerless circuit. It is internally identical to the MXL 910, and the new surface-mount MCA SP-1.

[![550](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/550-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/550-pcb.jpg)Current production microphones use surface-mount circuit boards. The circuit itself is MXL’s version of the Schoeps transformerless circuit.

The capsule is MXL’s standard 17.1mm diameter, small-diaphragm design, also found on the MXL 990, 910, MCA SP-1, and others.

As of June 2013, the 550 is sold only as part of MXL’s “550/551R Microphone Ensemble,” a two-mic set that also includes a small-diaphragm condenser (the 551). The two mics ship in a padded plastic lunchbox with a polishing cloth and two hard mounts (one per mic).
