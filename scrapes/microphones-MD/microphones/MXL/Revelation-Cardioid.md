## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The Revelation Cardioid is the name originally used for the third mic in the Revelation product line. It is a fixed Cardioid tube mic with an Electro-Harmonix EF86 tube.

The mic was first announced at Winter NAMM 2012, and is scheduled to begin shipping in March 2012.

However, the mic was never commercially released, until Summer NAMM 2013, at which time it had been renamed as the Revelation Solo.
