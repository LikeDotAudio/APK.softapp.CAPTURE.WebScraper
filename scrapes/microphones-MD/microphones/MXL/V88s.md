## CardioidMXL V88s

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL V88S is a large-diaphragm FET condenser designed for vocals and general studio use. It is an update to the V88; the primary differences are that the V88S is designed to sound “warmer,” and the V88S has a 3-way pad switch.

The pad can be set to 0dB, -10dB, or -20dB; the latter raises the mic’s max SPL to 158dB (0.5% distortion).

Both the V88 and V88S are transformerless, and have fixed Cardioid pickup patterns.

The V88S ships with the MXL06 shockmount and an aluminum flight case.

The mic was released in 2012.
