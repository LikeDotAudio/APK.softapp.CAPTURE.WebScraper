## MXL Studio Drum Kit

## 6-Mic Set

The MXL Studio Drum Kit includes:

* One A-55 dynamic bass-drum mic
* One MXL 606 pencil condenser for snare drum
* Two MXL 603S condensers for overheads, with shockmounts
* Two Cube condensers with ring mounts for toms

The kit ships in a deep aluminum flight case, which houses all the mics and mounts plus separate storage pouches for the A-55 and 603S pair.
