## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL 990S is essentially a 990 with the addition of a switchable pad (-10dB) and high-pass filter (-6dB/octave @ 150Hz). As part of the “900 Series,” the 990S is sold exclusively through Guitar Center and its affiliates.

[![MXL 770 and 990S](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/MXL-990S-770-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/MXL-990S-770.jpg)Aside from the finish colors and the height of the microphone, the MXL 770 is identical to the 990S. The two mics’ MSRPs, as of late 2012, are the same ($150).

The capsule assembly in the 990S can be found in the 770, 990, and MCA SP-1. It is MXL’s 603S/604 small-diaphragm model mounted within a brass baffle ring. The brass ring alters the capsule’s frequency and polar response.

The suspended area of the diaphragm has a 17mm diameter, with a 20mm backplate. The housing that contains these pieces has a 22mm diameter. See the [603S capsule listing](/microphones/tag/603s) for more mics with the same capsule.

[![MXL 770 and 990S PCBs](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/770-990S-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/770-990S-pcb.jpg)The 770/990S circuit is a variation on the Schoeps transformerless design. A simpler version, with no pad and filter, is found in the MXL 990, MCA SP-1, MXL 2006, and MXL V63M.

The MXL 990S ships in a padded rigid plastic case with an elastic shockmount.

The [MXL 990S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/990S) is also known as: 990-S.
