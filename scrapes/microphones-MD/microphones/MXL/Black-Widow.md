## CardioidMXL Black Widow

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Black Widow is a large-diaphragm condenser microphone with a fixed Cardioid pickup pattern.

The mic uses a 32mm 6µ diaphragm, FET circuitry and transformerless output.

A 3-position pad switch activates -10dB and -20dB attenuation.

It ships with a flight case and shockmount.

Permalink: [MXL Black-Widow](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/Black-Widow)

The mic was released in 2012.
