## CardioidMXL BCD-1

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The BCD-1 is a dynamic microphone built for the broadcast market.

Its moving-coil cartridge is shockmounted within the microphone body. Like all broadcast dynamics, this is an end-address design.

The mic has three high-pass filter settings to control low-frequency response and proximity effect.

More details are forthcoming.
