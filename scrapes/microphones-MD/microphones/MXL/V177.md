## CardioidMXL V177

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The V177 is a retro-styled large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone.

The mic has a large-diaphragm, 32mm diameter condenser capsule with a 6-micron diaphragm. It has a fixed cardioid pickup pattern.

The microphone’s self-noise is relatively low at 13dBA. The sensitivity is also somewhat low for modern vocal condensers, at ~9mV/Pa.

The V177 ships with a micro-fiber cleaning cloth and Mogami XLR cable. The mic is packaged in a velvet-lined wooden storage case.

Permalink: [MXL V177](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/V177)

The mic was released in 2010.
