## CardioidMXL 9000

## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The MXL 9000 is a tube condenser microphone built for MXL’s “900 series,” sold exclusively by Guitar Center and its affiliated companies.

The mic has a fixed Cardioid polar pattern. There are no switchable features (such as pad or high-pass filter).

The capsule is a 32mm K67 style, with only one diaphragm metallized. It is visually identical to the capsule from MXL’s other LDC models, such as the 920, V63M, or 2006.  
[![MXL 9000 circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/9000-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/9000-circuit.jpg)

The tube is reportedly a 12AT7. It is mounted behind the single circuit board, with a retaining cap haphazardly glued to the transformer shield.

The mic’s circuit is relatively noisy compared to modern microphones, at 22dBA.

The mic ships with its dedicated power supply, a 15-foot 7-pin cable, and a 15-foot 3-pin XLR cable.
