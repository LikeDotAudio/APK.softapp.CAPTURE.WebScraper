## MXL V67 Recording Kit

## 3-Mic Set

The V67 Recording Kit is a 3-mic set including:

* One V67GS large-diaphragm FET condenser, with both a ring mount and an elastic shockmount
* Two V67N FET pencil mics, with two mic clips and two elastic shockmounts
* Two omni capsules to be used with the V67N

The V67N has two interchangeable capsules; both Cardioid and Omni (for both mics) are included.

The kit ships in an aluminum flight case.
