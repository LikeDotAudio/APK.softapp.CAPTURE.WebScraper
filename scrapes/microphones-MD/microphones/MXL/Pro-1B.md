## CardioidMXL Pro 1-B

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Pro 1-B is a USB microphone intended for use in podcasting and online telephony, including Skype and video chat.

The capsule is a back-electret design.

The mic is identical to the earlier Studio 1 USB, aside from body color and marketing.

The mic includes a ring mount, a 10' USB cable, and a small tripod stand for desktop use.

Permalink: [MXL Pro-1B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/Pro-1B)

The mic was released in 2010.
