## CardioidMXL Trio

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL Trio is an electret cardioid condenser mic with a USB interface. The mic has an all-metal body and classic studio mic (C414) styling.

The A/D conversion chipset, described as “Delta Sigma,” is capable of 16 bit operation at a max sampling rate of 48kHz. An onboard 1/8'' headphone jack allows for low latency monitoring.

The Apple Camera Connector Kit (sold separately) is required for iPad connectivity.

The Trio ships with a folding tripod desk stand, hard mount and USB cable.

Permalink: [MXL Trio](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/Trio)

The mic was released in 2012.
