## MXL Club Drum Kit

## 4-Mic Set

The MXL Club Drum Kit is a basic 4-piece drum kit microphone set, including:

* One A-55 dynamic bass-drum mic
* Two 606 condensers for overheads
* One LSM-5 dynamic mic for snare
