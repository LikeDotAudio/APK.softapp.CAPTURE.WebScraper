## Wide Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The MXL 991 is a small-diaphragm condenser mic with a fixed cardioid (described by MXL as “wide cardioid”) pickup pattern.

The gold-sputtered diaphragm is 6 microns thick and measures 20mm in diameter. The capsule, and in fact the circuit design, match the [MXL 603S](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/603S), although the vendor’s published frequency charts describe the 991 as a much brighter mic, with a +3dB point at ~5k and +5dB at ~9k.

The 991 is no longer sold on its own, but is now bundled with the [990 condenser](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/MXL/990) in the MXL 990/991 Studio Package.

The mic ships with a standard mic clip. A suspension shockmount is available separately; look for p/n MXL-41-603.
