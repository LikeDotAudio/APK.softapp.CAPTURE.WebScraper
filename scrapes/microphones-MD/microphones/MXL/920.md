## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MXL 920 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone, from MXL’s “900 series,” manufactured exclusively for Guitar Center and its affiliate brands.

[![MXL 920 PCB](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mxl/MXL920-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mxl/MXL920-pcb.jpg)The microphone is a hybrid of the MXL 990 and V63M/2006. It shares the transformerless JFET circuitry of all three microphones (derived from the Schoeps CMC-5), with a single-PCB, through-hole implementation like the older 990. It uses MXL’s standard 32mm 6-micron K67-style capsule, like the V63M and 2006.

As of January 2014, the 920’s in Guitar Center’s inventory retain the through-hole PCB pictured here.

The body is essentially a taller version of MXL’s popular 910, although the characteristic cylindrical, fine-mesh 990 headbasket is replaced in the 920 with a tapered, two-layer mesh grille using a coarser outer mesh.
