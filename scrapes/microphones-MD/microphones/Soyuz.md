## Soyuz Microphones

Soyuz Microphones is based in Tula, Russia, home of Oktava Microphones. The company was launched in 2013, and introduced two new premium microphones at Winter NAMM 2014: a large diaphragm tube mic, and a small-diaphragm FET.

The company’s goal is to produce “small-batch,” premium handmade microphones that draw on hundreds of years’ worth of Russian culture and technology.
