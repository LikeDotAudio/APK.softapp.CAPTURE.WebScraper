## ART Pro Audio

Applied Research and Technology manufactures a variety of popular audio signal processors, including tube mic preamps, compressors, power amps, digital audio interfaces, and microphones.

The company has sold over 100,000 units of its “Tube MP,” a single-channel mic pre/DI with a street price of $30.

ART is part of the Yorkville Sound family, with Apex, Traynor, and VTC.
