## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Samson Q2U is a dynamic microphone with dual outputs: analog XLR and digital USB. It also features a headphone jack for monitoring.

The mic has a cardioid pattern and frequency response of 50–15kHz.

The onboard headphone amplifier provides direct monitoring via 1/8'' jack.

The A/D converter operates at 16-bit, 48kHz. Class compliant USB connection requires no drivers for Windows or OSX.

The mic ships with a mini desktop tripod, HP20 headphones, 10ft USB cable, 10ft XLR cable, mic clip, and Cakewalk Music Creator software.
