## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Q1U is a handheld dynamic mic with onboard ADC and USB output. Samson claims this to be the first dynamic mic with a USB interface.

The mic has a frequency response of 50-16kHz, with gentle roll-off below 100Hz and broad mid rise between 2 and 10kHz.

The onboard A/D converter operates at 16-bit, up to 48kHz.

Downloadable Softpre apple for OSX and Windows provide additional features: gain, LED input metering, Low-cut filter, polarity.

Includes desktop mic stand, mic clip, USB cable and carry pouch, Cakewalk Music Creator LE software.

Permalink: [Samson Q1U](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Samson/Q1U)

The mic was released in 2006.
