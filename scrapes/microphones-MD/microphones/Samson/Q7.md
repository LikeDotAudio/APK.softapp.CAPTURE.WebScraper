## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Samson Q7 is a handheld supercardioid stage vocal mic.

It shares numerous features with its sibling, the Q8:

* Neodymium magnets for higher output
* Supercardioid polar pattern
* Die-cast zinc alloy body
* Internal shockmounting of capsule assembly

Most of the text describing the Q7 is reproduced verbatim for the Q8, yet the published frequency response and polar pattern differ. The Q7 has a smoother high-frequency response (or, at least, a *more smoothed* response plot), which rolls off at a higher bass frequency and a lower treble frequency. The Q8, in contrast, has a fuller response plot, and a flatter midrange.

The mic ships with a stand clip and plastic case.
