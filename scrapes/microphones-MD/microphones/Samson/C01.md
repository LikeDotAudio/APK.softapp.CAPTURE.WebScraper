## CardioidSamson C01

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The C01 is a medium-diaphragm electret condenser microphone with a cardioid pickup pattern (although the manual claims the mic has a hypercardioid pattern).

This mic is reported to be made in Shanghai by [Feilo](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Feilo).[1](http://groups.google.com/group/rec.audio.pro/msg/afd48d26693d00fa?dmode=source)

> Sound On Sound
>
> Tonally, the C01 has the bright, open sound associated with this type of microphone and, in direct comparison with my Rode NT1, it sounded slightly more airy at the top (as the frequency response curve suggested it might), but less solid at the low end. However, when I checked the ambient noise level, (at similar levels measured at the preamp output), the Samson seemed to generate several dBs more noise than the mics I compared it with, including my Rode NT1.

Permalink: [Samson C01](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Samson/C01)

The mic was released in 2002.
