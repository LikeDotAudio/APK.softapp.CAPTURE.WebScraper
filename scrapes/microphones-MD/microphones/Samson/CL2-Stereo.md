## Samson CL2 Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

The Samson CL2 is an inexpensive FET pencil condenser with interchangeable omni and cardioid capsules. The stereo pair bundles two matched mics with four capsules, shockmounts, pop filters and foam windscreens in a padded aluminum case.
