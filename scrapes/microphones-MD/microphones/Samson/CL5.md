## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Samson CL5 is a handheld condenser for vocals and instruments.

The CL5 has a flat response from 100Hz–3kHz, a gentle presence peak centered at 4.5kHz and another centered at 10kHz.

The low mass capsule features a 3micron gold-sputtered diaphragm. The capsule is shockmounted. The capsule diameter is unknown.

The mic body is die-cast and available in two finishes, black (CL5B) or Nickel (CL5N). The dual stage windscreen reduces popping.

Ships with foam-lined impact resistant aluminum case.

The [Samson CL5](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Samson/CL5) is also known as: CL5N, CL5B.

The mic was released in 2007.
