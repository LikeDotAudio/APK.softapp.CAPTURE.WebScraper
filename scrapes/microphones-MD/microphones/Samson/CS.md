## SupercardioidSamson CS Series

## Detachable-Capsule Dynamic Microphone

The Samson CS (“Capsule Select”) Series Microphone is a modular dynamic microphone with two capsule heads:

**CS1 (Vocals)**  
The CS1 Capsule is designed for vocal use.

Its frequency plot shows a slight low boost in the 150Hz area, a broad presence peak centered at 6kHz with a narrow peak at 8kHz. The high end drops quickly above 10kHz.

**CS2 (Instruments)**  
The CS2 Capsule is designed for instrument miking applications.

Its frequency response is similar to the CS1, but somewhat flatter. Its low rolloff below 100Hz is less steep, and upper-mid to high response is less exaggerated.

Both capsules are supercardioid neodymium elements with steel cages and internal shockmounting.

Like Samson’s other dynamic mics, the CS Mic features a rugged, zinc-alloy die-cast enclosure and gold-plated XLR connector.

> Mixdown Magazine - 2012
>
> The design is very simple and sleek. The capsules and protective cages unscrew effortlessly allowing you to change them around in a matter of moments and increasing the versatility of one microphone dramatcally.

The mic ships with both capsules, body, and stand mount in padded zippered case.

The [Samson CS Series](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Samson/CS) is also known as: CS-MIC.

The mic was released in 2012.
