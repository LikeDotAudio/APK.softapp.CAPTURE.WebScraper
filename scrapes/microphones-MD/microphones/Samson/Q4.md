## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Samson Q4 is a low cost cardioid dynamic mic.

The mic features a shockmounted dynamic cartridge made with Neodymium magnets. A sliding switch on the microphone body turns the mic on or off.

Samson has published no technical specifications for this microphone. Should any become available, we will update this profile.

It ships with a mic cable, clip, and storage pouch.

The [Samson Q4](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Samson/Q4) is also known as: Q4 CL.
