## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Milab LSR-3000 is a transformerless handheld condenser mic designed for detailed live vocal applications.

The mic features a rubber isolated condenser capsule with a cardioid pattern and, according to the manufacturer, excellent feedback rejection. It can handle high sound pressure levels up to 138dB and 150 with switchable -12dB pad.

The LSR-3000 ships in padded case with stand mount and foam windscreen.

The [Milab Microphones LSR-3000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Milab/LSR-3000) is also known as: LSR3000.
