## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Milab DC-73 is a handheld cardioid condenser designed for high sound level, close to medium distance applications.

The mic uses a dual FET preamplifier. The circular condenser element is shockmounted within the headbasket to reduce movement noise.

Documentation for DC-73 claims the mic as having “Very smooth on-off axis response with minimal proximity effect.”

The mic included various accessories: Microphone Cable; 5/8'' stand adapter (mount); grey windscreen.

The [Milab Microphones DC-73](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Milab/DC-73) is also known as: DC73.

The mic was released in 1981.
