## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Milab EMBLA is a multipattern FET condenser utilizing Milab’s unique rectangular diaphragm capsule design.

The side-address EMBLA can be switched among five polar patterns; Cardioid, Wide Cardioid, Hypercardioid, Figure-8, Omni.

Response charts show nearly flat response from 20Hz to 20kHz with a slight bump around 12kHz in Cardioid mode. Each EMBLA ships with its own graph for the Cardioid setting.

The EMBLA is discontinued but can be manufactured upon request. See Milab Historic Mic List link in sidebar.
