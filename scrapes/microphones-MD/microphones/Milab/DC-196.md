## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The DC-196 is a small format multipattern FET condenser microphone with Milab’s unique rectangular capsule. It is intended as a multi-purpose mic, suitable for studio or live sound use.

A switch on the microphone body allows selection of Cardioid, Omni, or Figure-of-8 polar patterns. Sensitivity is 21.5 mV/Pa, with self-noise measured as 12dBA.

A second switch on the mic body provides a -12dB pad. The mic’s output circuit is transformerless.

[![Milab DC-196 cutaway, by Jonah Westrich of Full Compass Systems](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/milab/DC196-cutaway-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/milab/DC196-cutaway.jpg)This microphone is unique in that it sports a rectangular capsule, shockmounted within the headbasket. The diaphragm material is a polyester film covered by a thin layer of aluminum, thus having good electrical properties with light weight, giving the mic a fast transient response. The capsule is known at Milab as the 2900, which is a modernized version of their 2700 capsule. The 2900 has improved frequency response and lower self-noise than its predecessor.

> Audio Technology, 2006
>
> The hand-signed measurement graph supplied with the  
> review DC-196 shows a very smooth and level frequency  
> response from 50Hz to 4kHz where a gentle rise of no  
> more than 1dB peaks at around 5kHz, with a similar  
> peak at around 10kHz.

[![Milab DC-196](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/milab/DC196-pair-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/milab/DC196-pair.jpg)The mic is available in either a white or black finish, and comes packaged with a windscreen and either a stand holder or shockmount (depending on where you purchase).

> Resolution, 2006
>
> Used as a distant room microphone on a drum recording, the DC196 sounds fabulous … Not only does everything sound balanced and natural, but there seems to be less in the way of objectionable room modes, and a detail to the HF elements that you wouldn’t necessarily expect…

The [Milab Microphones DC-196](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Milab/DC-196) is also known as: DC196.

The mic was released in 2006.
