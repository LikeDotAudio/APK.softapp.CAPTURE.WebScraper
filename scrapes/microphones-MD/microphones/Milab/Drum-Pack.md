## Milab Microphones Drum Pack

## 8-Mic Set

The Milab Drum Pack is a premium drum kit miking system, with eight high-end condenser microphones:

* One BDM-01 for kick drum
* Seven VM-44 for snare, toms, overheads

All eight microphones have rubber isolation mounts. The seven VM44s have foam windscreens.

The kit also includes two rim mounts.

Because the VM-44 supports detachable capsules, this kit could be expanded for other studio applications with the addition of Milab’s Omni or Supercardioid capsules.
