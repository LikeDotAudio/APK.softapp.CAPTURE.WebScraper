## Ningbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd.

Alctron is one of the best-known Chinese microphone manufacturers/resellers. Their products are often modeled after brand-name European and American designs, and the finished products are rebadged and sold under a variety of brand names.

Many of the mics Alctron sells are actually manufactured by other Chinese companies, such as Feilo, ShuaiYin, and 797 Audio. Due both to these “sales agent” relationships and the fact that design specs are frequently shared or copied, it is often impossible to determine the actual source for a specific microphone.
