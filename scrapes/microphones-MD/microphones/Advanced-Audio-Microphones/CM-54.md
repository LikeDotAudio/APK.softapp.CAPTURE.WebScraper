## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The CM-54 is a true pencil condenser with two 20mm interchangeable capsules, omnidirectional and cardioid. It also is compatible with the Peluso capsules from the [CEMC-6](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Peluso-Microphone-Lab/CEMC-6) pencil condenser.

The case and capsule design is based on the Neumann KM-54 (which was a tube mic). The mic features a clean class “A” FET amplifier circuit coupled to a discrete transformeless Schoeps-like output circuit. The result is a rugged design which is extremely transparent and has very low self-noise.

The CM-54 has a -10db/-20db pad switch plus a 75hz/150hz low frequency roll-off switch located at the microphone.

The preamp body of the CM-54 is identical to the body of the [Peluso CEMC-6](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Peluso-Microphone-Lab/CEMC-6), and is compatible with John Peluso’s house-made capsules for that microphone (cardioid, wide cardioid, hypercardioid, omni).

Advanced Audio tests each mic individually upon receipt from the manufacturing facility.

The [Advanced Audio Microphones CM-54](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Advanced-Audio-Microphones/CM-54) is also known as: CM54.

The mic was released in 2006.
