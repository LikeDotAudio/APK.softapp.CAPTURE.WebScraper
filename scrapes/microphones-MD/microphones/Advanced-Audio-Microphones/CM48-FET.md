## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The CM48 FET is a 3-pattern FET condenser microphone.

[![AK47 Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/barecapsules/front/sm/advanced-audio-ak47.jpg)](http://recordinghacks.com/capsules/#AK47)The capsule is Advanced Audio’s AK47, a 32mm version of the 34mm Neumann K47 design, made with 6-micron diaphragms. This capsule is a dual-sided design, which allows the microphone to offer multiple polar patterns.

The circuit is a 2-stage FET amplifier with an output transformer. It is identical to that of the CM87.

Three switches circling the base of the mic’s headbasket allow multiple configuration options:

* Polar pattern: Cardioid, Omnidirectional, Figure-8
* Pad: -10dB
* High-pass filter: 100Hz rolloff

The CM48 FET ships with an elastic shockmount and an aluminum flightcase.

The [Advanced Audio Microphones CM48 FET](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Advanced-Audio-Microphones/CM48-FET) is also known as: CM48.

The mic was released in 2013.
