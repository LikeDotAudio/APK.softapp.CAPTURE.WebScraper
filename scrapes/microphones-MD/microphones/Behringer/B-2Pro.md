## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Behringer B-2 Pro is a multipattern large diaphragm FET condenser. It appears to be identical internally to the NT2S from 797 Audio.  

[![B-2 Pro PCB](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/behringer/NT2S-B2Pro1-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/behringer/NT2S-B2Pro1.jpg)  
[![B-2 Pro PCB](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/behringer/NT2S-B2Pro2-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/behringer/NT2S-B2Pro2.jpg)

The circuit board is built with through-hole components, facilitating DIY mods and upgrades.

The mic uses a dual-diaphragm capsule that is shockmounted within the headbasket. A switch on the mic body allows selection of three polar patterns: Cardioid, Omni, or Figure-8.

A -10dB attenuation and 125Hz low cut are available via switches on rear of the mic body.

Included accessories for the B-2 Pro: aluminum case, foam wind filter, shock mount.

Permalink: [Behringer B-2Pro](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Behringer/B-2Pro)

The mic was released in 2002.
