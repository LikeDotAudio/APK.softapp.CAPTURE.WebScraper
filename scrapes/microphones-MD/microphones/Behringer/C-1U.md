## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The C-1U is the USB version of the side-address C-1 condenser. Both are small-diaphragm FET microphones.

In the C-1U, the XLR connector has been replaced with a USB jack. The onboard DAC is capable of 16 bit, 44.1 kHz conversion.

Unlike most USB microphones, the C-1U does not include a low-latency headphone jack; monitoring must be done via the host computer.

A blue LED on the front indicates power from computer.

The C-1 ships with plastic hardmount and plastic foam-lined case.

The [Behringer C-1U](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Behringer/C-1U) is also known as: C1U.

The mic was released in 2009.
