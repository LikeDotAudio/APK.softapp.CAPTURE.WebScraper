## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Behringer C-3 is a multi-pattern condenser with a small-diaphragm capsule and a transformerless output circuit.

The mic features a dual-sided 16mm capsule, switchable low cut, -10dB attenuation, die cast metal body and red LED +48V indicator.

The C-3 ships with plastic hardmount and plastic foam-lined case.

The [Behringer C-3](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Behringer/C-3) is also known as: C3.

The mic was released in 2006.
