## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Behringer C-4 is a small-diaphragm FET condenser with a fixed Cardioid pickup pattern. The mic is sold in matched pairs.

A single switch on the front of the microphone body enables either a high-pass filter (-6dB/octave @ 120 Hz) or a -10dB cut. An identical switch can be found on the C-2 FET pencil mic pair.

The C-4 has higher sensitivity (at 12mV/Pa) than the C-2 (8.9 mV/Pa), and lower max SPL (136dB vs 150dB). Most other specifications are comparable.

No frequency-response graph nor self-noise figures for the C-4 have been published.

The C-4 package includes two microphones, two clips, two foam windscreens, a stereo mounting bar, and a carrying case.

The [Behringer C-4](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Behringer/C-4) is also known as: C4.

The mic was released in 2008.
