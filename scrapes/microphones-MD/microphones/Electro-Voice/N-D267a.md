## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

* N/DYM® dynamic cardioid vocal microphone
* Clear, consistent sound without low-end distortion
* Features VOB™ technology for increased vocal intelligibility
* as version is switchable version

The N/D267as version of this mic adds an on/off switch to the N/D267a design.

The [Electro-Voice N/D267a](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/N-D267a) is also known as: N/D267, N/D 267, N/D267as, N-D267a.
