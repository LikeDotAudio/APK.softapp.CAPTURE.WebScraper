## CardioidElectro-Voice RE920Tx

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The RE920Tx is a clip-on cardioid condenser mic, designed specially for wireless use with musical instruments such as drums and horns.

Its included mounting clip helps isolate the mic from mechanical vibration.

The mic is terminated in a TA4F connector wired for use with EV & Telex bodypack transmitters.

It ships with a two-stage foam windscreen and a gig bag.
