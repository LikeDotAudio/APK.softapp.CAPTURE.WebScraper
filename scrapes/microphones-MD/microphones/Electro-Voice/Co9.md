## CardioidElectro-Voice Co9

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Co9 is an inexpensive handheld dynamic microphone, intended for stage vocal usage.

A rare-earth Neodymium magnet gives the mic hotter output than competitive offerings.

The body is made of diecast Zinc.

The mic ships with a mic clip and a gig bag.
