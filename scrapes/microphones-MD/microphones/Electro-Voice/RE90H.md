## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

* Compact, hanging style condensor microphone
* Ideal for choir, instrumental and vocal groups, and live theater
* Controlled cardioid polar response for distant sound pickup without feedback
* 25-ft braided, sheilded cable maintains microphone's position without rotation
* 3-pin male XLR-type output connector with built-in pre-amp
* Also available in white(RE90HW)
