## OmnidirectionalElectro-Voice RE50N/D-B

## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The RE50N/D-B is a shock-mounted, handheld interview microphone with an omnidirectional pickup pattern.

The mic is identical to the RE50/B, with the exception of the use of neodymium magnets here for higher output.

The [Electro-Voice RE50N/D-B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/RE50N-D-B) is also known as: RE50N/DB.
