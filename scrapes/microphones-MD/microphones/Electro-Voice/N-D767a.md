## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The N/D767a is a handheld dynamic vocal microphone incorporating E-V’s “VOB” (Vocally Optimized Bass) EQ circuit to reduce boominess from close-miked sources.

The supercardioid pickup pattern reduces bleed from nearby instruments.

> Performing Musician, 2009
>
> The N/D767a … sounded very even and clear at modest volume. [At louder volumes], we were still pleased with the results and especially with its smooth character, particularly on the female vocals. On very loud passages the sound warmed up somewhat … but still cut through well.

The [Electro-Voice N/D767a](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/N-D767a) is also known as: N/D767, ND767.
