## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The Electro-Voice RE50/B is an omnidirectional moving-coil dynamic microphone designed for broadcast applications.

The RE50/B features a 4-stage pop and wind filtering and internal shockmount damping for extremely low handling noise. EV claims the diaphragm is unaffected by temperature extremes and atmospheric conditions.

The mic is finished in a non-reflecting, semi-gloss black, and ships with hard stand mount.

The [Electro-Voice RE50B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/RE50B) is also known as: RE50, RE50/B.
