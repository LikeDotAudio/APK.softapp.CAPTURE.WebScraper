## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Electro-Voice N/D868 is large diaphragm dynamic microphone with a frequency response tailored for the “perfect kick drum sound”.

* Large diaphram N/DYM element is specifically designed to mic kick drums
* Carefully engineered low frequency response gives great sound right out of the box
* Also ideal for floor tom, bass cabinet, and guitar cabinet

> MIX
>
> If I had to pick between the [N/D868 and [Audix D4](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/D4)], I’d give the edge to the N/D868, purely on the perfection of its tight, round (almost compressed-sounding) low end. The N/D868 is clearly a kick drum-only mic.

> Emusician
>
> All things considered, the N/D868 was the most consistently pleasing performer of the seven mics we tested, and the one that most of the judges preferred most of the time.

The [Electro-Voice N/D868](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/N-D868) is also known as: ND868.

The mic was released in 1998.
