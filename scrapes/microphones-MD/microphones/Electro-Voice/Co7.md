## CardioidElectro-Voice Co7

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Like the Co5, the Co7 was a handheld dynamic mic intended for stage vocal usage. It was more sensitive than the Co5, and had a broader and somewhat flatter frequency response, with a presence peak extending beyond 10kHz.

The mic shipped with a clip and a gig bag.
