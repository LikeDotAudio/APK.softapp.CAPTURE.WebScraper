## Electro-Voice PL-DK7

## 7-Mic Set

The PL-DK7 bundles 7 of Electro-Voice’s popular PL-series performance microphones into a kit suitable for drum miking. It includes:

* One PL 33 (with hard mount) for kick drum
* Four PL 35 (with hard mount and rim clamp) for snare and toms
* Two PL 37 condensers (with hard mounts) for overheads

The microphones are packed into a padded “gig bag,” which appears to be a rigid plastic case covered in woven black Nylon, with a shoulder strap. The case has foam cutouts to accommodate two additional PL35s and one additional PL37.
