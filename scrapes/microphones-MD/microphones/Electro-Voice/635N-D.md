## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

* Same “hammer-like” toughness as the 635A
* Increased output with neodymium magnet structure

The 635N/D microphone is a newer version of the [635A](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/635A) design, with the primary difference being the incorporation of higher-powered Neodymium magnets in the capsule. These raise the mic’s output and alter its frequency response.

Two colors are available:

* **635N/D** - nonreflecting fawn beige
* **635N/D-B** - Semigloss “camera” black

The diaphragm is made of “Acoustalloy,” a plastic compound that can withstand wide variations in humidity and temperature, in addition to corrosive environments (salt air).

The [Electro-Voice 635N/D](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/635N-D) is also known as: 635N/D-B, 635ND, 635N, 635NDB, 635N-D-B.
