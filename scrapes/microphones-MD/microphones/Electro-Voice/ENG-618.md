## Hypercardioid Condenser Microphone

The ENG 618 is a hypercardioid shotgun condenser microphone with an integrated boom pole. It can be powered either by phantom power or a 9V battery.

It includes a carrying case.

The [Electro-Voice ENG 618](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/ENG-618) is also known as: ENG618, ENG 618.
