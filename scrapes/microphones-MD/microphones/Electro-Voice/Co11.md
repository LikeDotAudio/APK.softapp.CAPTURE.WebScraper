## CardioidElectro-Voice Co11

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Co11 is a handheld condenser microphone, from EV’s Cobalt line of low-cost mics.

It ships with a mic clip and a storage pouch.

The mic has a cardioid pickup pattern. The mic is constructed to reduce handling noise.

The Co11 appears to have been discontinued. The moving-coil dynamic [Co9](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/Co9) is still in production.

> Electro-Voice
>
> Versatile condenser microphone, ideal for both vocals and instruments. Cardioid polar pattern with excellent feedback rejection and acoustic isolation. High compliance shock mount effectively eliminates handling noise. Multistage pop filter eliminates breath pop noises. “Memraflex” hardened grille screen.
