## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PL33 is a supercardioid dynamic mic designed for kick drum use.

The frequency response graph shows a +10dB boost from around 3–8kHz, and extreme proximity effect (over 15dB) centered around 80Hz with close placement. This “scooped” EQ curve is common for dedicated kick-drum microphones.

The mic’s polar pattern suggests a fairly colored off-axis response; although it produces a Cardioid pattern below 250Hz, sensitivity at 180° grows through 4kHz. The pattern becomes nearly figure-8 at 8kHz and clover-shaped at 16kHz.

The mic ships with stand adapter and soft zippered bag. A foam windscreen, p/n WSPL-2, is available separately.

The PL33 is also packaged within EV’s [PL-DK7 kit](http://www.electro-voice.com/product.php?id=780).

The [Electro-Voice PL33](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Electro-Voice/PL33) is also known as: PL-33.

The mic was released in 2009.
