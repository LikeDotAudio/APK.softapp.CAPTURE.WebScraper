## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The CR1-14 is a pencil condenser mic with a fixed cardioid capsule. Onboard switches providing a 10 or 20dB pad and a HPF at 75 or 150 Hz provide versatility in studio settings.

This mic is available under a variety of brand names.

A version of this microphone with detachable capsules (offering alternative pickup patterns) is available from [Advanced Audio Microphones](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Advanced-Audio-Microphones) and from [Peluso Microphone Lab](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Peluso-Microphone-Lab).
