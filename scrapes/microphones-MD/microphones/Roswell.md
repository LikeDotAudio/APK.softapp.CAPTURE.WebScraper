## Roswell Pro Audio

Roswell Pro Audio is a boutique microphone manufacturer based in Northern California. The company’s products follow the philosophy of delivering modern performance with vintage tone, using the best components on the market. All the Roswell microphones are assembled and tested in California, rather than imported whole from China as is true of most condenser mics under $2000.

The product line includes a variety of large-diaphragm condensers, each of which uses a unique combination of circuit topology and capsule voicing to deliver a specific sound, color, and texture.

The company’s “Mini K” series, despite the sub-$500 price points, provides exceptionally high performance, thanks to the handcrafted circuits, carefully voiced capsules, and extensive burn-in and test procedures.
