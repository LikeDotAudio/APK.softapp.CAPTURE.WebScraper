## BinauralT.H.E. Audio BS-3D

## Binaural Condenser Stereo Microphone

Billed as a 3D stereo microphone, THE’s BS-3D is a 160mm-diameter wooden orb with two half-inch condenser mic capsules mounted on opposite sides. Inside the sphere are a pair of preamps utilizing hand-selected metal film resistors, lab-grade capacitors and other high-end components.

The mic is available in Black Walnut or Golden Oak, and includes an XLR-5F to 2xXLR-3M breakout cable.

The [T.H.E. Audio BS-3D](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/T-H-E-Audio/BS-3D) is also known as: Binaural Sphere.
