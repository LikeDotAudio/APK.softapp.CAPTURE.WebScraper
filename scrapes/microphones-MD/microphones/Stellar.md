## Stellar Sounds

Stellar Sounds is a small audio gear manufacturer in Oregon, headed up by product designer Peter Bloch. The company’s earliest microphones were imported from China and sold at relatively low markup via Ebay. As of 2011, the company has begun introducing custom microphone designs, beginning with the CM-6, described as the only EF86-based tube mic in its price range.
