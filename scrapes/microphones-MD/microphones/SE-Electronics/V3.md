## CardioidSE Electronics V3

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The SE V3 is SE’s entry-level dynamic handheld microphone. It is designed as a all-around dynamic mic, suitable for stage use on vocals and instruments.

The V3’s moving-coil cartridge is internally shockmounted for protection from handling noise and drops. Also, there is an internal foam windscreen (red, though a black one is included) just under the grille. The body is made from zinc alloy, and the grille from spring steel.

The fact that the internal foam windscreens are removable means they can be easily cleaned and/or replaced when necessary.

The V3, like other sE dynamics, has beveled edges on the ring surrounding the grill. This means the mic won’t roll off a table when laid on its side.

The microphone has a pronounced upper-midrange peak, beginning at 1kHz and extending up to the highs at 10kHz. The low end rolls off starting at 200Hz to combat proximity effect.

The V3 is sold with a cloth bag, a mic clip, and an extra internal foam windscreen (black).
