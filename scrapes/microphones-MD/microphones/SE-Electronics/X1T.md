## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The X1 T is a large-diaphragm Cardioid condenser microphone built into the case of the X1 fet condenser. It uses a tube circuit, no details of which have been published.

The capsule is the same as is found in the X1: a 32mm O.D. K67 derivative.

Two switches on the mic body independently enable a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter (described by musicradar.com as filtering frequencies below 80Hz).

The mic ships with its 7-pin cable, power supply and cord, and a hard mount.

The [SE Electronics X1T](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/X1T) is also known as: X1-T.

The mic was released in 2014.
