## OmnidirectionalCardioidSE Electronics sE2200a II

## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The SE2200a II is a multipattern side-address condenser; it is essentially a tripolar version of the company’s popular 2200A.

The mic is finished in a black rubber paint which also functions to damp body resonance.

SE2200a II was announced at Winter NAMM, January 2012. Early shipments of the 2200a II include matching rubberized shockmount and removable pop filter.

We will update this profile as additional details become available.

The [SE Electronics sE2200a II](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/2200a-II) is also known as: 2200a MKII, se2200a mk2.

The mic was released in 2012.
