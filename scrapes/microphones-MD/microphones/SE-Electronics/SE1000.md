## CardioidSE Electronics SE1000

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The SE1000 was an early large-diaphragm condenser microphone from sE Electronics, prior to the construction of the sE factory. The company’s mics at the time were manufactured by Feilo.

The SE1000 was a rebadged Feilo CR1-86. It was a fixed-cardioid, large-diaphragm, transformer-coupled FET condenser mic.

> SoundOnSound
>
> The traditionally styled SE1000 will be familiar … as it has been badged and sold as an ‘own brand’ mic by numerous companies keen to cash in on cost-effective Chinese mic manufacture.

The [SE Electronics SE1000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/SE1000) is also known as: SE-1000.
