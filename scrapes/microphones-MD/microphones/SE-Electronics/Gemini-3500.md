## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Gemini 3500, aka G 3500, is a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone with a fixed cardioid pickup pattern.

As a member of the Gemini family, it uses the same capsule as the other single-pattern Gemini mics — the Gemini, Gemini II, and Gemini 5.

The mic ships with a shockmount in a black aluminum flight case.

Note that despite similarities in the name and physical appearance of the Gemini 3500 to the earlier [sE H 3500](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/H-3500), also a large-diaphragm FET condenser, the two mics have different capsules and different amplifier circuits. Further, the new Gemini 3500 is approximately 30% smaller than the H3500.

Further details about the mic are forthcoming.

The [SE Electronics Gemini 3500](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/Gemini-3500) is also known as: G 3500.

The mic was released in 2010.
