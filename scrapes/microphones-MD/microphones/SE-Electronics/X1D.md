## CardioidSE Electronics X1D

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The X1D is a large-diaphragm cardioid condenser microphone designed specifically for kick drum. The front diaphragm of its capsule is metallized with titanium rather than the more typical gold or gold/aluminum alloy, in order to give the microphone better transient response.

> This capsule is stiffer than our gold-sputtered capsules, meaning quicker transient response, which is important when recording instruments that create fast transients and attack like drums and percussion…

Switches on the microphone body independently enable a -10dB pad and high-pass filter.

Although nominally part of the X1 mic series, the X1I uniquely does not share the same body as the X1, X1R, and X1T microphones. The X1D has a more cylindrical grille, with radiused rather than sharp corners.

The sensitivity specification has not been provided, although sE has claimed a max SPL of 170dB (presumably with pad enabled).

The [SE Electronics X1D](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/X1D) is also known as: X1-D.

The mic was released in 2014.
