## CardioidSE Electronics H 3500

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The H3500 was a large-diaphragm FET condenser, described by one engineer as the “child” of the [Neumann TLM103](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/TLM103) and U-47. It was announced in late 2003, and discontinued sometime in 2005.

[![sE Electronics H3500 and shockmount](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/se/H3500-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/se/H3500.jpg)The H3500 shipped with an elastic shockmount in an aluminum flightcase (photo at right courtesy [Janne Lummaa](http://www2.uiah.fi/~jlummaa)).

The mic used a transformer-coupled output, and a 27mm gold-sputtered diaphragm made of 6-micron Mylar. Little else is known about this microphone.

> Curt Hemming
>
> I’ve done side by side comparisons with the SE 3500 and [Neumann TLM103](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/TLM103), and found to have a mic which sounds as crisp and clean but possibily a warmer low end for a fraction of the cost.

The [SE Electronics H 3500](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/H-3500) is also known as: 3500, H3500.

The mic was released in 2003.
