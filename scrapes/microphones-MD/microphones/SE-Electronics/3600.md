## CardioidSE Electronics 3600

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Part of the sE “Custom Series,” the sE 3600 was a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone built as a successor to the earlier model H3500. This mic dates to the days when sE products were manufactured by Feilo.

The 3600 used a 1.07” (27mm) capsule and Class A FET electronics.

The mic used an unusually tall headbasket design; the grille occupied nearly 3/4 of the height of the microphone.

It shipped with a shockmount in an aluminum flightcase.
