## DPA 3532-S

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The DPA 3532-S stereo kit includes two of the company’s acclaimed 4041-S one-inch omnidirectional condensers. The 4041-S is an exceptionally low self-noise model (7dBA), with a high max SPL rating of 144dB. The mic’s detachable MMC4041 capsule head produces a linear response to 5kHz, with a broad presence boost of 5dB from 5–10kHz.

The two mics are matched to within 1 dB for frequency response, sensitivity and self noise.

The kit includes the HMA5000 “Hi-Voltage Microphone Amplifier,” which powers two 4041-S microphones, plus the necessary cables and a calibrated stereo mounting bar.
