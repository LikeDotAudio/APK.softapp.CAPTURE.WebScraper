## DPA ST2011C

## Stereo Mic Set

The ST2011C is a stereo pair of DPA’s 2011C cardioid pencil microphones, selected by hand to match sensitivity within 1.5dB.

The microphones ship in a rugged, foam-lined Peli™ case, with hard mounts and foam windscreens.

DPA sells stereo boom mounts and shockmounts separately.
