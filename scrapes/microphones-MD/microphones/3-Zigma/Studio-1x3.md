## 3 Zigma Audio Studio 1x3 Toolkit

## 1-Mic Set

The studio 1x3 is 3-Zigma’s small kit consisting of one head-amp and 3 capsules.

Purchasers of this kit can choose which of the four available large-diaphragm capsules is included.
