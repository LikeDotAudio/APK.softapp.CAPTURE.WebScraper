## 3 Zigma Audio Master 4x10 Toolkit

## 4-Mic Set

The 4x10 is 3-Zigma’s largest Toolkit. It includes four of the company’s CHI HA-TL transformerless head amps, all four of the available large-diaphragm capsules, and two each of the small-diameter Cardioid, Hypercardioid and Diffuse-field Omni.

Four elastic shockmounts are included. The entire kit is delivered in a padded wooden storage case.
