## 3 Zigma Audio Studio 2x6 Toolkit

## Stereo Mic Set

The 2x6 is 3-Zigma Audio’s medium-size toolkit. It includes a pair of the CHI HA-TL transformerless head amps, a pair of SDC Cardioid, and one each of the Hypercardioid and diffuse-field Omni, plus any two of the company’s large-diaphragm capsules.

Purchasers of this kit can choose which two of the four available large-diaphragm capsules are included.

The six capsules, two shockmounts, and two microphone bodies are packed into a padded wooden storage case.
