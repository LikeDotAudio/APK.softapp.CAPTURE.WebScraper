## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The M900 is a small-diaphragm condenser mic with three detachable capsules, providing omni, cardioid, and hypercardioid pickup patterns. The preamp body is equipped with a 3-position pad (flat, -10dB, -20dB) and 3-position filter.

Although the capsules are visually similar to those used on the tube version of this mic (the M900T), the capsules are *not* interchangeable across the two head amps; the various capsules are sourced from different manufacturers, and in some cases require different polarization voltages.

The output circuit is transformerless. The M900 is the only transformerless microphone in CharterOak’s product line, as of late 2010.

The mic has a lifetime warranty on all parts and labor.

The [CharterOak Acoustic Devices M900](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CharterOak/M900) is also known as: M-900.
