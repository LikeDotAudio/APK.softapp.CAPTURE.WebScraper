## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

A reviewer at Harmony Central [noted](http://reviews.harmony-central.com/reviews/Microphone/product/Nady/RSM-2/10/1) that the circuit boards inside the RB500 are marked “RSM-2,” supporting the common conclusion that this mic is another Alctron HRM-1 clone (with a modified grille).

Includes soft padded carrying bag, yoke mount and permanently-attached XLR cable.

The [t.bone RB500](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/t.bone/RB500) is also known as: RB-500.

The mic was released in 2004.
