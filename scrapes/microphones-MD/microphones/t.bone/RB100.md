## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The T.Bone RB100 is a rebadged Alctron HRM-10, better known as the Nady RSM-3.

The MSRP is 99€, which is significantly lower than the price on the RSM-3. The street price (75 €) is a lot lower as well.

The mic ships with a metal suspension shockmount.

The [t.bone RB100](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/t.bone/RB100) is also known as: RB-100.

The mic was released in 2007.
