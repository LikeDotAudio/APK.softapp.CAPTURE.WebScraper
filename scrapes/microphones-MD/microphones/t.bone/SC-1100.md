## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The SC 1100 is a three-pattern FET condenser with switchable high-pass filter. The capsule is a center-terminated K67 type, advertised as 35mm (O.D.).

The mic uses two circuit boards with discrete components.

The capsule is a K67 type, variously described as “1.07''” and “35mm.”
