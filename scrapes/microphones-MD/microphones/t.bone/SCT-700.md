## Cardioidt.bone SCT 700

## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The t.bone SCT 700 is a large-diaphragm tube microphone with a fixed Cardioid polar pattern.

[![SCT 700](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/tbone/SCT700-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/tbone/SCT700-pcb.jpg)  
It uses a very similar circuit and components as numerous other inexpensive tube microphones: a 12AX7 dual triode tube, a CCDA circuit design, a 32mm K67 capsule. Its circuit is the same as is found in the Apex 460, with the exception of the 460’s pattern control feature.

The SCT 700 is somewhat less attractive as a modification platform than other models, because its 12AX7 tube is soldered in place.

The mic ships with an elastic shockmount in a foam-lined flightcase.

The [t.bone SCT 700](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/t.bone/SCT-700) is also known as: SCT700.
