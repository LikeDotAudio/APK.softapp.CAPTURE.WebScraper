## Supercardioid Boundary Microphone

The Bartlett Audio Stage Floor Mic-EC (Extra Clarity) — formerly the Bartlett TM-125 — is a boundary microphone with a super-cardioid polar pattern. It is intended for use on stage floors, to pick up musical and dramatic performances as well as dance performances where the floor percussion is integral to the show.

Depending on the size of the stage, 1-3 Stage Floor Mics can reduce or eliminate the need for wireless headworn mics if the recommended loudspeaker placement is used.

This mic has a permanently attached 6-foot cable and requires phantom power.

The Bartlett Audio Stage Floor Mic-EC’s predecessor was the called the TM-125.
