## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The Bartlett Audio Bass Mic is a clip-on miniature condenser intended to provide a full and mellow low end for upright bassists while allowing freedom of movement in a performance setting.

This omnidirectional microphone is held inside the f-hole with a small foam pad, and connected to a rugged XLR connector via a permanent 8-foot cable.

According to the manufacturer, the lower frequencies have been tailored to compensate for the inherent “boominess” of a mic placed near the f-hole of the instrument.

The Bartlett Bass Mic can be plugged into an instrument amplifier or a mixer. It requires phantom power.
