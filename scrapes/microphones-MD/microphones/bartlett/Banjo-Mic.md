## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The Bartlett Audio Banjo Mic is a clip-on miniature condenser intended to provide natural reproduction of the banjo’s sound while allowing freedom of movement in a performance setting. This omnidirectional microphone is affixed to the head of the instrument with the provided Velcro™ fastener and has a permanently attached 8-foot cord terminating in a rugged XLR connector.

Given the frequency range of the banjo, this mic is also the manufacturer’s recommendation for resonator guitars and bandoneon.

The Banjo Mic can be plugged into an instrument amplifier, or a mixer. It requires phantom power.
