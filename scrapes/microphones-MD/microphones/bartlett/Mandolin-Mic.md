## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The Bartlett Audio Mandolin Mic is an omnidirectional miniature condenser mic designed to reproduce the natural sound of mandolin-family instruments while allowing the player freedom of motion in a performance setting. The mic is mounted outside the f-hole (or oval-hole) with a felt-covered clip.

The Mandolin Mic has a permanently attached 8-foot cable terminating in an XLR connector. It can be plugged into an instrument amp or mixer. It requires phantom power.

For higher volume band situations, Bartlett also makes the Mandolin Mic-LB, which provides greater volume before feedback.
