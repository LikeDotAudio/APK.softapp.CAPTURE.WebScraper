## Lewitt Audio

Lewitt Audio is a global professional audio company, with design facilities in Austria and manufacturing facilities in China. The company’s founders and partners are Roman Perschon (CEO) and Ken Yang (President).

The company’s goal is to produce premium stage and studio microphones, with a focus on products with a modern sound and innovative features. Lewitt Audio’s initial product line, announced in at the 2010 winter NAMM show, includes a full range of condenser and dynamic microphones, including drum mics and wireless products.

For more information, see our [Lewitt MTP Performance Mic Review](http://recordinghacks.com/2010/12/07/lewitt-mtp-microphone-review/) and [Lewitt Authentica LCT540 and LCT640 Mic Review](http://recordinghacks.com/2011/01/01/lewitt-lct540-lct640-review/).
