## Oktava MK-012-10

## Stereo Mic Set

The MK-012-10 is a [hybrid mic](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/tag/hybrid-mic) kit consisting of a factory matched stereo pair of MK-012 head amps plus four pairs of capsules:

- SDC Cardioid
- SDC Hypercardioid
- SDC Omni
- LDC Cardioid (side-address)

Also included are a pair of inline -10dB pads (which attach between capsule and mic body), and two metal mic clips. The kit is packed into a hardshell plastic carrying case.
