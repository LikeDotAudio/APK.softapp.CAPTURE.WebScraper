## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The Oktava ML-52-01 is an variation on the [ML-52](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/ML-52) (aka ML-52-02), which was introduced 5 years earlier. The ‘01 has an improved internal blast filter, which provides two benefits:

* better protection of the ribbons from blasts of air and from moisture
* reduced LF sensitivity, which helps counteract proximity effect when close-miking.

In all other respects, the 52-01 is identical to the 52-02; see the ‘02 page for history, details, mods, and more.

In September, 2009, Oktava retailer Oktava-Online began offering the **ML-52-01-L,** which upgrades the ML-52-01 with a Lundahl LL2912 transformer.

The [Oktava ML-52-01](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/ML-52-01) is also known as: 52-01, ML5201, ML-52-01-L.

The mic was released in 2005.
