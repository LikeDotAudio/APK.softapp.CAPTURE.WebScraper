## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

The MKL-012 is a tube condenser head amp that accepts all MK-012 capsules.

The amplifier circuit is based on the Lomo 19A4 circuit. There is an output transformer within the external power supply.

![Oktava MKL-012 kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/oktava/mini_tube_box.jpg)The tube is a subminiature triode, model 6С6Б-В. (The Western designation is unknown at this time.) As in all subminiature tube mics, this tube is *not* socketed, but hardwired to the circuit board.

The MKL-012 is available in black or silver finishes. It ships with a dual-voltage (110/220) power supply, power cable, the microphone head amp, a metal mic clamp, and a -10dB detachable pad, in a padded wooden storage box.

![Oktava MKL-012 Power Supply](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/oktava/mini-tube-psu.jpg)Note: no capsules are included! Purchase any of the MK-012-compatible capsules separately.

The [Oktava MKL-012](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MKL-012) is also known as: Mini Tube.

The mic was released in 2008.
