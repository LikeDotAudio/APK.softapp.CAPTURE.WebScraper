## CardioidOktava MK-219A

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MK-219A is a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone with a fixed Cardioid pattern, a high-pass filter, and a -10dB pad.

[![MK-219A](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/oktava/MK219A-switches-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/oktava/MK219A-switches.jpg)It was the prototype of the microphone eventually released as the [MK-319](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MK-319). According to Oktava, the capsule and electronics are identical to the MK-319; only the body differs.

(Photo credit: Jeff Seitz)

The [Oktava MK-219A](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MK-219A) is also known as: MK219A.
