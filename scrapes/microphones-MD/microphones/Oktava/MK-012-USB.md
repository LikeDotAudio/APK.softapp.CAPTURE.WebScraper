## Detachable-Capsule Condenser Microphone

The MK-012-USB combines the FET preamp circuit of the famous [MK-012](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MK-012) with USB power and an A/D converter (16Bit/48kHz resolution). The mic preamp is compatible with most Oktava capsules; see below for a full list.

The 012-USB ships in a soft case with a USB cable. No capsules are included; the product appears to be targeted at MK-012 owners who seek the convenience of USB-powered recording, and who already own a selection of applicable capsules.

The [Oktava MK-012-USB](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MK-012-USB) is also known as: MK012 USB.

The mic was released in 2008.
