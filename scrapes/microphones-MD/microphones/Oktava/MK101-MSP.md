## Oktava MK-101 MSP

## Stereo Mic Set

The MK-101 MSP is a factory matched stereo pair of MK-012 head amplifiers with a matched pair of MK-101 large-diaphragm capsule heads. The kit includes two detachable inline pads (-10dB) and two metal mic clips, and ships either in a Pelican-style padded case or a wooden display case.

As part of the MK-012 family, the MK-101 kit can be expanded through the purchase of any MK-012 small-diaphragm capsule heads, or the MK-100 series large-diaphragm capsule heads.
