## Oktava MK-012 MSP6

## Stereo Mic Set

The MK-012 MSP6 is a factory-matched stereo pair of Oktava’s popular MK-012 pencil condenser, with two each of the company’s cardioid, omni, and hypercardioid capsules.

Also included are two inline -10dB pads, and a pair of metal mic clips, all packed into a padded wooden display case. Two colors are available: matte silver and black.

Note that studio applications for the MK-012 head amps can be further expanded by addition of the medium- and large-diaphragm capsules, such as the MK-101, MK-102, and MK-103.
