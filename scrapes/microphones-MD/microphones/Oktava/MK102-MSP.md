## Oktava MK-102 MSP

## Stereo Mic Set

The MK-102 MSP is a factory-matched stereo pair of the MK-102 large-diaphragm capsule with a pair of Oktava’s popular MK-012 transformerless head amplifier.

Two metal mic clips, and two inline pads (-10dB) are also included. The kit ships in a padded wooden storage case.

The MK-102 kit can be expanded through the purchase of compatible MK-012 small-diaphragm capsule heads, or the other MK-100 series large-diaphragm capsule heads.
