## CAD Audio Al DiMeola Acoustic Mic Pack

## Stereo Mic Set

The CAD Al Di Meola Signature Series Acoustic Mic Pack paired two of CAD’s equitek condenser microphones, the e60 and e70.

The e70 is a transformerless pencil mic; it includes both Cardioid and Omni capsules.

The e60 is a medium-diaphragm condenser with a fixed Cardioid pattern.

> CAD, 2007
>
> We sent Al an array of CAD microphones and asked him to pick his favorites for capturing his acoustic guitar. We have packaged Al’s top choices for stage and studio in this Signature Series Al Di Meola Acoustic Mic Pack.
