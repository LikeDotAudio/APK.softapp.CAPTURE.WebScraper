## CAD Audio Joe Satriani Mic Pack

## Stereo Mic Set

CAD’s Joe Satriani Signature Series microphone pack contains two mics reportedly hand-picked by Satriani for both studio and stage use. The kit contains the Trion 7000 ribbon and the D189 dynamic.

> CAD, 2007
>
> We sent Joe an array of CAD microphones and asked him to pick his favorites for capturing his guitar amp. We have packaged Joe’s top choice for stage and studio in this Signature Series Joe Satriani Guitar Amp Mic Pack.
