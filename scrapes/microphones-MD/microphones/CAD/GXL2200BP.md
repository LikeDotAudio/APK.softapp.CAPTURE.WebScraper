## CardioidCAD Audio GXL2200BP

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The CAD GXL2200BP is an inexpensive large-diaphragm condenser with a fixed Cardioid polar pattern.

The CAD GXL2200BP was released in 2011 to mark the 10th anniversary of CAD’s best selling GXL line of microphones.

> CAD Press Release July 2011
>
> The versatile new GXL1200BP, GXL2200BP and GXL3000BP condenser mics all feature a lustrous black pearl chrome finish along with exceptional performance specs that allow the mics to stand out in a range of studio and live sound reinforcement applications.

According to the published specifications, aside from the new black pearl finish, the 2200BP is identical to the original.

See the [CAD GXL2200](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/GXL2200) profile for more information.

The [CAD Audio GXL2200BP](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/GXL2200BP) is also known as: GXL-2200BP.

The mic was released in 2011.
