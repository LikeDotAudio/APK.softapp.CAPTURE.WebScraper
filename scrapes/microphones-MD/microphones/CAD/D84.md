## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Part of the CADLive™ series, the D84 is a large-diaphragm condenser designed for stage amplification.

The capsule is a single-diaphragm K67 type, with high sensitivity (30mV/Pa). The circuitry is transformerless, but relatively noisy at 19dBA.

The D84 shares the styling of the D82 ribbon and D80 dynamic microphone from the CADLive™ series.
