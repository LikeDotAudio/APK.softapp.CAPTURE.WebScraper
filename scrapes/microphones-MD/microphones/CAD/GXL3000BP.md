## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The GXL3000BP is a multipattern FET condenser, offering switchable pickup patterns: cardioid, omni, figure-of-8.

The CAD GXL3000BP was released in 2011 to mark the 10th anniversary of CAD’s best selling GXL line of microphones.

> CAD Press Release July 2011
>
> The versatile new GXL1200BP, GXL2200BP and GXL3000BP condenser mics all feature a lustrous black pearl chrome finish along with exceptional performance specs that allow the mics to stand out in a range of studio and live sound reinforcement applications.

According to the published specifications, aside from the new black pearl finish, the 3000BP is identical to the original.

See [CAD GXL3000](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/GXL3000) for more details.

The [CAD Audio GXL3000BP](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/GXL3000BP) is also known as: GXL-3000BP.

The mic was released in 2011.
