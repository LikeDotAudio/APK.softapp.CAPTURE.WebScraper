## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The CAD D29 is a compact cardioid dynamic designed for toms. The mic features an integrated drum rim clamp.

The frequency response rolls off from about 150Hz, flattens up to 1kHz, then slowly peaks at about 6kHz and drops sharply after 10kHz.

The D29 is sold as part of the Stage 4 and Stage 7 drum mic packages.

The [CAD Audio D29](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/D29) is also known as: D-29.
