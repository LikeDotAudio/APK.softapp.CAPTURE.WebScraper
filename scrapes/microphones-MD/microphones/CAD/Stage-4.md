## CAD Audio Stage 4

## 4-Mic Set

The Stage 4 is CAD’s entry-level drum mic kit. It covers snare (D19), toms (D29), and kick (D10). The snare and tom mics have integrated rim mounts; all four mics have integrated swiveling hard mounts.

The mics ship in a padded hardshell case.

See also the Stage 7, which adds one D29 tom mic and two C9 overheads.

The Stage 4 could be purchased as a supplement to the Stage 7, to provide additional coverage of a large or double-bass kit.
