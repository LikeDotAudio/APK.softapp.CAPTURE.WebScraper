## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Trion 6000 is a 3-pattern FET condenser mic with a transformer-coupled output. It is made in China, and bears a strong resemblance to the ShuaiYin SYC3000.

[![Trion 6000 capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/cad/trion-capsule-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/cad/trion-capsule.jpg)The capsule is a 32mm version of the Neumann K67 (note that the photo at right shows a very dirty capsule; new Trion microphones would not look like this). The FET amplifier circuit has high self-noise (20dBA), rendering it somewhat unsuitable for quiet acoustic sources.

It ships with an elastic shockmount in an aluminum flight case.

> StudioAuditions
>
> Very, very, very detailed sound.

The mic weighs over 2 lbs with the shockmount.

Ships with shockmount and metal carrying case.

> ProAudioReview
>
> In the figure 8 configuration … the 6000 sounded a little brighter on the back side than the front.

> Resolution, 2007
>
> Male vocals on the Cardioid setting delivered a sound with plenty of presence, but quite edgy sounding. Off-axis the microphone loses high-frequency detail very quickly, but this did not seem to get rid of the slightly abrasive quality. The 6000 reminded me most of an [sE Z5600](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/SE-Electronics/Z5600) [tube mic], although to my ears the sE sounds smoother.

The [CAD Audio Trion 6000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/Trion-6000) is also known as: Trion6000, 6000.

The mic was released in 2006.
