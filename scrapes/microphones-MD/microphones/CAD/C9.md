## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The CAD C9 is a small-diaphragm pencil condenser with a fixed cardioid polar pattern.

It has an electret capsule, although a different model than the one found in the ICM417.

A -10dB pad and 200Hz Hi-pass filter options are switchable on the microphone body.

The mic has a transformerless output circuit.

The C9 is sold in pairs or as part of the Stage 7 drum mic package.
