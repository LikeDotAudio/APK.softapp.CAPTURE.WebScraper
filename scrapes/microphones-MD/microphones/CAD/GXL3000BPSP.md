## CAD Audio GXL3000BPSP

## Stereo Mic Set

The GXL3000BPSP is a 2-mic “Studio Pack” featuring two microphones from CAD’s popular GXL line, finished in Black Pearl Chrome.

The GXL3000 is a multipattern large-diaphragm condenser. The GXL1200 is a small-diaphragm condenser. Both include padded storage pounches. The 3000 includes a shockmount and pop filter. The 1200 includes a mic clip.

This kit also includes a pair of CAD MH110 closed-back headphones.

See also the GXL2200BPSP kit, which swaps the 3-pattern 3000 for a fixed-Cardioid GXL2200, at a slightly lower price point.
