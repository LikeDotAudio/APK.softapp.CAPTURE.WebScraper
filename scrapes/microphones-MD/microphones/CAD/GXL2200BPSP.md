## CAD Audio GXL2200BPSP

## Stereo Mic Set

The GXL2200BPSP Studio Pack includes a large-diaphragm condenser, a small-diaphragm condenser, shockmount and pop filter, plus a pair of headphones.

The two microphones are from CAD’s popular GXL line of microphones, with the new Black Pearl Chrome finish.

The headphones are model MH110. This is a closed-back design with 50mm neodymium drivers.
