## CAD Audio Stage 7

## 7-Mic Set

The Stage7 is a 7-piece drum mic kit:

* One D10 dynamic for kick
* One D19 for snare, with integrated rim clamp
* Three D29 dynamics for toms, with integrated rim clamp
* Two C9 pencil condensers for overheads

No shockmounts are included, but as noted above the snare and tom mics have integrated rim clamps for ease of placement.

The set ships in a vinyl carrying case with a shoulder strap.
