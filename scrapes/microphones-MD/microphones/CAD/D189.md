## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The D189 is a supercardioid dynamic microphone designed for stage instrument and vocal use.

It houses its moving-coil cartridge in a rubber shockmount to reduce mechanical noise. The enclosing grille is “Flex-Form” hardened steel, to resist dents and damage from physical impact.

It ships with a mic clip and a zippered vinyl storage pouch.

Permalink: [CAD Audio D189](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/CAD/D189)

The mic was released in 2005.
