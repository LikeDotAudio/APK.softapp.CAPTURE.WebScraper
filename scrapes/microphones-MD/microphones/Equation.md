## Equation Audio

Equation Audio manufactures a small line of microphones and pro audio headphones in Nashville, TN.

The company was founded by the former VP of Product Development at CAD, Bruce A. Forbes. Forbes’ team at CAD was responsible for the original line of Equitek microphones, which set new performance standards for low-cost recording microphones.
