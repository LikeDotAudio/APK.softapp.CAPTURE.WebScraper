## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The TL 6 is the high-sensitivity (80 mV/Pa) version of the TL66.

Both are end-address, transformerless FET condensers in a pencil-microphone form factor. The TL 6 is intended for distant miking applications (such as audience use), where high sensitivity can be especially useful.

The TL 66 does not use the company’s trademark rectangular membrane. Like the TL6C, the mic uses a circular electret condenser capsule obtained from a third party.

The [Pearl Microphone Laboratory TL 6C](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Pearl/TL-6C) is also known as: TL6C, TL6.
