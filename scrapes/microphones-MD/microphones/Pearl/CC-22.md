## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Pearl’s CC 22 is one of a trio of single-pattern FET condenser mics, each with the company’s [Model 2800](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/tag/model-2800) rectangular-diaphragm capsule. All three mics provide “accurate”/neutral rather than colored voicing. All three use a similar transformerless amplifier circuit. The CC22 is a fixed-cardioid microphone.

The capsule diaphragm measures 14x29mm, and consists of a thin polyester film metallized with aluminum. The same capsule can be found in most of Pearl’s condenser microphones. Read more about the characteristics and performance of [rectangular condenser capsules](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/tag/rectangular-diaphragm).

Although the mic’s advertised frequency response is 20Hz–25kHz, the -3dB points are closer to 35Hz and 20kHz.

The microphone body is brass, finished with a gloss black lacquer. The company name and model name are engraved into the microphone body.

The CC 22 can be paired with the CB 22 (figure-of-8) for Mid-Side stereo recording.

The mic ships in a padded aluminum flight case (p/n AC01). Two models of shockmount are available separately:

* Model 1927 - essentially a rubber-lined mic clip
* Model 1928 - a traditional elastic shockmount

> SoundOnSound, 2004
>
> The CC22 is an honest-sounding microphone, tending towards neutral rather than colored. In my comparisons it sounded closer to the AKG C414B-ULS than any of the other mics [on] hand. The Microtech [[M 930](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-930)] and Neumann [[TLM 103](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/TLM103)] models were noticeably more colored and heavier sounding. In terms of resolution and the ability to capture complex detail the CC22 performed very well, capturing the complex harmonics of a 12-string acoustic guitar quite creditably.
>
> It delivers a smooth, extended, and neutral sound quality, seeming to combine the resolution and precision of a small-diaphragm mic with a hint of the ‘larger than life’ character and body of a large-diaphragm mic. It is particularly well suited to classical or folk-music recording, where accuracy and detail are key attributes.

The [Pearl Microphone Laboratory CC 22](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Pearl/CC-22) is also known as: CC22.
