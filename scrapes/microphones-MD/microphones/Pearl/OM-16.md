## Omnidirectional Pencil Condenser Microphone

The OM 16 is an omnidirectional FET mic with a transformerless output stage.

Unlike most of PML’s products, the OM16 has a traditional round capsule, with a diameter of 14mm. These capsules are made in-house at PML.

The mic delivers a flat frequency response from 50Hz through 4kHz, with a 2dB presence boost from 6kHz–10kHz. It is presented as a neutral, uncolored microphone.

The [Pearl Microphone Laboratory OM 16](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Pearl/OM-16) is also known as: OM16.

The mic was released in 2005.
