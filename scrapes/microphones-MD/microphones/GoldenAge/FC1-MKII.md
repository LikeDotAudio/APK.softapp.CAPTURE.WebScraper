## CardioidGolden Age Project FC 1 MKII

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The GAP FC 1 (MKII) is an inexpensive large-diaphragm FET condenser with a fixed cardioid polar pattern. It is built into the familiar U87-style body, although like most such mics is smaller than the U87.

The mic uses a large-diaphragm capsule with a 6-micron, center-terminated diaphragm.

The [Golden Age Project FC 1 MKII](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/GoldenAge/FC1-MKII) is also known as: FC1.
