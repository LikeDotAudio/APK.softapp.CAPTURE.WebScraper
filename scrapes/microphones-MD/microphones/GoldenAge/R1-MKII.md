## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The GAP R1 (Mk II) is a passive ribbon microphone, manufactured in China. It is a long-geometry ribbon, with the same motor and yoke-mount assembly as a variety of microphones from Nady, Apex, and other importers.
