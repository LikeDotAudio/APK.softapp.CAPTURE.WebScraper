## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Golden Age Project D1 is a stand-mount cardioid dynamic mic designed for close-proximity vocal use.

The mic features a die cast metal body with angle adjustment and integrated mount. A noiseless on/off switch is on the mic front.

The body design is reminiscent of the classic [Shure 55SH](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/55SH), aka ‘the Elvis mic’.

The published frequency response plot shows significant peak (+8dB) at 4kHz, and low roll off below 100Hz.
