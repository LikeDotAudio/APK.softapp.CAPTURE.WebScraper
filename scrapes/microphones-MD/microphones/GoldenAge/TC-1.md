## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The GAP TC-1 is a large diaphragm, multipattern, vacuum tube condenser microphone.

[![TC-1 Schematic](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/gap/TC1-schematic-sm.png)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/gap/TC1-schematic.png)The mic uses a 6072/12AT7 tube in class-A circuit with what GAP describes as “vintage style” EQ and a transformer-coupled output.

The mic’s capsule is believed to be a 32mm OD, 26mm diaphragm design with 3-micron membranes. It is most likely the standard Chinese-made 32mm K67 capsule found in most inexpensive imported condensers.

Switches on the body enable optional high-pass filter and -10dB pad.

A 9-way rotary control on the external power supply allows selection of Omni, Cardioid, Figure-8, or any of 6 intermediate patterns.

The [Golden Age Project TC-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/GoldenAge/TC-1) is also known as: TC1.
