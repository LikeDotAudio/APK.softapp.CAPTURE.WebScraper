## CardioidGolden Age Project FC 2

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The GAP FC2 is a large-diaphragm cardioid condenser. According to the manufacturer, it is essentially the same as the FC1 MKII, with a pad (-10dB) and high-pass filter.

Both mics use a low-noise FET amplifier circuit and transformer balanced output.

The [Golden Age Project FC 2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/GoldenAge/FC2) is also known as: FC2.
