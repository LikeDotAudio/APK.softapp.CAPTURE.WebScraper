## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The FC 4 MC is the 3-capsule version of the FC4.

The head amplifiers are identical: FET circuitry with transformerless output, a -10dB pad, and a high-pass filter.

Whereas the FC 4 includes only the Cardioid capsule, the MC includes three capsules (Cardioid, Omni, Hyper).
