## Golden Age Project FC 2 ST

## Stereo Mic Set

FC2ST is a matched pair of large-diaphram cardioid condenser mics.

The FC 2 is essentially the same mic as the FC 1 with -10dB pad and HPF added.

The FC 2 is only sold in pairs.

FC2ST set includes spider shockmount and sturdy plastic carrying case.
