## Cathedral Guitars

Cathedral Guitars was founded by guitarist Stephen Andrew Bright, an expert classical harp guitarist. He began by selling harp guitars, then branched out into selling microphones, preamps, and recording gear to his guitar customers.

Partnerships with AMI/Tab-Funkenwerk and Cascade Microphones led to the introduction, in 2012, of Cascade ribbon mics refitted with AMI transformers, and in 2013 to the introduction of replica models of the Neumann U67 and the AKG C12.
