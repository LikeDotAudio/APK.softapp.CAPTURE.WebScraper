## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The CG VIN-JET is Cascade Microphones’ passive VIN-JET ribbon, upgraded with an AMI TR42 bobbin-style output transformer. The mic is sold exclusively by Cathedral Guitars, via Ebay.

The mic ships with a shockmount and aluminum flight case.

Permalink: [Cathedral Guitars CG-VIN-JET](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Cathedral-Guitars/CG-VIN-JET)

The mic was released in 2012.
