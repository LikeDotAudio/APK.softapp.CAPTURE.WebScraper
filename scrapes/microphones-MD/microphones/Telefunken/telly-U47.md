## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Telefunken U 47 was a two-pattern tube condenser microphone developed by Neumann.

Telefunken distributed the microphone originally, with Telefunken’s logo badge on the front (as pictured here).

For more information, see the [Neumann U 47](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/U-47) profile.
