## Brauner

Founded by Dirk Brauner in 1993, Brauner Microphones manufactures a small line of high-end microphones for studio use.

> Dirk Brauner
>
> In a little more than 10 years, [our] relentless pursuit of perfection has made us one of the leading German companies in our field internationally.

The company’s current (2010) line of 14 microphones is split into two sonic classifications, described as “natural” or “charming.” All the mics are large-diaphragm condensers, some with tube circuits and the rest solid-state.
