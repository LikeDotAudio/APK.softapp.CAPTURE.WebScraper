## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Equation Audio DMI.102S is a compact supercardioid dynamic microphone designed for for use on snare and toms.

The DMI.102S is part of Equation’s Chinese manufactured Alpha Series, aka “MicTools for Drummers.”

Equation Audio has published very little information about these microphones.

> Sound On Sound 2008
>
> The tom and snare mics turned in a very respectable performance, delivering a well-balanced sound with perhaps just a touch less definition than you’d get from a set of pro dynamic mics, such as [Sennheiser 421](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/MD-421)s. However, Chris actually observed that the snare mic (the hypercardioid DMI 102S) gave a crisper sound than the [Shure SM57](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/SM57) he normally uses.

The 102S uses the same body as the DMI.101 but has a tighter supercardioid pattern and a more mid-scooped frequency response. A yellow ring around the body easily identifies the DMI.102S.

A swivel microphone mount is integrated into the body.

The [Equation Audio DMI.102S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Equation/DMI-102S) is also known as: DMI-102S.
