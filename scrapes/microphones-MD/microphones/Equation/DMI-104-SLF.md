## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Equation Audio DMI.104.SLF is a supercardioid large-diaphragm dynamic, part of Equation’s “MicTools for Drummers.”

Little information about the design or specifications of this mic has been made available; we will update this profile as more data is provided.

> Sound On Sound 2008  
> The weakest element of the set, for me, was the kick mic, which had a rather ‘soggy’ sound prior to being equalised, but if you’re prepared to work on the EQ, drum tuning and fine positioning, the result is very acceptable once the sound of the overheads is mixed in.
