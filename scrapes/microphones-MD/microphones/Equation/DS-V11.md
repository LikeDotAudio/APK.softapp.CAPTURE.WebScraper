## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The DS-V11 is a handheld stage dynamic microphone with a Cardioid pickup pattern.

It uses a moving-coil element made with Neodymium magnets for increased sensitivity. The mic is rated at 2.82 mV/Pa, which is higher than comparable models from Shure.

The capsule is shockmounted within the mic’s headbasket, to reduce handling noise. Its response is improved by the use of a resonant “polymer tuning chamber.”

The mic uses a steel-mesh grille and a contoured handle. It ships with a mic clip and a zippered storage pouch.
