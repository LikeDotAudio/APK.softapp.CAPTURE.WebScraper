## CardioidEquation Audio DMI.101

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Equation Audio DMI.101 is a compact cardioid dynamic microphone designed for for use on toms.

The DMI.101 is part of Equation’s Alpha Series.

Equation Audio has published very little information about their Alpha Series.

> Sound On Sound 2008
>
> The tom and snare mics turned in a very respectable performance, delivering a well-balanced sound with perhaps just a touch less definition than you’d get from a set of pro dynamic mics, such as [Sennheiser 421](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/MD-421)s. However, Chris actually observed that the snare mic (the hypercardioid DMI 102S) gave a crisper sound than the [Shure SM57](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/SM57) he normally uses.

The 101 uses the same body as the DMI.102S but has a wider cardioid pattern and flatter mid-frequency response.

A swivel microphone mount is integrated into the body.

The [Equation Audio DMI.101](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Equation/DMI-101) is also known as: DMI-101.
