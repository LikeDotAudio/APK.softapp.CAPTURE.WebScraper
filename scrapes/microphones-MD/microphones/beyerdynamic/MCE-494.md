## Cardioidbeyerdynamic MCE 494

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Designed as a sonic upgrade to the microphones built in to laptops and portable recorders, the MCE 494 is a miniature cardioid condenser mic with a 3.5mm audio jack.

The condenser capsule is a back-electret type. The mic is powered via the microphone jack on the host recorder or laptop.

Applications include speech recognition, interviewing, conference recording, etc.

The [beyerdynamic MCE 494](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/MCE-494) is also known as: MCE494.

The mic was released in 2002.
