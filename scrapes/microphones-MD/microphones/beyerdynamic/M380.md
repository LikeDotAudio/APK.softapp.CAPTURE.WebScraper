## Bidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The M380 is a moving-coil dynamic microphone with a bidirectional (figure-of-8) polar pattern. The capsule is adapted from the driver of one of Beyerdynamic’s headphone lines, reportedly the DT770/990.

The capsule is fitted with a humbucking coil. No output transformer is used.

The mic’s housing is robust diecast zinc.

[![Beyerdynamic M 380 moving-coil element](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/beyerdynamic/M380-coil-sm.jpg)](http://picasaweb.google.com/electricpress/M380)The microphone exhibits a strong proximity effect. At 1m, the response is -10dB at 20 Hz. At 2cm, the response is +14dB at 20 Hz.

[![M380, black](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/beyerdynamic/M380-black-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/beyerdynamic/M380-black.jpg)The M380 utilizes the beyerdynamic MKV8 stand adapter. The mic was available in at least two colors: bronze and black.

[Steve Albini lists the M380](http://www.soundonsound.com/sos/sep05/articles/albini.htm) as one of his favorite mics for bass cab and kick drum.

The [beyerdynamic M380](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/M380) is also known as: M380N(C), M380NC, M380N, 380, M-380.
