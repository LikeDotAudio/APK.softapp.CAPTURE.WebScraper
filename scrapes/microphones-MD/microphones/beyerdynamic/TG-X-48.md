## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The TG-X 48 is a dynamic vocal mic in the style of the [SM-58](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/SM58).

It has a supercardioid pickup pattern, which provides superior off-axis rejection as compared to the cardioid SM-58.

A foam pop screen built into the headbasket reduces plosives.

The mic body is coated with a special soft finish said to make handling easier and more comfortable.

It ships with a microphone clip and storage pouch.

The [beyerdynamic TG-X 48](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/TG-X-48) is also known as: TGX-48.
