## Cardioidbeyerdynamic Opus 87

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Opus 87 is a miniature electret condenser mic with an integrated spring-loaded “clip mount,” intended for drum and percussion miking. The mic capsule is shockmounted at the end of a 75mm gooseneck, facilitating placement around the drum kit or percussion instrument.
