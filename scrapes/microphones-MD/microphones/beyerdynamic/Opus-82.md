## Cardioidbeyerdynamic Opus 82

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Opus 82 is a miniature electret condenser mic designed for miking brass instruments such as trumpet, trombone and saxophone. The mic capsule is mounted on a short, flexible gooseneck with a clip for attachment to the bell of the horn.

The mic has a fixed Cardioid pickup pattern, which becomes slightly less directional below 125Hz and above 4kHz.

There are two versions of the mic available:

* **Opus 82** - with 3.5mm mini jack output, intended for use with beltpack transmitters
* **Opus 82 Set** - with separate power supply (model CVU 82) and 3-pin XLRM output

The [beyerdynamic Opus 82](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/Opus-82) is also known as: Opus 82 Set.
