## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Opus 66 is a compact supercardioid dynamic mic designed for percussion miking. Its small, end-address body has an integrated mic clip. It is part of Beyerdynamic’s “Opus” series of instrument microphones.

The mic ships with a storage bag (and the integrated MKV 87 mic clip).

Permalink: [beyerdynamic Opus-66](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/Opus-66)

The mic was released in 2001.
