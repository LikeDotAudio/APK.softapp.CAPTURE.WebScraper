## Omnidirectional Pencil Condenser Microphone

Beyerdynamic’s MM1 is a measurement mic — a small-diaphragm omnidirectional with a near-linear frequency response, packaged in a traditional tapered-body form factor. It is designed for calibration and measurement, rather than studio recording tasks.
