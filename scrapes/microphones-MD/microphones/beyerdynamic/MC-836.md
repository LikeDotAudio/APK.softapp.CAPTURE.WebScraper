## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The MC 836 is a “short shotgun” microphone, designed for news gathering and field production applications.

A switchable high-pass filter reduces low frequency sound below 90Hz.

For tighter pattern control, see the longer version, model MC 837. Its frequency response graph is nearly identical.

The [beyerdynamic MC 836](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/beyerdynamic/MC-836) is also known as: MC836.
