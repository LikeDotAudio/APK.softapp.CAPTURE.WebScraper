## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The M 58 is a handheld omni dynamic designed for Electric News Gathering (ENG) and Electronic Field Production (EFP) applications.

The dynamic capsule is shockmounted within the microphone to reduce handling noise.

The mic’s aluminum case is built to withstand the abuse of field use.
