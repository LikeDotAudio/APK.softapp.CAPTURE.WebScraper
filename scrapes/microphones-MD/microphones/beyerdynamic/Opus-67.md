## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Opus 67 is a miniature dynamic instrument mic with an integrated clip mount and gooseneck, allowing for easy placement on the rim of a snare drum, tom, or conga.

The mic has a hypercardioid pickup pattern.
