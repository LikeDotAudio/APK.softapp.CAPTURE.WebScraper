## Stereo Mic Set

The MC930 Stereo Set includes two of Beyerdynamic’s premium MC930 pencil condensers, with shockmounts and windscreens, in a padded hardshell plastic case.

The MC930 was reviewed in our [drum overhead microphone test](http://recordinghacks.com/2011/07/04/beyerdynamic-mc930-review-overheads/), and sounded wonderful.
