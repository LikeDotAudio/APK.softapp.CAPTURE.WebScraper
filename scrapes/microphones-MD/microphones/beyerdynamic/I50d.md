## Cardioidbeyerdynamic I50d

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The I50d is an all-purpose stage dynamic microphone, intended for close-miked drums, amplifiers, and brass instruments.

The mic has a flat response from 200Hz through 2kHz, with presence peaks at 5kHz and 10kHz. The high and low frequency responses fall off fairly sharply below 150Hz and above 10kHz.
