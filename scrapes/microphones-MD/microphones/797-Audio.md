## Beijing 797 Audio Co. Ltd.

797 Audio is one of the best-known Chinese manufacturers of audio gear.

The company partnered with US-based [Studio Projects](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Studio-Projects) to produce the first generation of Studio Projects-branded microphones. 797 is the source of some of the best K67-style condenser capsules outside of Neumann; the 797 capsule can be found in the [Soundelux U95](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Soundelux/U95) and numerous other mid- to high-end condensers.

797 Audio manufactures its own line of microphones, amplifiers, studio electronics, and loudspeakers, and continues to serve as OEM/Contract Manufacturer for many US and European brands.
