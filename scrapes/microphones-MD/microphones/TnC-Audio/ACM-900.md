## BidirectionalTNC Audio ACM-900

## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The ACM-900 is an active ribbon microphone with a tube amplifier/impedance conversion circuit. It was sold only as part of a “group buy” of low-cost microphones in 2007.

[![ACM-900 PCB](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/tnc/ACM900-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/tnc/ACM900-pcb.jpg)The circuit incorporates two transformers, the first of which presumably provides significant step-up gain. Both transformers are mounted in metal cans on the PCB.

The tube is a 6J1 subminiature pentode.

[![ACM-900 Side View](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/tnc/ACM900-side-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/tnc/ACM900-side.jpg)The mic included an elastic shockmount, power supply, cables, and a padded aluminum flightcase.
