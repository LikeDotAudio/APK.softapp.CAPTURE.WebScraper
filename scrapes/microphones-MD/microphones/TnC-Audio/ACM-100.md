## CardioidTNC Audio ACM-100

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The ACM-100 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser in a “lollipop” form factor. The mic was manufactured in China and sold as part of the TnC “Group Buy” of microphones and preamps in 2007.

[![ACM-100 PCB and Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/tnc/ACM100-open-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/tnc/ACM100-open.jpg)The mic used a large-diaphragm, center-terminated capsule that is a copy of the Neumann K67. The rear diaphragm was not sputtered, nor wired into the circuitry. The mic has a fixed Cardioid pickup pattern.

The amplifier circuit is transformerless, and uses through-hole components.
