## Bidirectional Active Ribbon Microphone

The ACM-200 is a phantom-powered dual-ribbon microphone, most likely manufactured by ShuaiYin (see their model SYFR-1).

Few specs are available at this time. (If you own one, feel free to send me the mic’s dimensions, weight, ribbon motor measurements, photos, etc.)

The [TNC Audio ACM-200](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/TnC-Audio/ACM-200) is also known as: ACM200.

The mic was released in 2008.
