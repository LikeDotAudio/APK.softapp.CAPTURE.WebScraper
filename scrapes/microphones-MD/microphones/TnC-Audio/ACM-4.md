## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The ACM-4 is a dual-ribbon microphone, manufactured by ShuaiYin (see their SYR50). It is likely identical to the Apex 215 and Avant CR-14 (see the sidebar).

(Photo courtesy drBill)

The [TNC Audio ACM-4](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/TnC-Audio/ACM-4) is also known as: ACM4.

The mic was released in 2008.
