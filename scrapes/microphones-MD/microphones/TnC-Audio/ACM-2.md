## BidirectionalTNC Audio ACM-2

## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The ACM-2 is a passive ribbon microphone with a 45mm aluminum ribbon. It appears to be another ShuaiYin SYR-30 clone, and is most likely identical to the t.bone RM700 and Nady RSM-4.

The large headbasket measures 3'' in diameter. The body diameter is ~7/8'' (22mm).

[![TnC Audio ACM-2 Microphone](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3293/3128515317_3b009f9d71_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/24455487@N05/3128515317/ "TnC Audio ACM-2 Microphone by recordinghacks, on Flickr")There is no circuit board or chassis under the body. The three screws nearest the head detach the capsule assembly and reveal the transformer; the bottom set of screws hold the XLR jack in place.

[![TnC Audio ACM-2 Microphone](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3223/3128515253_70b4acdc40_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/24455487@N05/3128515253/ "TnC Audio ACM-2 Microphone by recordinghacks, on Flickr")The front and back grilles are flat, rather than bowed (as in some SYR-30 derivatives).

Three layers of screening protect the ribbon element from blasts: the heavy outer grille (visible in the photos), a medium gauge layer of metal mesh just under the outer grille, and a layer of fine mesh mounted to the ribbon motor, covering just the ribbon.  
   
The ACM-2 ships in a padded wooden storage case with a plastic mic clip.

The [TNC Audio ACM-2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/TnC-Audio/ACM-2) is also known as: ACM2.

The mic was released in 2008.
