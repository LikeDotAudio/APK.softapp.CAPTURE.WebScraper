## BidirectionalSM Pro Audio MC04

## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The MC04 is passive ribbon microphone, made in China.

It is a large-geometry design, with a ribbon measuring approximately 50mm by 5mm, made of 2-micron aluminum.

![](http://www.emusician.com/emusician/images/Ribbon-Mic-Roundup-Fig.-7.gif)

> Mix - 2005  
> The SM Pro Audio MC 04 has an XLR connector hub on the back, allowing you to use any mic cable. This handy feature is somewhat negated by poor placement, because the connector hub protrudes out from the body far enough to impede upward angling, with or without an XLR cable attached.

The mic ships with:

* Light weight soft padded carry case
* Aluminium flight case
* Microphone cable
* Microphone stand mount
