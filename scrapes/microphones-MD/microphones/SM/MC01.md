## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The SM Pro Audio MC01 is a large diaphragm FET condenser, intended for general studio use.

The mic uses a large-diaphragm (1-inch) capsule, and Class A FET electronics with transformer coupled output. According to an MC01 owner, the capsule is a 32mm OD model, which we presume is the standard Chinese-made K67 copy.

-10dB pad and 100Hz bass roll-off are switchable on the mic body. A blue LED indicates power status.

The MC01 ships with an elastic shockmount and leather pouch.
