## Avenson Audio

Avenson Audio is a small manufacturer of pro audio gear. The company was founded by Brad Avenson, a designer of audio electronics whose product credits include mics, preamps, and consoles from Shiny Box, Wunder Audio, and Pete’s Place.

The Avenson Audio line includes one microphone (to date), two direct boxes, a headphone amp, and a mid-side matrixing device.
