## Royer Labs R-101 Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

Royer Labs hand-matches pairs of the R-101 passive ribbon mic. Mics are matched both for sensitivity (0.5dB) and for frequency response (±1dB).

The kit includes two elastic shockmounts, and a protective sock for each microphone. The mics and accessories are packed into a compact flightcase.

The mics are covered by a lifetime warranty, including a free first ribbon replacement.
