## Royer Labs R-121 Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

Pairs of R-121 passive ribbon microphones are matched by hand, both for sensitivity (0.5dB) and frequency response (±1dB), then packaged into individual wooden boxes. The stereo kit includes mic clips (but no shockmounts), and a protective sock for each microphone.

The mics are covered by a lifetime warranty, including a free first ribbon replacement.
