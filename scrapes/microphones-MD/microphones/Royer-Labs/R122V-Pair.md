## Royer Labs R-122V Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

Royer Labs hand-matches pairs of R-122V active ribbon microphones, both for sensitivity (0.5dB) and frequency response (±1dB). The kit includes power supplies, cables, protective wind socks, and hard mounts for the microphones. (Shockmounts are available separately.)

The mics are covered by a lifetime warranty, including a free first ribbon replacement.
