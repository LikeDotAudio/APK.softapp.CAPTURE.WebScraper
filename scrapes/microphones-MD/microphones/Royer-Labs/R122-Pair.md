## Royer Labs R-122 Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

The R-122 active ribbon mic is available in hand-matched stereo pairs. Mics are matched both for sensitivity (0.5dB) and for frequency response (±1dB).

The set includes two microphones, protective socks, wooden storage cases, and hard mounts. Shockmounts are available separately.

The mics are covered by a lifetime warranty, including a free first ribbon replacement.
