## M-Audio Pulsar II Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The Pulsar II Matched Pair is a simple stereo kit, with two of M-Audio’s Pulsar II FET pencil condensers. The kit includes a stereo bar for ORTF or XY mounting, two mic clips, two “mic socks,” and two foam windscreens, packed into a padded wooden presentation case.
