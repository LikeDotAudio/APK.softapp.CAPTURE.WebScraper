## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Luna II is a large diaphragm FET condenser microphone released in 2008 as an update to the 2003 Luna, adding -10dB pad and gentle 125Hz low-cut (6dB/octave) filter.

The Luna II’s specifications are very similar to M-Audio’s multipattern [Solaris](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/M-Audio/solaris).

The mic ships with a metal shockmount and wooden case.

The [M-Audio Luna II](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/M-Audio/Luna-II) is also known as: Luna 2.

The mic was released in 2008.
