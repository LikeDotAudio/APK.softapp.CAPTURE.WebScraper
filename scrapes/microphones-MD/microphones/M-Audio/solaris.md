## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Solaris is a large diaphragm FET condenser with 3 polar patterns, -10dB pad and gentle 125Hz low-cut filter.

Like M-Audio’s other studio condenser mics, the Luna and [Sputnik](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/M-Audio/sputnik), the Solaris features a lollipop-style machined metal body. The Solaris has a matte silver finish. The metal used for the body is not specified.

> Electronic Musician 2004  
> The highs of the Solaris weren’t quite as smooth as those of the [Dragonfly](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Blue-Microphones/Dragonfly) and the [C 3000 B](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/AKG-Acoustics/C-3000-B), but the overall sound was clean and clear. The Solaris was especially nice on a soft-spoken female vocalist reading text, because it provided good definition on a quiet, smooth voice without emphasizing sibilance.

In 2006, the mic’s MSRP was $349.95.

The Solaris is packaged with a shockmount and aluminum case.
