## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The MTP 240 DM is Lewitt’s entry-level dynamic vocal microphone, intended for live vocal reproduction. It has a fixed Cardioid pickup pattern.

[![Lewitt Audio MTP 240 DM](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/MTP240DM-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/lewitt/MTP240DM.jpg)The mic’s frequency response is nominally flat from 150Hz to 2kHz, with a broad, smooth +4dB presence boost from 4–14kHz. The high-frequency response drops rapidly above 15kHz.

Two versions of the microphone are available:

* **MTP 240 DM** - no switch
* **MTP 240 DMs** - Includes on/off switch on mic body

[![Lewitt Audio MTP 540 DMs](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/MTP540DMs-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/lewitt/MTP540DMs-kit.jpg)Both versions are packaged identically to the pictured MTP 540 DMs: the mics ship in a padded cardboard box, with a mic clip (Lewitt p/n MTP 40 MCs) and a leather storage pouch (p/n DTP 40 Lb).

The [Lewitt Audio MTP 240 DM](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Lewitt/MTP-240-DM) is also known as: MTP-240-DMS.

The mic was released in 2010.
