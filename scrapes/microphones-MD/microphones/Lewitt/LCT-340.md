## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The LCT 340 is Lewitt Audio’s flagship small-diaphragm condenser microphone. It is a transformerless FET design with interchangeable half-inch capsules. Currently, Cardioid and Omni capsules are provided.

Two switches on the mic body provide a variety of pad and filter options. A backlighted legend indicates the current selection for each.

[![Lewitt Audio LCT-340](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/LCT340-stand-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/lewitt/LCT340-stand.jpg)The 4-way pad attenuates the signal by 0dB, 6dB, 12dB, or 18dB.

The 4-way high-pass filter can be set to:

* flat/disabled
* -6dB/octave @ 300 Hz
* -6dB/octave @ 150 Hz
* -12dB/octave @ 40 Hz

The two included capsules use externally-polarized (true condenser) elements measuring 12mm in diameter. They are coupled to a low-noise transformerless amplifier circuit. The published self-noise figure for this microphone, 13dBA, is very low for a small-diaphragm design.

![Lewitt Audio DTP 40 Mts](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/DTP40Mts-shockmount.jpg)The mic ships with two interchangeable capsules (cardioid and omni) in a foam-lined cardboard box. It includes a rubberized mic clip (Lewitt Audio p/n DTP 40 Mts), foam windscreen (LCT 40 Ws), and leather storage pouch (p/n DTP 40 Lb).

As of 2012, Lewitt is offering the microphone with only the Cardioid capsule for a reduced price of MSRP $519.
