## Lewitt Audio DTP Beat Kit 6

## 6-Mic Set

The Beat Kit 6 is a six-piece drum miking kit, consisting of Lewitt’s lower-priced microphones:

* One DTP 340 REX for Kick Drum
* Three DTP 340 TT dynamics for toms, with DTP 40 Mt rim mounts and DTP 40 Mts rubber isolation mounts
* Two LCT 140 pencil condensers for overheads, with DTP 40 Mts rubber isolation mounts

The kit ships in a padded black aluminum flight case.
