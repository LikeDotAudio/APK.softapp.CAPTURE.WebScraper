## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MTP 340 CM is a handheld condenser microphone with a small-diameter electret capsule. It is intended primarily for stage vocal applications.

The mic’s frequency response is flat from 150Hz–3kHz, with a gentle presence rise (+3dB) through 15k that is offset by a sharper ~2dB dip at 8–9kHz. The cardioid pickup pattern is consistent above 1kHz.

[![Lewitt Audio MTP 340 CM](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/MTP340CM-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/lewitt/MTP340CM.jpg)At 3.1 mV/Pa, the mic is slightly more sensitive than Lewitt’s handheld dynamics, e.g. the MTP 540 DP at 2 mV/Pa, but less sensitive than comparable condensers such as Blue’s Encore 300 (11 mV/Pa), Neumann’s [KMS 104](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/KMS-104) (4.5 mV/Pa), or Audio-Technica’s [AE3300](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/AE3300) (7.9 mV/Pa).

Despite the similarity in model names, the MTP 340 CM uses a different condenser capsule than the [LCT 340](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Lewitt/LCT-340) pencil mic.

Two versions of the microphone are available:

* **MTP 340 CM** - no switch
* **MTP 340 CMs** - Includes on/off switch on mic body

[![Lewitt Audio MTP 540 DMs](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lewitt/MTP540DMs-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/lewitt/MTP540DMs-kit.jpg)Both are packaged identically to the other MTP mics (such as the 540 DMs pictured at right); they ship in a padded cardboard box, with a mic clip (Lewitt p/n MTP 40 MCs) and an embossed leather storage pouch (p/n DTP 40 Lb).

The [Lewitt Audio MTP 340 CM](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Lewitt/MTP-340-CM) is also known as: MTP 340 CMs.

The mic was released in 2010.
