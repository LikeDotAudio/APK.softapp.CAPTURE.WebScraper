## Lewitt Audio DTP Beat Kit Pro 7

## 7-Mic Set

The Beat Kit Pro 7 is Lewitt Audio’s premium drum microphone kit. It contains seven microphones:

* One DTP 640 REX dual-element kick drum mic
* Two LCT 340 pencil condensers for overheads, with rubber isolation mounts
* Three DTP 340 TT dynamic mics for toms, with DTP 40 Mts rubber isolation mounts and DTP 40 Mt rim mounts
* One DTP 440 DM dynamic mic for snare drum, with a mic clip and DTP 40 Mt rim mount

The kit ships in a padded black aluminum flight case. Two versions are available:

* **DTP Beat Kit Pro 7** - includes both Cardioid and Omni capsules for LCT 340 pair: $1699 MSRP
* **DTP Beat Kit Pro 7 Cardioid** - no Omni capsules: $1299
