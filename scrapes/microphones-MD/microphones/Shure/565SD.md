## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Shipped connected for low-impedance operation, 565SDprovides excellent vocal or speech reproduction on lecterns and stages,and is a popular choice for use in public auditoriums, churches,convention halls, and schools.

* Versatile, high-quality microphone with sound characteristics similar to the SM58®
  
* Selectable dual-impedance operation
  
* Silent magnetic reed on/off switch with lock-on option
* Cardioid dynamic
  
* Frequency response: 50 to 15,000 Hz

The [Shure 565SD](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/565SD) is also known as: unisphere.
