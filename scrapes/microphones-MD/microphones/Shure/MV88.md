## Mid-Side Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The Shure MV88 is a stereo mid/side mic that connects directly to any iOS device with a Lightning port.

The MV88 can be operated through the free Shure MOTIV app, which allows control of stereo width, 5-band EQ, compression, limiting, and/or any of five DSP presets (speech, singing, acoustic, loud, flat).

The microphone has two electret capsules, each 1cm in diameter. One is cardioid (end address), and the other is figure-8 (side address), making the microphone a mid-side stereo mic. The mic has a hinge that allows the mic to pivot 90 degrees.

Various sample and bit rates are selectable using the MOTIV app, with a maximum resolution of 24bits and a 48kHz sample rate.

The MV88 comes with a windscreen, an 1/8-inch headphone extender cable, and a zippered carrying case.

The [Shure MV88](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/MV88) is also known as: MV-88, MV 88, .

The mic was released in 2016.
