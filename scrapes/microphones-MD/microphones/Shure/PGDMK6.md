## Shure PGDMK6

## 6-Mic Set

The PGDMK6 is a six-mic drum kit microphone set. It adds a pair of overhead mics to the PGDMK4 (which includes a kick mic and three snare/tom mics). Included:

* One PG52 dynamic for kick drum
* Three PG56 for snare and toms, with three A50D rim mounts
* Two PG81 pencil condensers for overheads

The kit also includes six 15-foot XLR cables, and a padded plastic carrying case.
