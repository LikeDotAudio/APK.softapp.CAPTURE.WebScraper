## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Shure’s 515X is an inexpensive dynamic mic with a cardioid pickup pattern. It is available in numerous form factors:

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| 515BSM | Handheld, Cardioid Dynamic, Low Impedance, Push-to-Talk Switch, Attached Cable |
| 515BSLX | Cardioid Dynamic, for Gooseneck Mounting, Low Impedance, Locking On/Off Slide Switch, Attached Cable, Gooseneck not Included |
| 515SBGX | Cardioid Dynamic, for Gooseneck Mounting, Low Impedance, Push-to-Talk Switch, Attached Cable, without Gooseneck |
| 515SBG-18X | Cardioid Dynamic, Low Impedance, Push-to-Talk Switch, with 18-inch (45 cm) Gooseneck and A13HD Mounting Flange |
| 515SBG-18XF | Cardioid Dynamic, Low Impedance, Attached 18-inch (46 cm) Gooseneck, Push-to-Talk Switch, with A12 Flange |
| 515SDX | Cardioid Dynamic, Handheld, High or Low Impedance (Plug Selectable), Locking On/Off Switch |

The [Shure 515X](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/515X-Series) is also known as: 515XSeries, 515X Series.
