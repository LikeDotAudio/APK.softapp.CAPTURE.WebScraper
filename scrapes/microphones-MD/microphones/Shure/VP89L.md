## ShotgunShure VP89L

## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The VP89L is a professional full-length shotgun microphone. It is part of the VP89 series of modular shotguns, and consists of the VP89 preamplifier (p/n RPM89/PRE) plus the longest available capsule head (p/n RPM89L), measuring 386mm (or 488 including the head amp).

Two shorter capsule heads are available, with progressively wider acceptance angles (due to their shorter interference tubes):

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Module | Length | Acceptance Angle |
| RPM89/PRE head amp | 102mm | – |
| RPM89S Short Capsule | 137mm | 70° |
| RPM89M Med. Capsule | 239mm | 50° |
| RPM89L Long Capsule | 386mm | 30° |

The VP89L has a narrow acceptance angle of only 30 degrees. Its requency response has a natural taper below 200 Hz, which is steeper with the high-pass filter engaged, and increased HF response approximately +5dB from 6kHz - 20kHz.

The capsule assembly is made from aircraft-grade aluminum, within which is the aluminum interference tube and half-inch electret capsule.

The VP89M includes the VP89 head amplifier, the long capsule head, a foam windscreen (p/n A89LW), and a cylindrical storage case (p/n A89LC).

[![A89U Adapter for VP89 Shotgun Microphone](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shure/A89U-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shure/A89U.jpg)Optional accessories include Shure’s unique U-shaped “double-barrel adapter,” (p/n A89U) which allows the long capsule head to be mounted parallel to the head amplifier, reducing the overall length of the assembly.

Industry-standard “softie” windscreen accessories are made for the VP89 by Rycote. The company also offers numerous shockmounts based on its Lyre suspension technology; the selection includes a pistol grip (Shure p/n A89M-PG), a camera clamp adapter (p/n A89M-CC), a hotshoe mount (p/n A89M-SH), and a boom adapter (p/n A89LM-BA).
