## Shure PGDMK4

## 4-Mic Set

The PGDMK4 / PGDMK4XLR is Shure’s entry-level drum microphone kit. It includes:

* One PG52 for kick drum
* Three PG56 dynamic tom mics, with A50D rim mounts

Also included are four 15-foot XLR cables, and a rigid plastic carrying case.
