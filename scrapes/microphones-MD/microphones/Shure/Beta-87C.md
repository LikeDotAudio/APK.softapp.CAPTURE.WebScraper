## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Beta 87C is a handheld condenser vocal mic. It is identical to the [Beta 87A](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/Beta-87A), although with a cardioid pickup pattern.

> [Shure FAQ](http://shure.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/shure.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=527)The 87C was introduced for performers using in-ear monitors. In brief, a cardioid mic is often preferred by in-ear monitor users as it sounds more “natural” with the in-ear mix.
>
> If you use floor monitors, the BETA 87A may be a better choice as it will often provide a bit more gain before feedback than the BETA 87C.

The [Shure Beta 87C](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/Beta-87C) is also known as: Beta87C, Beta 87C.
