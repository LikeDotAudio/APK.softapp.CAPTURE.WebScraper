## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PG58 is an entry-level dynamic vocal mic, styled much like the SM58.

Like most stage dynamic microphones, the PG58 has a high max SPL rating. The mic produces a Cardioid pickup pattern.

The rare-earth Neodymium magnet in the moving-coil cartridge gives the mic higher output levels (sensitivity 2.2 mV/Pa) than many comparable mics, including the SM58 (1.85 mV/Pa).

The moving-coil cartridge is shockmounted to reduce handling noise.

The mic has an on/off switch.

The mic is available with three choices of accessories:

* **PG58-QTR** - Includes 15' (4.57m) XLR-to-1/4'' audio cable, mic clip, european mic stand thread adapter, and a storage pouch
* **PG58-XLR** - As above, with standard XLR cable in place of the 1/4'' cable
* **PG58-LC** - includes mic clip, thread adapter, and storage pouch (no cable)
