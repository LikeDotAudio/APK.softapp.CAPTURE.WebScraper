## Detachable-Capsule Condenser Microphone

The Beta 98 is a high performance mini-condenser microphone designed for drums, cymbals, and percussion. According to Shure, it is the replacement for the SM98 microphone, which employed a capsule that was “difficult to manufacture.”[\*](http://shure.custhelp.com/cgi-bin/shure.cfg/php/enduser/std_adp.php?p_faqid=1022&&p_topview=1)

The mic has a unique two-piece design, in which the inline preamp (Shure p/n RPM626) is housed separately from the capsule housing.

As the successor to the SM98A, the Beta 98 features several improvements. Dynamic range is 4 dB greater, as is headroom, while maximum SPL is 5 dB higher (now 160 dB!). Physically, the mic utilizes a thicker detachable cable.

Three mounting options are available:

* A98D drum mount (included with model Beta 98D/S)
* Shockmount swivel adapter (included with model Beta 98/S)
* A98KCS universal horn mount

The A98D gooseneck drum mount has been redesigned for quicker, more secure rim-mounting without the need for a drum key.

[![RPM626 Preamp](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shure/RPM626-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shure/RPM626.jpg)The Beta 98 ships with an inline preamp (RPM626) and the necessary cable to connect the capsule to the preamp, a soft case (26B21), and either a swivel mount (for Beta 98/S) or gooseneck rim mount (for Beta 98D/S).

The [Shure Beta-98](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/Beta-98) is also known as: Beta-98D-S, Beta 98D-S, Beta98DS, Beta98S, Beta 98S.
