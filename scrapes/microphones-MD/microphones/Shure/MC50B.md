## OmnidirectionalShure MC50B

## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

Features

* Extended frequency response with user changeable equalization caps for response shaping (omni only)
* Low visibility with a variety of options for mounting
* Low handling noise
* Legendary Shure quality, ruggedness, and reliability
* Includes In-line preamplifier and 5-foot (1.5m) cable with TA4F connector
* All models include magnet mount, lapel clip, dual tie clip, pin mount, and two foam windscreens

The [Shure MC50B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/MC50B) is also known as: MC50B-MC51B.
