## Shure DMK57-52

## 4-Mic Set

The DMK57-52 is a four-mic kit consisting of Shure’s classic drum microphones:

* One Beta 52A for kick drum
* Three SM57 for snare and toms, with A56D rim clamps

[![Shure A56D Rim Clamp](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shure/A56D-sm.jpg)](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shure/A56D.jpg)
