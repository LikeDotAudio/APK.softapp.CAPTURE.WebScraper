## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PG57 is an entry-level instrument microphone, part of Shure’s ‘PG’ series. In general, the SM series mics outperform the PG mics in most respects.

Features include: hardened steel grille, internal shock-mounting, neodymium magnets in capsule, on/off switch.

The mic includes a cable, mic clip and storage bag.
