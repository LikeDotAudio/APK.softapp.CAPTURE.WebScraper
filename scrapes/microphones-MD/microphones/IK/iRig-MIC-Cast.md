## CardioidIK Multimedia iRig MIC Cast

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The iRig MIC Cast is a compact external microphone designed for Apple’s iPhone. It uses the TRRS (combination microphone/earphone jack) and is compatible with Android phones and tables, as well as the iPod Touch and iPad.

[![iRig MIC Cast](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/ik/irig_mic_cast-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/ik/irig_mic_cast.jpg)The element is a miniature electret condenser with a Cardioid polar pattern. The mic has two sensitivity levels, selectable via switch on the face of the unit.

The iRig MIC Cast has a passthrough headphone jack (1/8''/3.5mm) to enable monitoring.

The product is designed primarily for voice recording: conferencing, Skype, podcasting, etc.

[![IK Desk Stand](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/ik/irig-stand-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/ik/irig-stand.jpg)It ships with the iRig Recorder and VocaLive iOS apps, as well as iRig Recording FREE for Android. It includes a lightweight, compact desk stand for smartphones, so that the iRig MIC Cast can stand up away from the surface of a desk or table.
