## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The Cyclops is an original, US-made ribbon microphone from Ocean Park Audio Labs founder Peter Alton. It is a passive ribbon with a natural bidirectional pickup pattern.

The mic’s design was inspired by the Rauland Univel and Duovel, the [American DR 330](http://www.coutant.org/american/index.html), and Geloso ribbon mics whose iconic looks are prized by collectors.

Each microphone is completely handmade. The body is machined from polished aluminum and Delrin (acetyl resin plastic). A built-in yoke mount saves the need for external mic clips; the mic’s angle within the yoke can be adjusted via a pair of bright red thumbscrew knobs.

[![OPAL ribbon motor prototype](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/opal/ribbon_motor_rear_sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/opal/ribbon_motor_rear.jpg)The ribbon motor incorporates a 2.25'' (57mm) length of corrugated 1.8-micron aluminum and twin 52mm Neodymium magnets. Pictured at right is a prototype ribbon motor similar to the one developed for the Cyclops.
