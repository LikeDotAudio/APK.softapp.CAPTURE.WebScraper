## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The Clarion FC-357 is a multipattern FET condenser with transformer-coupled output.

The capsule diameter measures 35mm, with a diaphragm diameter of 28.25mm. The capsule is a dual-diaphragm, center-terminated design; its manufacture is closely monitored to ensure that diaphragm tension and damping are to specification. The diaphragm is made of gold-coated Dupont Mylar.

The mic offers three pickup patterns (cardioid, omni, and figure-of-8) that can be switched via the microphone body.

A second switch offers a common -10dB pad and a unique +10dB high-gain position (as well as a neutral middle position). The pad allows the mic to be used at high-SPL sources without overdriving the mic’s amplifier. The +10dB position could be used for quiet sources, and/or to reduce the amount of gain applied by the mic pre. This switch allows the user to “color” the mic by using more or less preamp gain for any given source.

![Lauten FC-357 Clarion Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/lauten/fc357_case.gif)The Clarion ships in a flight case with a suspension shockmount, foam windscreen, and a sustainably-produced wooden storage box (for the mic).

The [Lauten Audio FC-357 Clarion](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Lauten/Clarion) is also known as: FC-357, FC357.

The mic was released in 2008.
