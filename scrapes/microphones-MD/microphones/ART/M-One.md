## CardioidART Pro Audio M-One

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The M-One is a fixed-cardioid FET condenser microphone.

The diaphragm is a large-diaphragm design with a 32mm capsule. The diaphragm diameter is likely 25mm.

An LED inside the headbasket indicates when the required +48V phantom power is present.

The mic is presented as a “no frills” design; users looking for a pad or high-pass filter can find those features on the M-Two.

The mic includes a ring mount.

The M-One is manufactured in China, according to the product spec sheet.

Permalink: [ART Pro Audio M-One](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ART/M-One)

The mic was released in 2009.
