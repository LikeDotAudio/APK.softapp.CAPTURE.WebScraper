## CardioidART Pro Audio M-One/USB

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The M-One/USB is ART’s M-One microphone with an integrated 24-bit, 48-kHz ADC and a low-latency headphone jack. Both mics are large-diaphragm, fixed-cardioid designs.

Both mics have wire-mesh screens and lightweight zinc/aluminum shells. Both mics ship with a stand mount. The USB version also includes a soft pouch for storage, a 10' USB cable, and a shockmount.

The USB mic has two dials on the mic body. One controls the gain of the built-in headphone jack. The other controls the mix level, allowing the user to adjust the relative levels of the live signal (as captured by the mic) versus any pre-recorded material (playing back from the computer).

The mic was introduced at Summer NAMM 2009, and is expected to begin shipping in August 2009.
