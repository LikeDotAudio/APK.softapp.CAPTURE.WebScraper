## RMS Audioworks

RMS Audioworks is a small boutique microphone manufacturer in Chicago, Illinois. Founded and run by Robert Sestili, the company does vintage microphone restoration and repair, in addition to manufacturing replicas of numerous vintage microphones, including the U47, C12, and U49.
