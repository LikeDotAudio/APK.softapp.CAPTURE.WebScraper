## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The KM202-1 is a revision of the company’s KM202. Both are high-SPL condenser microphones intended for use on kick drum.

The “-1” revision is slightly more sensitive, at 1mV/Pa. The body metalwork has changed somewhat. Elation has not described the nature of the changes from the original KM202.

Permalink: [Elation Mic Lab KM202-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Elation/KM202-1)

The mic was released in 2011.
