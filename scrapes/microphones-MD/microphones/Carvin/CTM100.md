## 9 polar patternsCarvin Corporation CTM100

## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Carvin CTM100 is an Alctron HST-11a, manufactured in China and available in the US and Europe under numerous brand names.

[![CTM100](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/carvin/CTM100-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/carvin/CTM100-pcb.jpg)It is a 9-pattern tube mic with included power supply and shockmount. As do all the rebadged HST-11a mics, the CTM100 responds well to modification; search the web for “Apex 460 mod” to find relevant message-board discussions, or see our [Apex 460](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/460) page for a starter list of common mods.

Because Carvin has not supplied frequency-response graphs for the CMT100, the graphs below are from the HST-11A.
