## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The SYT1200 is a multipattern, large-diaphragm tube mic, styled after the AKG C12. It uses a 12AX7 tube that is believed to be socketed within the preamp circuit.

The mic includes a HPF and pad (details forthcoming).

The [ShuaiYin SYT1200](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYT1200) is also known as: SYT-1200.

The mic was released in 2005.
