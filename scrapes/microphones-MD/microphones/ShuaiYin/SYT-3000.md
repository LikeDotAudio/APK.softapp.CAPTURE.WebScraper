## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The SYT-3000 is a large-diaphragm tube mic. A pattern-selection switch on the front of the mic body offers cardioid, omni, and figure-of-8 pickup patterns. Two switches on the rear of the mic provide an attenuation pad and HPF.

> ShuaiYin
>
> We use triode wiring on specially selected low-noise pentode vacuum tube (6J1) to yield low noise and incredibly realistic dynamic response.

Internal shockmounting is claimed to make an external shockmount unnecessary; the SYT-3000 ships with a yoke mount.

The [ShuaiYin SYT-3000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYT-3000) is also known as: SYT3000.
