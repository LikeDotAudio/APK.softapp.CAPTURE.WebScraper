## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The SYT-2000 tube mic provides three pickup patterns, controlled via a switch on the microphone body. We speculate that internally it is very similar to the SYT-3000, which also has a pattern-selection switch on the front, and HPF and pad switches on the rear. Capsule details are forthcoming, unless this is a Chinese holiday weekend.

The tube is a “specially selected low-noise pentode vacuum tube (6J1),” wired as a triode.

The [ShuaiYin SYT-2000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYT-2000) is also known as: SYT2000.
