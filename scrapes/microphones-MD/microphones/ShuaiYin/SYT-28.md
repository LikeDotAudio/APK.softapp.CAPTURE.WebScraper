## ShuaiYin SYT-28

## Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

ShuaiYin’s SYT-28 is a small-diaphragm tube condenser with interchangeable capsule elements. The company provides four different capsules: cardioid, wide cardioid, hypercardioid, and omnidirectional; these are installed by removing the metal head assembly from the microphone, then changing the capsule element inside.

[![ShuayYin SYT-28 Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shuaiyin/syt-28-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shuaiyin/syt-28-kit.jpg)The SYT-28 kit includes the tube mic body, up to four detachable capsule heads (omni, hyper, cardioid, wide cardioid), leather bag, dedicated power supply (p/n SYD-2), suspension shockmount (p/n SYZ-9), foam windscreen, 7-pin XLR power cable (10 meters), and an aluminum flight case.

[![ShuayYin SYT-28 shockmount](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shuaiyin/syt-28-mount-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shuaiyin/syt-28-mount.jpg)

The [ShuaiYin SYT-28](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYT-28) is also known as: SYT28, SYT-28A, SYT-28B.
