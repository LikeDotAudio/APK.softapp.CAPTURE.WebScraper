## X/Y Stereo Ribbon Microphone

The SYRS-32 is an XY-stereo ribbon microphone, created by positioning two SYR-40s end-to-end, one rotated 90°. Both elements are passive ribbons, wired separately to a 5-pin XLR jack (split via the included Y-cable into twin 3-pin XLRM jacks).

The [ShuaiYin SYRS-32](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYRS-32) is also known as: SYRS32.
