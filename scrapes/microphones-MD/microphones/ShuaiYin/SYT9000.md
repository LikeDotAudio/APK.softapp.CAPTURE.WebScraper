## Omnidirectional Tube Condenser Microphone

The SYT9000 is a bottle-style multipattern tube condenser microphone. It was never sold in the US commercially, but is reportedly the source of lookalike microphones sold under other names.

A pattern switch on the front of the microphone body allows selection of Cardioid, Omni, or Figure-8 patterns.

The Class-A amplifier circuit is based on the 6J1B subminiature pentode (a tube more commonly found in small-diaphragm “pencil” microphones).
