## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

ShuaiYin’s SYC3000 is pictured on the company website, but no specifications are published. The mic is manufactured exclusively for one of ShuaiYin’s OEM clients — presumably CAD, as this mic is believed to be the Trion 6000.

The mic’s self-noise is 20dBA.

The [ShuaiYin SYC3000](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYC3000) is also known as: SYC-3000.

The mic was released in 2005.
