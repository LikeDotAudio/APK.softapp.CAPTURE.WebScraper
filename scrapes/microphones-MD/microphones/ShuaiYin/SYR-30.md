## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The ShuaiYin SYR-30 is a lollipop-style ribbon mic with specs that match Alctron’s HRM-15. Given that Alctron is known to sell 3rd-party products under its own name, it’s possible that the SYR-30 is actually the source of all the rebadged HRM-15s, e.g. the Nady RSM-4, Cascade Fathead, etc.

The [ShuaiYin SYR-30](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYR-30) is also known as: SYR30.
