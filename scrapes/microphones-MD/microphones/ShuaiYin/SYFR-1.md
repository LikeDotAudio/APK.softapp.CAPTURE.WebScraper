## Bidirectional Active Ribbon Microphone

ShuaiYin’s SYFR-1 is perhaps the first inexpensive phantom-powered ribbon microphone on the market. It is today best known as the ACM-200, part of a “group buy” of Chinese microphones organized on various audio forums by TnC Audio.

The SYFR-1 has a dual-ribbon motor.

Only minimal specs have been made available thus far.

The [ShuaiYin SYFR-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYFR-1) is also known as: SYFR1.
