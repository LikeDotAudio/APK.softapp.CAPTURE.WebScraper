## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

ShuaiYin’s SYR50 is most likely the original model of the rectangular form-factor dual-ribbon mics available from numerous rebadgers.

The [ShuaiYin SYR50](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/ShuaiYin/SYR50) is also known as: SYR-50.
