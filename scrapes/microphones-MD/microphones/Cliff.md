## Cliff Mics

Cliff Mics is the eponymous corporate identity for inventor Cliff Henricksen, who introduced his RM1 ribbon microphone at the AES conference in Los Angeles in October, 2014.

The company’s initial product, the RM1, is manufactured in the US.
