## Detachable-Capsule Tube Condenser Microphone

The Red Type A was manufactured by Blue Mics as one of a pair of low-cost microphone bodies that served as the foundation for a system of interchangeable capsules. The Type A was a tube condenser; the Type B was a FET condenser.

The tube was an ECC88; this is a dual-triode tube. The output circuit is transformerless; it uses 3 transistors (presumably one phase-splitter and two bipolars) to produce balanced output.

![Type A Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/blue/TypeA-kit.jpg)As of May, 2007, pricing for the Type A was as follows:

* $1345 for the Type A body, no capsule
* $649 for most of the Red capsules (R0-R7, not R4)
* $849 for the R4, aka “The Big Omni,” described as a “small diaphragm sphere pressure omni” capsule

The Red mic line was discontinued in 2008, and rebranded by Blue as the Stage 1 and Stage 2; the capsules are now called Bottle Rockets.

The [Red Microphones Type A](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Red/Type-A) is also known as: typea.
