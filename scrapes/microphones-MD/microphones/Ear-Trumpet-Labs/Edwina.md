## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Edwina is a large-diaphragm electret condenser with distinctive visual style. Like all Ear Trumpet Labs microphones, it is designed for both stage and studio use.

The mic utilizes a transformerless FET circuit and Chinese-made TSB-2555 electret condenser capsule, yoke-mounted to facilitate positioning. The capsule can be swiveled to accommodate both side-address and end-address orientations.

[![Edwina microphone](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/eartrumpet/edwina-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/eartrumpet/edwina.jpg)The impedance conversion circuit includes a bass rolloff to compensate for proximity effect. High frequency equalization is used to reduce sibilance.

The frequency response plot shows a nominally flat response to 3kHz, rising to +5dB at 10kHz and fast roll off beyond 15kHz.
