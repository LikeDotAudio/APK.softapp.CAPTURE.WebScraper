## Senal

Senal Sound is the pro audio brand of Gradus Group, a family of companies in the pro photography/video/lighting industry. The product line focuses on sound for photo and video; it is composed primarily of shotgun and lavalier mics.

The company also makes nearfield monitors for studio use.
