## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The X-M is a compact omni condenser built around a 6mm electret capsule.

The X-M is offered with three output options:

1. **active-balanced output (transformerless); normal sensitivity** - requires 12V phantom; 20 - 20kHz response; self-noise 22 dBA; sensitivity 40 mV/Pa
2. **active-balanced output (transformerless); low sensitivity** - requires 12V phantom; 20 - 20kHz response; self-noise 25 dBA; sensitivity 10 mV/Pa
3. **transformer-balanced output** - runs on 9V phantom; 40 - 20kHz response; self-noise 21 dBA; sensitivity 5.6 mV/Pa

Further, the X-M can be made with three response options:

1. **free-field** - flat HF response on-axis, for use in near-field instrument miking or measurement applications where neutral high-frequency response is desired.
2. **diffuse-field** - rising HF response above 10kHz, peaking at +10dB @ 20kHz.
3. **switched** - for a $10 charge, the X-M can be prepared with a switchable EQ circuit that offers both free-field and diffuse-field responses in one microphone

[![Naiant X-M in clip](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/naiant/XM-clip-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/naiant/XM-clip.jpg)All three response options are available with any of the three output-circuit options, yielding a total of 9 varieties of X-M. Note that the sensitivity, frequency response, self-noise, and power requirements differ across these choices. See the Naiant site for full details.

Like all Naiant microphones, the X-M is built to order by Jon O’Neil, and carries a 5-year warranty. It is also available in matched pairs for stereo recording.
