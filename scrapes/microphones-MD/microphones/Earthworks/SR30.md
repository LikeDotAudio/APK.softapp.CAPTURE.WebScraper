## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The SR30 is a cardioid condenser microphone with extremely flat frequency response across an extended frequency range, and Earthworks’ trademark “fast impulse response” capsule.

[![Earthworks SR77](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/earthworks/SR77-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/earthworks/SR77.jpg)The mic was initially introduced in 2000, as the SR77. According to Earthworks, the two mics are the same.

The SR77 is reportedly identical internally to an older mic, Earthworks’ Z30X. According to Electronic Musician, the Z30X had a 3-piece stainless-steel body, whereas the SR77 packaged the same capsule and electronics into a 1-piece aluminum body that could be produced more easily.

See the [Z30X](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Earthworks/Z30X) profile for reviews.

[![SR25](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/earthworks/cardioid-patent-sm.png)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/earthworks/cardioid-patent.png)The mic’s physical housing, particularly the internal components engineered to produce a consistent Cardioid polar response, are covered by David Blackmer’s July, 2000 patent #6,091,829. The patent describes a conical, tapered structure behind the capsule, which serves multiple benefits to the mic’s polar response.

> Patent 6,091,829
>
> The tapered structure has a generally conic shape to provide a gradually increasing acoustic impedance… [it] preferentially collects signal components from behind the microphone and ducts them into the rear ports of the [capsule]; and guides reflected, unwanted signals outwardly and away…
>
> [It] improves the front frequency response of the microphone, and [provides] more-effective rear-port signal cancellation, thereby providing a better front-to-back signal ratio than provided in the prior art.

*How does the SR30 differ from the SR25?*

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | [**SR25** Earthworks Audio SR25](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Earthworks/SR25 "Earthworks Audio SR25") | [**SR30** Earthworks Audio SR30](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Earthworks/SR30 "Earthworks Audio SR30") |
| **Sensitivity** | 10 mV/Pa | 10 mV/Pa |
| **Frequency Response** | 50 - 25,000 Hz ±2dB | 30 - 30,000 Hz ±1.5dB |
| **Self-Noise** | 22 dBA | 22 dBA |
| **Max SPL** | 145dB | 145dB |
| **Length** | 165mm | 212mm |
| **Weight** | 160g | 225g |
| **MSRP** (2010) | $795 | $1095 |

The SR30 is also available in matched pairs; the pair of mics ships with two mic clips in a cherry-wood box.

> Electronic Musician, 2000
>
> [T]he SR77 maintained its reputation for capturing realistic, uncolored sound with exceptional dynamic response. However, compared to some of the other mics’ tracks, the realism was not always flattering to the source.
