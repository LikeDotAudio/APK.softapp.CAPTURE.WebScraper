## Earthworks Audio M30mp

## Stereo Mic Set

The Earthworks M30mp is a matched pair of M30 condensers.

The M30 is an electret condenser with flat frequency response up to 30kHz.

The M30mp ships with 2 plastic clips and a cherry wood box.
