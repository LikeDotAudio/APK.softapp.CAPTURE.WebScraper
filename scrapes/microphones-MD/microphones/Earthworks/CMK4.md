## Earthworks Audio CMK4

## 4-Mic Set

The CMK4 is a set of mics, inline pads and clamps for close drum miking of snare and 3 toms. The set consists of:

* One DP25/C Periscope Snare mic
* Three DP30/C Periscope Tom Mics
* Four Level Pad inline attenuators
* Four RM1 RimMounts
* Four PW1 Foam Windscreens
* One CMK-C High Impact Carrying Case

The kit does not include a kick drum microphone. It could be paired with one of Earthworks’ high-definition Cardioid condensers and a KickPad inline pad/filter device.
