## Earthworks Audio DK25/L

## 3-Mic Set

The DK25/L is the entry-level drum kit microphone set from Earthworks. It is a 3-mic set, containing three of the company’s cardioid SR25 microphones, plus one KickPad inline pad/filter device for use on the kick drum.

It is intended to be used alone, or to supplement close mics, either in the studio or on stage. It ships in a compact padded metal flightcase with a single foam windscreen (presumably for the kick mic, to attenuate the air blast).

See also the DK25/R, which swaps two of the Cardioid mics here for omnis.
