## Earthworks Audio CMK5

## 5-Mic Set

The CMK5 is a set of mics, inline pads and clamps for close drum miking of snare and 4 toms. It is Earthworks’ CMK4, with an additional DP30/C tom microphone.

The CMK5 includes:

* One DP25/C Periscope Snare mic
* Four DP30/C Periscope Tom Mics
* Five Level Pad inline attenuators
* Five RM1 RimMount
* Five PW1 Foam Windscreens
* One CMK-C High Impact Carrying Case
