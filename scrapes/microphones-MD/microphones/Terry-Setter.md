## Terry Setter

Terry Setter is a professor at [Evergreen State College](http://www.evergreen.edu/) in Olympia, Washington, an occasional contributor to *Tape Op,* and an expert in audio circuit design. He created the namesake [TS-1](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Chameleon-Labs/TS-1) and [TS-2](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Chameleon-Labs/TS-2) microphones for Chameleon Labs.

### Mods and Repairs

Always on the lookout for “interesting projects,” Terry does mic mods to a variety of mics, primarily recent Chinese and Russian imports. His approach tends to be personalized to the mic and the specific goals of the mod, targeting whatever voicing or application is requested.

Contact Terry [via email](http://www.evergreen.edu/faculty/instructor/tas).
