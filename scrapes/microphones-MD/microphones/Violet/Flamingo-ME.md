## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The Flamingo ME is Violet’s top-of-the-line tube condenser microphone. The body and electronics are identical to the [Flamingo Standard](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Violet/Flamingo-Standard); the difference between these mics (besides the price) is that the ME incorporates a unique capsule design.

[![Violet Magic Ear capsule with Golden Drops diaphragm](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/violet/magic_ear_sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/violet/magic_ear.jpg)Called the “Magic Ear,” the transducer is not round. Rather, it is shaped somewhat like an ear, or a [liver](http://www.proaudioreview.com/article/2596), depending on your perspective.

The ME diaphragm is Mylar, sputtered with Violet’s gold alloy in the company’s proprietary “Golden Drops” formation.

> VioletUSA
>
> The Flamingo Magic Ear uses an [extra large ear-shaped transducer](http://www.violetusa.com/flamingo.php) which provides a unique, resonance-free, warm and full vocal sound. The irregular shape of the capsule removes circular resonances and reflections. As a result, it has a warm, clean, more dense and natural tone without the typical round capsule resonance effect.

[![Violet Flamingo ME cutaway, by Jonah Westrich](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/violet/flamingo-cutaway-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/violet/flamingo-cutaway.jpg)  
The reviews are excellent.

> AppleProAudio
>
> The combination of this custom tube amplification circuitry and this capsule is breath-taking.

> ProAudioReview
>
> How surprised I was to discover that I actually love the sound of the Magic Ear, as it sounds shockingly good on virtually everything.

The [Violet Design Ltd. Flamingo ME](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Violet/Flamingo-ME) is also known as: Flamingo Magic Ear.

The mic was released in 2007.
