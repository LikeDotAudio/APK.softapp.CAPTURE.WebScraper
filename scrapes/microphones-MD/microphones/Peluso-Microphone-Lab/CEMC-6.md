## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The CEMC-6 is a highly regarded transformerless pencil condenser. The preamp body and circuitry are believed to be manufactured by Feilo in China (see the [CR1-14](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Feilo/CR1-14)), but the detachable capsules are skinned and tuned by Peluso in the US.

Four 20mm capsules (with 17mm, 6-micron diaphragms) are available:

* Cardioid
* Wide Cardioid
* Omnidirectional
* Hypercardioid

These capsules should also fit and function with the CR1-14 and the Advanced Audio CM-54.

[![Peluso CEMC6 Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/peluso/cemc6_kit_sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/peluso/cemc6_kit.jpg)The mic ships with a cardioid capsule (the others are available separately) and a mic clip in a padded wooden box. Shockmounts are also available separately.

The mic bodies and capsules are also available in matched stereo pairs; see the “CEMC6 Stereo Kit” link.

> I ran through all of the capsules, and thought that the cardioid cap is one of the best I’ve heard. Easily smoking any other condenser mic out there under a grand.
