## CardioidSheng Yue BM-700

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The BM-700 is an small-diaphragm condenser microphone with a fixed Cardioid polar pattern.

[![BM-700](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shengyue/BM700-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shengyue/BM700-pcb.jpg)The circuit is implemented with two small surface-mount PCBs.

[![BM-700](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/shengyue/BM700-SCM800-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/shengyue/BM700-SCM800.jpg)The exterior metalwork is identical to the Nady SCM-800.

The mic ships with an elastic shockmount, a foam windscreen, and an unbalanced XLR-to-1/8'' cord.
