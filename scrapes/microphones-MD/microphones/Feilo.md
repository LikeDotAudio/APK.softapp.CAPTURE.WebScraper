## Feilo

Feilo Electric-Audio Equipment Co., Ltd. is one of the affiliated companies of Shanghai Feilo Co., Ltd., which is the oldest company in China engaged in the manufacturing of electric-audio equipments.

The company manufactures a wide variety of microphones and components, some of which turn up in midrange and high-end boutique microphones from other manufacturers.
