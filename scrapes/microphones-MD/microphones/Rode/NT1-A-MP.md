## RØDE NT1-A Matched Pair

## Stereo Mic Set

The NT1-A-MP kit contains a stereo pair of NT1-A large-diaphragm microphones, matched to 0.5dB in sensitivity. The kit includes a pair of 20' cables, elastic shockmounts, and pop filters.

With registration, the 1-year warranty is extended to 10 years.
