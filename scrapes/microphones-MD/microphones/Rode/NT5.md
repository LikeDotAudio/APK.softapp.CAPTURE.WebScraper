## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Rode NT5 is a small diaphragm, transformerless condenser microphone with detachable cardioid capsule.

Intended for recording acoustic instruments, drum overheads, cymbals and live performances, the compact externally polarized mic employs a .5'' capsule, an active J-FET impedance converter with bipolar output buffer.

Finished in durable satin nickel.

> Terry Manning
>
> The NT5’s seemed every bit the equal of the new KM 184 as the room mics…

The NT5 is available singly (NT5-S) or in matched pairs.  
The Rode NT45-O Omnidirectional capsule is sold separately.

The [RØDE NT5](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NT5) is also known as: NT5-S.

The mic was released in 2002.
