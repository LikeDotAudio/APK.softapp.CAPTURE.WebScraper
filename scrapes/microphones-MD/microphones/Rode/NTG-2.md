## ShotgunRØDE NTG-2

## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The NTG-2 adds an onboard battery compartment (1.5V AA) to the basic shotgun-mic design of the NTG-1. The two mics are otherwise identical; both can be phantom-powered (and the NTG-1 requires it).

The capsule is the same as in the [VideoMic](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/VideoMic), although both the NTG-1 and NTG-2 are more directional.

Rode claims that a single AA battery will power the NTG-2 for 500 hours.

The NTG-2 is made of aluminum. It ships with a mount and foam windshield. Additional accessories are available, including the “Dead Cat” fur wind cover.

The [RØDE NTG-2](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NTG-2) is also known as: NTG2.
