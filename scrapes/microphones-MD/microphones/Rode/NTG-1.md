## ShotgunRØDE NTG-1

## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The NTG-1 is a phantom-powered shotgun mic.

It is a low-noise, lightweight microphone intended for mounting directly to video cameras (with optional accessories) or a Boom pole.

The NTG-1 comes with a mount and foam windshield.

Should you need a mic with battery power, see Rode’s NTG-2, which is otherwise the same as the NTG-1.

Both the NTG-1 and NTG-2 share a capsule design with the VideoMic.

The [RØDE NTG-1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NTG-1) is also known as: NTG1.
