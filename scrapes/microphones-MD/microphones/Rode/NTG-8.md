## SupercardioidRØDE NTG-8

## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

NTG-8 is a long shotgun for electronic news gathering and film use.

NTG-8 uses the same RF-Bias technology developed for the NTG-3, making it impervious to humidity and temperature changes.

The NTG-8 comes with SM8 shockmount, WSNTG8 foam windscreen, and RCNTG8 aluminum storage tube.

We will update this profile as more information becomes available.

The [RØDE NTG-8](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NTG-8) is also known as: NTG8.

The mic was released in 2011.
