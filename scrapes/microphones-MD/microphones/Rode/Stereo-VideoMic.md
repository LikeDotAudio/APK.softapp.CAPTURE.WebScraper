## X/Y Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The Stereo VideoMic is an XY-stereo condenser mic designed for use atop a video camera. The body design includes a hotshoe mount, and the onboard FET amplifier can be battery powered.

The SVM, as Rode calls it, shares an amplifier circuit with the mono VideoMic (aka VM). Additional features include an LED to indicate battery status, a high-pass filter switch, and a switchable -10dB pad.

The [RØDE Stereo VideoMic](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/Stereo-VideoMic) is also known as: SVM.
