## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The NT3 is a fixed-cardioid FET condenser mic that is unique in its ability to provide an externally-biased, “true” condenser capsule that can be powered by a 9V battery (or 12, 24 or 48V phantom power).

The 3/4'' capsule (roughly 19mm) is internally shockmounted. The internal preamp is designed for high RF rejection, and incorporates a (transformerless) bipolar-transistor-based output circuit.

The body is metal. The mic is supplied in a zip pouch with a foam windshield, stand mount and thread adaptor.

The mic is compatible with Rode SM3, SM4 and SM5 shockmounts and with the Rode Blimp windscreen.

The [RØDE NT3](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NT3) is also known as: NT-3.
