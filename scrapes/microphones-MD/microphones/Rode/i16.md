## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The i16 is the world’s first 16-channel surround sound microphone for the iPhone. It was announced on April 1, 2013.

It uses 16 of the half-inch electret condenser capsules found on its 2-channel sibling, the iXY, and comes in both Lightning and Apple 30-pin connector versions for compatibility with iPhones 3–5.
