## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The Apex 205 is the best-known retail version of Alctron’s HRM-8B, a large-format ribbon design that has, according to Oktavamod’s Michael Joly, “obvious internal resemblance to the [AEA R84](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/AEA/R84).” The same ribbon motor design can be found in the [Nady RSM-2](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nady/RSM-2) and the [Shinybox 46](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shinybox/46) (the latter being remarkable in that it has all the mods you’d want to do built in from the vendor).

Common mods for these mics include:

* Re-tensioning sagging ribbons
* Removing blast filters from behind grille
* Swapping the stock transformer for a [Cinemag](http://www.cinemag.biz/mic_output/mic_output.html) (e.g. CM-9888) or [Lundahl](http://www.lundahl.se/catframe.html) (e.g. LL2911) or Edcor (e.g. RMX1)

For sound samples of a shootout between the [AEA R92](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/AEA/R92), [Coles 4038](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Coles/4038), and a pair of [OktavaMod](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktavamod)-modded Apex 205s, see [drBill’s ribbon mic shootout on solo piano](http://www.gearslutz.com/board/gear-shoot-outs-sound-file-comparisons-audio-tests/150891-ribbon-madness-some-comparisons.html).

The [Apex Electronics 205](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/205) is also known as: Apex205.
