## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Apex 540 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser with a fixed Cardioid polar pattern.

It is described as a “low self noise” design, yet its self noise figure is not published.

Its sensitivity is cited as 13mV/Pa on the Apex website, or 21mV/Pa in the mic’s datasheet.

The 540 ships with an aluminum case, a foam windscreen and “cradle” shockmount.

This profile will be updated as more info becomes available.

Permalink: [Apex Electronics 540](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Apex-Electronics/540)

The mic was released in 2011.
