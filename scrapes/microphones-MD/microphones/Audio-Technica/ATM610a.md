## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

ATM610a is an updated version of Audio-Technica’s ATM610. Both are hypercardioid stage vocal microphones made with Neodymium magnets.

The ATM610a features an upgraded capsule shockmount and a multi-stage windscreen.

The differences between the ATM610 and ATM610a include:

* The 610a has an improved internal shockmount, which reduces handling noise
* The 610a is 10g lighter
* The 610a has 3dB better rejection at 180° (although the polar patterns are otherwise nearly identical).
* The frequency response differs slightly; the 610a is flat from 100–300Hz, whereas the ATM610 had a small boost in this region.

A variation on ATM610a, the ATM610a/S features a lockable MagnaLock on/off switch design providing silent on/off operation.

The [Audio-Technica ATM610a](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/ATM610a) is also known as: ATM-610A, ATM-610a/S, ATM-610.

The mic was released in 2012.
