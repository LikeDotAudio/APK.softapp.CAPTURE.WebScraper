## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

This attractively priced handheld dynamic mic is tuned for clear vocal and instrument reproduction and consistent performance. Its cardioid polar pattern reduces pickup of sounds from the sides and rear, improving isolation of desired sound source and protecting against feedback.

* Designed especially for both vocal and instrument miking
* Cardioid polar pattern rejects undesirable off-axis sound and background noise
* Permanently-attached 16.5’ (5 m) cable terminated with a 3.5 mm mini-plug for use with home or portable recording systems
* Conveniently-located on/off switch
* Includes 1/4” (6.35 mm) plug adapter and desk stand
