## Audio-Technica MB/Dk4

## 4-Mic Set

The MB/Dk4 is an inexpensive, entry-level drum mic kit consisting of four Audio-Technica “Midnight Blues” dynamic mics:

* One MB 6k for kick drum
* Three MB 5k for snare and toms

Three rim mounts are included. The kit ships in a compact, padded plastic carrying case.
