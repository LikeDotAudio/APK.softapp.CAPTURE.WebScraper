## Audio-Technica MB/Dk6

## 6-Mic Set

The MB/Dk6 is the larger of Audio-Technica’s two “Midnight Blues” drum kit microphone packs. It adds two overhead condensers to the MB/Dk4.

The Dk6 is a six-mic kit that includes:

* One MB 6k for kick drum
* Three MB 5k for snare and toms
* Two MB 4K condenser for overheads

Three rim mounts are included for the MB 5k’s. The MB 6k has an integrated swivel hard mount. The kit includes two AT8405a mic clips for the MB4K condensers.
