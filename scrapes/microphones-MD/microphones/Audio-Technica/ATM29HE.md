## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The ATM29HE was a low-cost dynamic microphone for instrument miking.

It included a hard mount (p/n AT8406a), a foam windscreen, and a soft pouch for storage.

* Hi-ENERGY® neodymium element
* Fast transient response and clean high-frequency response
* Double-isolated element reduces mechanical noise
* Internal shock mounting reduces handling noise
