## OmnidirectionalAudio-Technica ATM10a

## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The ATM10a was a compact electret condenser that could be powered by 48V phantom or AA battery. It was presented as an all-purpose microphone, suitable for numerous vocal and instrumental applications.

This mic was replaced in Audio-Technica’s produce lineup by the AT8010.
