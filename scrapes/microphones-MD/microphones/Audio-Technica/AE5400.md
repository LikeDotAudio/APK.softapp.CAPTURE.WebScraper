## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The AE5400 is a handheld condenser microphone designed for live performance applications. It uses a true condenser capsule and a transformer-coupled output circuit.

Handling noise is reduced through “anti-shock engineering,” which presumably means the capsule is shockmounted within the mic body.

[![AE5400 Cutaway](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AE5400-cutaway-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AE5400-cutaway.jpg)The amplifier circuit is a dual-sided, surface-mount PCB.

Two switches on the microphone body enable a high-pass filter (80Hz) and a -10dB pad.

The mic ships with a clip (p/n AT8470) and a storage pouch.
