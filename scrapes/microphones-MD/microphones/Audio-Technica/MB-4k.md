## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The MB 4k is an inexpensive vocal condenser microphone from Audio-Technica’s Midnight Blues series.

The mic uses an electret condenser element. The low power requirements of this capsule allow the mic to be powered by either 48V phantom, or a 1.5V AA battery (which fits into the mic handle).

When running on battery power, the mic’s specs change:

| Battery | Phantom || Sensitivity | 3.9 mV/Pa | 5 mV/Pa |
| Impedance | 250Ω | 200Ω |
| Max SPL (1% THD) | 125 dB | 137 dB |

The mic’s body construction is all metal, with a “soft touch” finish an an on/off switch.

Two versions of the mic are available; the **MB 4k/c** includes a 15-foot XLR cable.

The [Audio-Technica MB 4k](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/MB-4k) is also known as: MB4k.
