## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The AT4051b is a revision of the company’s 4051a. Both are transformerless “pencil” microphones that accept interchangeable capsules. The 4051 is nominally a Cardioid microphone, but with appropriate capsules would support other patterns.

[![AT4051b Capsule Connection](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051b-capsule-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051b-capsule.jpg)The preamp body, part number AT4900b-48, is common to these microphones:

* AT4900b-48 body + AT4051b-EL cardioid capsule = AT4051b
* AT4900b-48 body + AT4049b-EL omni capsule = AT4049b
* AT4900b-48 body + AT4053b-EL hyper capsule = AT4053b

*What is the difference between the AT4051b and AT4051a?*

* [![AT4051a vs AT4051b Capsule Vents](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051-capsules-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051-capsules.jpg)The vents on the cardioid capsule changed; see comparison image at right.
* The AT4051b has a flatter HF response (due, in part, to the capsule venting change); gone is the +6dB presence peak at 7kHz (on the AT4051a).
* Self-noise has been cut from 19dBA to 16dBA on the AT4051b.
* The AT4051b includes a -10dB pad, raising the max SPL from 146dB to 155dB.
* The AT4051b is RoHS certified (free of hazardous substances).
* The AT4051b has better EMI/RFI shielding.
* Impedance has dropped from 100Ω to 50Ω.
* The ‘b’ revision is 7g heavier.

The ‘b’ series mics — the AT4051b, AT4053b, AT4049b — include two recessed switches on the mic body; one controls the pad, and the other controls the high-pass filter.

[![AT4051b Kit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051b-kit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4051b-kit.jpg)The mic ships with a clip (A-T p/n AT8405a), a foam windscreen, and a carrying case (p/n S1).
