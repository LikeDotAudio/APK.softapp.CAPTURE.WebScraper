## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The PRO 70 is a miniature electret condenser microphone supplied with both a tie clip and a guitar mount.

It can be powered by battery (AA/UM3) or phantom power.

The microphone capsule has a permanently-attached 6-foot (1.8 meter) cable to the power module.

The [Audio-Technica PRO 70](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-70) is also known as: PRO70, PRO 70.
