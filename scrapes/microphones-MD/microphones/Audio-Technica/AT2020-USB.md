## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The AT2020-USB is a USB version of the AT2020 medium-diaphragm electret condenser. The XLR connector of the AT2020 has been replaced with a USB jack, and the body of the USB mic contains an AD converter that outputs 16-bit, 44.1 kHz digital audio.

The mic includes a tripod desk stand, pivoting stand mount, USB cable & storage pouch.

Permalink: [Audio-Technica AT2020-USB](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/AT2020-USB)

The mic was released in 2008.
