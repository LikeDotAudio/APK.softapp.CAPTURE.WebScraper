## Omnidirectional Boundary Microphone

The Pro 44 is an electret condenser boundary mic with a half-cardioid pickup pattern.

The [Audio-Technica PRO 44](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-44) is also known as: PRO44, PRO 44.
