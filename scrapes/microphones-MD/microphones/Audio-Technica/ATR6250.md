## X/Y StereoAudio-Technica ATR6250

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Microphone

The ATR6250 is an inexpensive stereo microphone intended for use on video cameras.

The mic ships with a camera mount, a mic-stand base, foam windscreen, a pair of 3.5mm (1/8'') to 6.3mm (1/4'') mono adapters, and an AA battery. The audio jack on the microphone is a female 1/8'' stereo plug.
