## Omnidirectional Boundary Microphone

A wide range condenser microphone with a cardioid polar pattern, the ultra-smart U891RC is equipped with local or remote switching options. For flexible control in conference and boardroom installations, the unit’s external contact closure ability allows a user to trigger an external deviceï¿½a camera, for instanceï¿½from a switch on the microphone. Another ease-of-use feature: the microphone’s electronic on-off switch can be set to three operating modes: touch-on/touch-off, press to talk, and press to mute. The mic is also equipped an 80 Hz low-cut UniSteep® filter to reduce pickup of low-frequency ambient noise. Its red LED status indicator can be controlled remotely. UniGuard™ innovations offer unsurpassed immunity from radio frequency interference; interchangeable elements are available in cardioid, hypercardioid and omnidirectional polar patterns; a unique rotating PivotPoint™ connector changes the cable exit pointï¿½no tools required. It requires 11 to 52V DC phantom power, which operates the microphone and switch circuitry.

* Designed for surface-mount applications such as high-quality sound reinforcement, conferencing, distance learning and other demanding sound pickup situations
* Ultra-quiet electronic switch can be set to any of three operating modes: “touch-on/touch-off,” “touch-to-talk” and “touch-to-mute”
* External contact closure capability permits control of remote devices from microphone switch
* External LED control allows for accurate depiction of the live status of the microphone
* PivotPoint™ rotating output connector allows cable to exit from either the rear or the bottom of the microphone
* Small-diameter UniPoint capsule near boundary eliminates phase distortion and delivers clear, high-output performance
* UniGuard™ RFI-shielding technology offers outstanding rejection of radio frequency interference (RFI)
* Heavy die-cast case and non-slip silicon foam bottom pads minimize coupling of surface vibration to the microphone
* Low-profile design with low-reflectance black finish for minimum visibility
