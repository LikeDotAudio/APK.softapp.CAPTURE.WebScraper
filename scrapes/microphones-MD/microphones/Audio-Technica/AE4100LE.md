## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The AE4100 is a handheld vocal dynamic designed for stage use.

The AE4100/LE is a limited 50th Anniversary edition featuring silver metallic finish with blue accents, and etched serial number. All other features and specifications are identical to AE4100.

The mic has a multi-layer pop filter built into its headbasket: an outer grille of steel wire, lined with a fine steel mesh, lined with open-cell acoustic foam. Further, the top of the dynamic cartridge is covered with an additional layer of foam.

Permalink: [Audio-Technica AE4100LE](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/AE4100LE)

The mic was released in 2012.
