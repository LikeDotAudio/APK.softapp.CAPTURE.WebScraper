## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The BP4002 is an omnidirectional dynamic microphone, intended primarily for handheld broadcast/ENG use.

The mic’s long shaft was designed to accommodate media-company identification flags.

The mic’s polar pattern is nominally omnidirectional below 1kHz, narrowing progessively at higher frequencies — e.g. at 5kHz, the response is -5dB at 0°/90° and -10dB at 180°

The BP4002 ships with a foam windscreen, vinyl carrying case, soft protective pouch, and a stand clamp.

A lookalike mic with a Cardioid polar pattern is also available; see the BP4001.

Permalink: [Audio-Technica BP4002](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/BP4002)

The mic was released in 2010.
