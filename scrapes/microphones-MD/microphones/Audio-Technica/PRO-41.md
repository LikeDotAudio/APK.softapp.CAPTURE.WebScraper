## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Pro41 is a low-cost ball-style dynamic vocal mic.

The dynamic capsule is shockmounted internally to reduce handling noise. Its use of Neodymium magnets improves the mic’s sensitivity.

The headbasket design helps reduce plosives.

The [Audio-Technica PRO 41](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-41) is also known as: PRO41, PRO 41.
