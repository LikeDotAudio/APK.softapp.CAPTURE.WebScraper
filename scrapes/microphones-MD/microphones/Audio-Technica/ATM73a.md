## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The ATM73 is a headworn electret microphone, designed for performers who need hands-free operation.

The mic’s power supply can be clipped to a belt. It is powered via a single AA/UM3 battery, or Phantom power.

The mic includes its dedicated power supply (A-T p/n AT8531), a foam windscreen (p/n AT8128), and an AA battery.

The ATM73a is available in multiple versions:

* **ATM73cW** - omits the power supply in favor of cabling compatible with Audio-Technica’s UniPak wireless transmitter system.
* **ATM73ac** - omits the power supply (presumably made available as a replacement option for expired headsets?)

The [Audio-Technica ATM73a](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/ATM73a) is also known as: ATM73ac, ATM73cW.
