## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The MB 1k is an entry-level vocal dynamic microphone, from Audio-Technica’s “Midnight Blues” series.

It has a Neodymium magnet in its dynamic cartridge, which gives it high sensitivity of 2.2 mV/Pa.

The mic has an all-metal body with a silent on/off switch. It ships with the AT8470 hard mount with thread adapter.

The mic is available with a 15-foot XLR cable, as the **MB 1k/c**.

The [Audio-Technica MB 1k](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/MB-1k) is also known as: MB1k, MB 1k.
