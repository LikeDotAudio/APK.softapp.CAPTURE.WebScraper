## Multi-Pattern Condenser Stereo Microphone

The AT4050ST is a stereo version of the company’s multipattern FET LDC, the AT4050. It contains two dual-diaphragm capsules; the front-facing capsule is fixed for Cardioid pickup, while the side-facing capsule is fixed for Figure-8. The output of these two capsules is internally matrixed into three possible stereo formats:

* Mid-side
* 90° (XY) stereo
* 127° stereo

[![AT4050ST Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4050ST-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT4050ST-circuit.jpg)We believe the capsules are identical to those in the AT4050: 2-micron, 21.4mm diameter, externally-polarized, large-diaphragm, center-terminated designs covered with a nickel-plated brass baffle. In other mics this functions as a resonator plate, often to enhance the mic’s HF response; we speculate that this baffle has a similar effect here.

Switches on the mic body enable an optional pad (10dB), low-frequency rolloff (-12dB/octave @ 80Hz), and control the stereo output as noted above.

Sensitivity and self-noise figures vary with output pattern:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | **Sensitivity** | **Self-noise** |
| Mid-side | 15.8 mV/Pa | 15dBA mid, 17dBA side |
| 90° Stereo | 7.9 mV/Pa | 25 dBA |
| 127° Stereo | 8.9 mV/Pa | 16 dBA |

The mic’s headbasket is described as an “open acoustical environment” designed to “minimize unwanted internal reflections.”

The mic ships with a low-profile elastic shockmount (Audio-Technica p/n AT8449), a “dust cover,” a 5-meter breakout cable (XLR-5F to 2xXLR-3M), and a padded vinyl case (p/n R1).

The AT4050 was first introduced at the AES-NYC show in the Fall of 2009.
