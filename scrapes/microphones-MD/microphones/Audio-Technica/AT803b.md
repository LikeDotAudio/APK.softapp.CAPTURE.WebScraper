## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The AT803b provides excellent yet unobtrusive sound pickup for lecturers and stage/TV performers. Designed for clip-on lavalier and musical instrument use, it features an integral 80 Hz high-pass filter that provides easy switching from a flat frequency response to a low-end roll-off. The microphone operates on battery or phantom power.

* Designed for clip-on lavalier and musical instrument use
* Small size is ideal for applications requiring minimum visibility
* Operates on battery or phantom power
* 6’ (1.8 m) cable permanently attached between microphone and power module

The [Audio-Technica AT803b](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/AT803b) is also known as: AT803.
