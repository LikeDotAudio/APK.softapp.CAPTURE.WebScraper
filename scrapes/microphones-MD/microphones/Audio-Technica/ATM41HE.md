## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The ATM41HE is a handheld vocal dynamic microphone.

Its Neodymium magnet structure gives it higher output (2.8 mV/pa) than its sibling mic, the ATM41a (1.5 mV/Pa).

The ATM41HE incorporated patented technology to reduce handling noise by “floating” the edges of the diaphragm in a “viscous liquid.”
