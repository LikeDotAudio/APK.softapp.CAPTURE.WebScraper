## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The ATR3M is a quality dynamic talkback microphone engineered for durability, excellent speech intelligibility and reliable performance—at a very attractive price. With its flexible gooseneck design, XLR-type plug-in, protective windscreen and press-on/press-off switch, the microphone is ideal for everyday use in entertainment, commercial and industrial applications.

* Designed for use as a quality talk-back microphone
* Ideal for entertainment, commercial and industrial applications
* Excellent intelligibility and dependable performance
* Protective windscreen and rugged construction
* Conveniently-located on/off switch
* Overall length of 17.52” (445.0 mm)
