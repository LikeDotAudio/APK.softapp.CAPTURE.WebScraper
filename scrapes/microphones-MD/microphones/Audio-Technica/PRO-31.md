## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

This affordable cardioid dynamic microphone is designed for close-up vocal performance. The PRO 31 offers a broad frequency response and low handling noise in a tough, durable design that delivers consistent performance on the road. The mic sets price/performance standards for intelligibility, transparent sound quality and noise suppression. Available in two models for easy use with consumer and pro electronics: PRO 31 (XLRF-XLRM cable) and PRO 31QTR (XLRF- Â¼” cable).

* Close-up vocal performance microphone sets price/performance standards for intelligibility, transparent sound quality and noise suppression
* Cardioid polar pattern reduces pickup of sounds from the sides and rear, improving isolation of desired sound source
* Hi-ENERGY® neodymium magnet for high output and articulate vocal reproduction
* Two-stage ball-type headcase for superior “pop” protection
* MagnaLock™ on/off switch
* Corrosion-resistant contacts from gold-plated XLRM-type connector
* Rugged design and construction for reliable performance

Also available as the “PRO 31QTR,” which is supplied with an XLRF to 1/4'' male cable.

The [Audio-Technica PRO 31](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-31) is also known as: PRO 31, PRO31, 31.
