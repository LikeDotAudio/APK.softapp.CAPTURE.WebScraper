## X/Y StereoAudio-Technica AT822

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The AT822 was a battery-powered XY-stereo microphone intended for field recording and broadcast use.

> Audio-Technica
>
> The microphone is equipped with unbalanced outputs for use with readily available audio and video equipment… The mic offers excellent channel separation and switchable low-frequency roll-off.
>
> * Compact, lightweight design is perfect for camera-mount use
> * Closely-matched elements provide the spatial impact and realism of a live sound field
> * Includes a 1.7' (0.5 m) cable (XLRF-type to stereo 3.5 mm mini-plug); and a 10' (3 m) cable (XLRF-type to two mono 3.5 mm mini-plugs)
> * Battery operation only
>
> Warning: The AT822 is designed for battery operation only. Do not attempt to use when phantom power is present. Possible damage to the microphone may result.
