## Hypercardioid Condenser Microphone

The ATM89R is a handheld electret condenser mic with a hypercardioid pickup pattern. It was designed for stage vocal applications.

Its published signal-to-noise ratio is 67dB, which translates to a self-noise level of 27dBA.
