## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The MB 3k is an inexpensive handheld dynamic vocal mic, part of Audio Technica’s “Midnight Blues” series.

It has a hypercardioid polar pattern that widens at lower frequencies.

Like many of the mics in this series, it has an all-metal body with “soft touch” finish, a silent on/off switch, a gold-plated XLR jack, and a “HI-ENERGY” Neodymium magnet in the dynamic cartridge.

It ships with the AT8470 hard mount, a thread adapter, and a storage pouch. It is also available bundled with a 15-foot XLR cable, as the **MB3k/c**.

The [Audio-Technica MB 3k](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/MB-3k) is also known as: MB3k.
