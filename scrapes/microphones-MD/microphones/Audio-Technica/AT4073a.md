## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The AT4071a is a short (9.3''), lightweight shotgun microphone with a true condenser capsule and a patented interference tube that provides “directivity equal to mics up to 50% longer.”

The mic is also remarkable for its high sensitivity (70.8 mV/Pa) and low self-noise (14dBA). A switch on the mic body enables a high-pass filter (-12dB/octave @ 150 Hz).

According to Patent 4,789,044, most “shotgun” microphones attempt to narrow their pickup pattern by attaching an interference pipe to the face of a cardioid transducer, leaving the rear of the capsule is exposed (“positioned at a free audio sound field”).

> Patent 4,789,044
>
> [This arrangement] has a defect and drawback [in] that it picks up wind noise and raises a proximity effect which distorts [the] low-band area when the sound source is near.

Audio-Technica’s innovation incorporates the following changes:

* The holes along the top of the interference tube are elongated, and covered with a polyvinylidene fluoride membrane; these work together to prevent resonance inside the pipe and to introduce a phase change.
* The transducer is mounted toward the rear of the tube, but offset toward the front to allow space for additional vents in the tube behind the transducer.
* The outside diameter of the microphone capsule is smaller than the inside diameter of the interference tube, allowing the spaces before and behind the capsule to be acoustically mated.
* Most, possibly all of the vents in the interference tube are covered with a “non-woven textile,” presumably to reduce wind noise.

See also the AT4071a, a longer version of the same microphone, with higher sensitivity and lower self-noise.

The [Audio-Technica AT4073a](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/AT4073a) is also known as: AT4073.
