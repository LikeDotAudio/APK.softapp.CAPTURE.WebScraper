## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

* Clips to brass, reeds, piano, acoustic bass, snare and toms
* Crisp, clear, well-balanced response, even at high SPLs
* Interchangeable elements available for hypercardioid and subcardioid polar patterns
* UniMount® clip permits accurate positioning while protecting element
* Operates on battery or phantom power
* Also available as: ATM35cW — less power module; 55” (1.4 m) cable terminated with locking 4-pin connector for A-T UniPak™ wireless systems
