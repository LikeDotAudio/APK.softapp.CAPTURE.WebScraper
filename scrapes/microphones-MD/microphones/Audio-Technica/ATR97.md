## Omnidirectional Boundary Microphone

The ATR97 is an inexpensive boundary mic with a permanently-attached audio cable terminated in a 3.5mm mini-plug.

> Audio-Technica
>
> The ATR97 is a condenser boundary microphone with an omnidirectional polar pattern. It is useful in surface-mounted applications, for meetings, teleconferencing and desktop computer use. The microphone’s low-profile design assures minimum visibility.
>
> * Ideal for meetings, teleconferencing and desktop computer use
> * Omni pickup pattern offers full area coverage
> * On/off switch on bottom
> * Rugged housing features low-profile design for minimum visibility
> * Permanently-attached 4.9' (1.5 m) cable terminated with 3.5 mm mini-plug
