## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The ATR50 is an inexpensive handheld dynamic vocal microphone in the traditional “ball microphone” form factor. It has a fixed cardioid pickup pattern and an on-off switch.

It includes a 15' detachable XLR cable and a mic clip.
