## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PRO 63 is an inexpensive handheld dynamic microphone with a cardioid pickup pattern.

It is part of Audio-Technica’s series of “HI-ENERGY” microphones with Neodymium magnets in the moving-coil cartridge.

The [Audio-Technica PRO 63](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-63) is also known as: PRO63, PRO 63.
