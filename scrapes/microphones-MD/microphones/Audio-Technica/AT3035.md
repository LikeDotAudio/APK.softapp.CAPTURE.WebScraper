## CardioidAudio-Technica AT3035

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The AT3035 is an electret condenser mic, designed as a general-purpose studio condenser. The pickup pattern is cardioid. Onboard switches provide a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter (-12dB/octave @ 80 Hz).

Audio-Technica claims this microphone delivers “exceptional detail and low noise,” “extended low-frequency response,” “rich, full sound reproduction.”

[![AT3035 PCB](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT3035-pcb-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT3035-pcb.jpg)The mic’s self-noise figure is 12dB. It uses a surface-mount circuit board and an output transformer.

The oversized capsule diffusor is unique. It helps prevent sonic reflections within the headbasket.

The capsule is a 1-inch O.D. electret model with a single diaphragm. The diaphragm diameter is 19mm (0.75'').

The mic ships with a shockmount (p/n AT8458) and a soft carrying pouch.  
[![AT3035 Internals](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audio-technica/AT3035-open-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/audio-technica/AT3035-open.jpg)

> Mojo Pie (2002)
>
> The Bottom Line: One of the best lower cost large diaphragm condenser mics made.

> SoundOnSound (2001)
>
> On the whole I’d say the AT3035 has a nice balance between accuracy and flattery with a convincingly natural sound that should suit most vocalists. It also works fine on just about any acoustic instrument — its high SPL handling means it won’t flinch at loud percussion or guitar amplifiers. At street prices, the obvious competition for the AT3035 is the [Rode NT1](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/NT1-A), the AKG C2000 and C3000 and the [Octava MK219](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Oktava/MK-219)/219A models. Each has a slightly different tonal character that will suit some singers better than others. I don’t think it’s possible to say which of these is best, as ‘best’ can mean different things to different people, but given its sound, its build quality and its bundled shockmount, I think the AT3035 will have no trouble attracting admirers.

AT3035 internal photos by DJS~NJ
