## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The ATM63HE is an instrument dynamic, similar in size and application to the Shure SM57.

Unlike the SM57, the ATM63HE uses Neodymium magnets within its moving-coil cartridge, giving it higher output. Its hypercardioid pickup pattern makes it less sensitive to off-axis sounds than the SM57. Further, the mic’s frequency response is smoother and flatter than the SM57.

> Audio-Technica
>
> * Ideal for toms, snare, guitar cabinets, piano and vocals
> * Features fast transients, excellent isolation and peak-free response
> * Hi-ENERGY® neodymium element
> * Natural, balanced pickup both on- and off-axis
> * Extremely rugged design with all-metal grille

The mic includes a clip (p/n AT8406a), windscreen, and a soft pouch.
