## X/Y StereoAudio-Technica PRO 24

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The Pro 24 is an inexpensive stereo condenser microphone intended for field or video-camera use.

> Audio-Technica
>
> The microphone features a pair of cardioid elements in an X-Y configuration to provide the spatial impact and realism of a live sound field. It features battery-free operation when used with recording devices that provide plug-in power at the mic input. Like all Audio-Technica mics, the PRO 24 is rugged; it’s built to endure the rigors of field production.
>
> * Designed for a wide range of stereo applications, including field recording, video production and studio use
> * Affordable, high-quality stereo pickup in a single microphone
> * Compact, lightweight design is perfect for camera-mount use
> * Pair of cardioid elements in X-Y configuration provides the spatial impact and realism of a live sound field
> * Battery-free operation when used with recording devices that provide plug-in power at the mic input
> * Rugged construction; built to endure the rigors of field production
> * On/off switch for convenient operation
> * 6' (1.8 m) cable permanently attached to microphone

The [Audio-Technica PRO 24](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/PRO-24) is also known as: PRO24.
