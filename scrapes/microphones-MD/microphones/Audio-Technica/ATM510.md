## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

ATM510 is a handheld cardioid dynamic designed for live vocal use.

The mic uses a neodymium magnet which allows for a smaller, more powerful capsule with less weight. The mic is lighter than the Shure [SM-58](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/SM58), [Beta 58A](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Shure/Beta-58A) and most other similar handheld dynamics.

The frequency response is reportedly tailored to produce smooth, natural vocals; however, as of this writing,

According to Audio-Technica, internal shockmounting of the capsule greatly reduces handling noise. A multi-stage windscreen controls popping and sibilance.

The mic body and grille are all metal. The XLR connector is gold plated.

The mic ships with a clip and a storage pouch.

The [Audio-Technica ATM510](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audio-Technica/ATM510) is also known as: ATM-510.

The mic was released in 2012.
