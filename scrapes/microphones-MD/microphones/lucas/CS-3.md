## Omnidirectional Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

The Lucas Engineering CS3 is a fixed Omni tube condenser mic with a small-diaphragm capsule.

The microphone has a NOS Mullard tube and AMI transformer. The body finish is Lucazite, developed by Latch Lake.

The Omni capsule will *not* be removable or interchangeable, as this would require too many compromises to the mic’s performance. Lucas plans to produce a Cardioid mic under the name CS-2, after the CS-3 is in production.

We will update this profile as more information is made available.

Permalink: [Lucas Engineering CS-3](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/lucas/CS-3)

The mic was released in 2013.
