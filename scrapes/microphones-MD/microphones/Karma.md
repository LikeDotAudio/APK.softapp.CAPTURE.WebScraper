## Karma Audio

Karma Audio is a US company that owns a microphone factory in Shanghai. The company focuses on creating new, innovative, and affordable microphones rather than on cloning famous vintage designs.

The product line runs from $10 mini-condensers to multipattern tube condensers and ribbon mics that are gathering rave reviews.
