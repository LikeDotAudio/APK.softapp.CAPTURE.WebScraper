## Supercardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The e614 is short pencil condenser with an electret capsule and a Supercardioid polar pattern.

The mic’s published frequency response is nominally flat from 200Hz–8kHz; below 200Hz, the response rolls off slightly.

The polar pattern is a very consistent Supercardioid, through 16kHz, where the pattern widens (-2dB @ 90°). Rejection at 180° is about 15dB; the mic’s null points are around ±135°.

The mic ships with a pouch and clip (p/n MZQ 100).

The [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation e 614](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/e-614) is also known as: e614, evolution 614.
