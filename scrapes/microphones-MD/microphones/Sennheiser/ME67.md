## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

Sennheiser’s ME 67 is a long shotgun capsule head for the K6 series of head amplifiers. Its sibling, the ME 66, is a shorter shotgun style.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Model | Length |
| **ME 66** | 221mm |
| **ME 67** | 343mm |

The “K6” product series includes two power modules, one of which is required for use with any of the compatible ME 60-series capsule heads.

The ME 67 capsule head has very high sensitivity at 50 mV/Pa, and remarkably low self-noise for a small-diaphragm design — just 10dBA.

Its frequency response is flat from 200Hz–3kHz. Above that point, it has a broad, +4dB presence boost centered at 9kHz. Its -3dB points are 40Hz and ~16kHz.

This is generally a fuller, flatter response than the shorter shotgun model (ME 66), which rolls off below 500Hz (-3dB @ 150Hz).

Both models are generally supercardioid below 1kHz, and progressively more directional (lobar) at higher frequencies.

The [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation ME 67](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/ME67) is also known as: ME 67.
