## Sennheiser Electronics Corporation Drum Kit 600

## 7-Mic Set

The DRUMKIT600 is Sennheiser’s entry-level kit for drum set miking. It includes seven microphones from the e600 evolution line:

* One e602-II for kick drum (with integrated hard mount)
* Four e 604 for snare and toms (with integrated hard mount and rim clamp)
* Two e 614 condensers for overheads (with hard mounts)

The set ships in a slimline, padded aluminum flightcase.
