## ShotgunSennheiser Electronics Corporation MKH 70

## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The MKH 70 is a lightweight long shotgun microphone. The mic uses Sennheiser’s [RF Condenser](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/tag/rf-condenser) technology, which gives it very high sensitivity with low self-noise, as well as resistance to environmental conditions such as humidity.

The mic has 3 switches on the body: a -10dB pad, a low-frequency roll-off, and high-frequency emphasis. The latter is a high shelf compensation for treble loss with distant miking.

The MKH 70 is the only long-shotgun in the X0 line, and is similar to the newer [MKH 8070](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/MKH-8070). The 8070 has an extended frequency response, and is modular.

The [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation MKH 70](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/MKH-70) is also known as: MKH70.
