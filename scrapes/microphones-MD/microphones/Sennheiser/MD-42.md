## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The MD 42 is a handheld dynamic reporter’s microphone with an extra-length body and an omnidirectional piickup pattern.

The headbasket uses two-layer mesh to help reduce plosives and wind noise.

The MD 42 is very similar to the MD 46, but with an Omni rather than Cardioid polar pattern.

Permalink: [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation MD-42](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/MD-42)

The mic was released in 2008.
