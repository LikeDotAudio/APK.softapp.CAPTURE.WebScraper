## CardioidSennheiser Electronics Corporation e 904

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Sennheiser e904 is a compact dynamic instrument mic designed especially for drums.

The mic has a cardioid polar pattern. The frequency response graph shows a roll-off around 150Hz, to compensate for proximity effect, and a gentle 3dB rise extending from 500Hz. However, because microphone frequency charts are typically measured at a distance of 1M, this information does not reveal much about the sound of the mic when mounted on a drum rim, within a few centimeters from the drum head.

[![e 904 with MZH 604 clamp](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/sennheiser/e904-clamp-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/sennheiser/e904-clamp.jpg)The e904 ships with the MZH 604 drum clamp.

Sennheiser received a TEC Award for their e-series drum mics in 2005.

The [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation e 904](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/e904) is also known as: evolution 904.
