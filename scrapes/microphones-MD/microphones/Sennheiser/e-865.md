## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

The Sennheiser e 865 is a handheld supercardioid condenser mic for stage use. This is the only condenser vocal mic in the evolution line.

The e865 was nominated for a TEC award in 2001.

The mic uses uses an electret condenser capsule with a response of 40 - 20kHz. The mic is rated at 150 dB SPL.

[![e 865, Sting Signature Edition](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/sennheiser/e865-sting-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/sennheiser/e865-sting.jpg)Three models of the mic are available:

* **e865** - microphone, MZQ 800 clamp, storage pouch
* **e865 S** - as above, with a lockable, noiseless on/off switch.
* **Sting Signature Edition** - e865 with Sting’s signature, presumably packaged as the original e865 with clip and pouch

The [Sennheiser Electronics Corporation e 865](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sennheiser/e-865) is also known as: e865s, evolution 865, e865.
