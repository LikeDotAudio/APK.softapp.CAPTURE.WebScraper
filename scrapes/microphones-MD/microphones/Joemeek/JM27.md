## CardioidJoemeek JM27

## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The JM27 is a fixed-cardioid, transformer-coupled, true condenser FET microphone.

It was originally designed by Ted Fletcher and built under his supervision by [Feilo](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Feilo), and [released](http://news.harmony-central.com/Newp/2001/JM27.html) in 2001 as the “JOEMEEK MicroMic JM27.” The mic was subsequently re-released at the Summer 2004 NAMM show as a “Meekrophone,” after PMI (Joemeek’s US distributor) acquired the rights to the product line. The specs for the JM27 appear to be identical before and after the aquisition.

![Original JM27, circa 2001](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/joemeek/jm27_2001.jpg)The green version of the mic was a PMI innovation — the originals were silver.

On the circuit design, Brent Casey of PMI Audio [said](http://homerecording.com/bbs/showpost.php?p=1115415&postcount=8), “The JM27 uses the 2SK170 configured as a common source amplifier which is capacitively coupled to a step down transformer on the output. The capsule is phantom polarized at around +46v.”

No pads or filters are available on the mic.

[According](http://homerecording.com/bbs/showpost.php?p=1184702&postcount=13)  to Alan Hyatt of PMI, the JM27 is manufactured by Feilo, but is not a copy of lookalike SDC mics… despite having earlier [stated](http://homerecording.com/bbs/showpost.php?p=769236&postcount=8) that the JM27 is substantially the same as other Feilo-made SDCs such as the [ADK SC-1](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/ADK/SC-1).

The body is made of brass and is available in “Joemeek” green or satin silver finishes. The mic carries a 1 year warranty.

The mic is also available as a stereo pair, shipped in a case with a stereo mounting bar and metal shockmounts; look for p/n JM27PAC.

The [Joemeek JM27](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Joemeek/JM27) is also known as: JM-27, MicroMic.

The mic was released in 2001.
