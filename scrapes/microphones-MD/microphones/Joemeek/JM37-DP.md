## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The JM37-DP is a dual pattern, side-address, large diaphragm, FET condenser. It is the multipattern version of the JM37, and shares a machined-brass body.

The capsule is a dual-diaphragm, center-terminated model described as “1.035''” (~26mm); presumably the outside diameter is 32mm. (We await confirmation from PMI.)

Two switches on the front of the microphone body allow for pattern selection (Cardioid or Omni) and pad (0dB, -16dB, -20dB).

Like the fixed-Cardioid JM37, the JM37DP has a transformerless output circuit, and relatively high self-noise of 19dBA.

The [Joemeek JM37-DP](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Joemeek/JM37-DP) is also known as: JM37DP.

The mic was released in 2005.
