## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The R92 is a “large geometry” passive ribbon microphone, with a thin (1.8 micron) aluminum ribbon nearly 60mm in length. It was designed to avoid the proximity effect of many passive ribbon designs, and therefore can be used relatively close to a sound source without excessive low-frequency buildup.

As do most ribbon-mic designs, the R92 has a bidirectional pickup pattern. The front and rear responses are not identical, but rather produce two different tones, termed “crisp” (front) and “smooth” (rear) by the manufacturer.

The R92 is designed with an integral elastic shock mount, featuring a unique mounting system that allows excellent flexibility of mic positioning. The mic ships with a two meter cable and a durable plastic carrying case.

The AEA R92 was part of our [$60,000 Ribbon mic shootout](http://recordinghacks.com/2011/07/11/60000-ribbon-mic-shootout/).

Before now a frequency response that extends up to 19 kHz was unheard of in a ribbon mic but the R92 has changed that. Its top end is wonderfully pristine. You truly have to hear it to believe it.

With the R92, the extended top and reduced bottom brings something completely new to the mix. And although it sounds decidedly dark when compared to a condenser, it more than makes up for it with personality.

This tendency and its tailored frequency response make the R92 a hands-down winner at close quarters… The R92 — like its forefathers carrying the same engine, the [R84](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/AEA/R84) and R98 — is unequaled in its price range.
