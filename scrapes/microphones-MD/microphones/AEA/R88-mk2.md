## Multi-Pattern Ribbon Stereo Microphone

The R88 mk2 is a second-generation Blumlein stereo ribbon microphone, a 2010 update to the AEA R88. Both mics are passive dual-ribbon microphones with stereo outputs.

The two ribbon elements measure 59.7mm x 4.7mm. Both are corrugated 1.8µ aluminum, tuned to 16.5Hz. They are mounted end-to-end, at 90° to one another.

Each ribbon has its own step-up transformer (1:28) and individual outputs via the XLR5M jack.

As in the original R88, blast protection is minimal: “only black technical-fabric and stainless steel separate the ribbons from the music,” according to AEA.

The R88 mk2’s frequency response is identical to the original R88.

*How does the R88 mk2 differ from the original R88?*

> AEA
>
> We redesigned the shockmount, the cable connections, and the mounting system…

Whereas the R88 had a “captive” (permanently-attached) cable, the mk2 has an onboard XLR5M jack.

The specs and frequency response of the R88 and mk2 are identical.

The mic is finished in “stealth” black. It ships in a padded, hard storage case and includes a 4m breakout cable (XLR5F to 2x XLR3M).
