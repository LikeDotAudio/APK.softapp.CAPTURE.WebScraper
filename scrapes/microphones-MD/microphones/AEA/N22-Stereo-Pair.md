## AEA Nuvo N22 Stereo Pair

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

AEA’s two Nuvo microphones were made available as stereo pairs in the summer of 2015. The bundle includes an SKB case with custom diecut foam, two suspension mounts, Windtech windscreens, a Blumlein adapter, and AEA’s stereo bar.

As an introductory special, the stereo kit with all accessories is included as a free upgrade for anyone buying two of the N8 or N22 microphones, through October 31, 2015.
