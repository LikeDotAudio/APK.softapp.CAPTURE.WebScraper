## Soyuz Microphones SU-011

## Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

The SU-011 is a tube microphone with detachable capsules, built into a compact small-diaphragm form factor. Yet its ability to host large diaphragm capsules from its sibling SU-017 makes it a “[hybrid microphone](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/tag/hybrid-mic)” design, like the Oktava MK-012 and 3-Zigma CHI series.

The tube is a subminiature glass pentode, called the 6S6B-V when transliterated from the Cyrillic designation.

We do not have specifics about the SU-011 circuit, except to say that it differs from the SU-017. The SU-011’s output transformer is located in the power supply.

The microphone was first shown publicly at the AES show in Los Angeles in 2014; Soyuz provided the following pricing at that time:

* $1200 for a single microphone (including power supply)
* $2000 for a matched pair of microphones
