## MXL

MXL is the brand name for pro audio gear, including microphones, made by Marshall Electronics.

The company headquarters is located in southern California, employing approximately 200 staffmembers at that location, including Test, Engineering, and R&D. A small satellite office in Portland, OR employs about six people.

Most of their microphones are manufactured in China, in a factory owned and outfitted by MXL.
