## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The ST6050 “Allen Sides” is the first microphone in Sterling Audio’s Signature Series. It is a premium FET condenser voiced by Allen Sides of Ocean Way Studios, designed primarily for studio vocal applications.

> Allen Sides
>
> It’s a spectacular vocal microphone. In some respects I’d compare it to the [![Neumann U47 fet](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mics/00582/00582_150.jpg)U47 fet](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/U47fet "Neumann U47 fet")… they were trying to come out with something comparable to a [U 47](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/U-47). Well, it didn’t sound like a ‘47, but it sounded very, very good. And became a classic of its own. So I wanted something along those lines, but I wanted to step it up a little bit…
>
> I don’t think there’s anything commercially available, at any price, that’s better than what this microphone is.

The microphone’s capsule is a center-terminated design, specified as “1'' diameter” with 8-micron Mylar diaphragms. The backplate design has not been specified.

The mic has an output transformer.

High consistency in the sound of this microphone throughout a production run has been ensured by enforcing tight tolerances for component selection and manufacturing processes.

[![ST6050](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/sterling/ST6050-rear-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/sterling/ST6050-rear.jpg)The rear of the microphone body shows Allen Sides’ signature.

The ST6050 includes a foam windscreen, a velvet-lined oak storage box, and an elastic shockmount (p/n SM7-N).

The [Sterling Audio ST6050](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sterling/ST6050) is also known as: Allen Sides.

The mic was released in 2011.
