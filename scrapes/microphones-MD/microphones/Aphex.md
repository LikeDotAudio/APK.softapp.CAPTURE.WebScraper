## Aphex

Aphex is a manufacturer of outboard gear, interfaces, and pro audio accessories. The company is best known for its patented “Aural Exciter” and “Big Bottom” circuits, which are available in rackmount outboard gear, 500 series devices, the new USB Microphone X, and as Pro Tools plug-ins.
