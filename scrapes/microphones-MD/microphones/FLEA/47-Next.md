## CardioidFLEA 47 NEXT

## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The Flea 47 NEXT is a Cardioid-only version of the company’s “Flea 47” replica of the Neumann U47. It is presented as a lower-cost alternative to the Flea 47, for customers who expect to use the 47’s Cardioid pattern exclusively.

Because the mic does not support the Omni polar pattern, no switching hardware or circuitry is needed.

The mic uses Flea’s F7 capsule (an M7 replica) and an EF12 tube. The transformer is Flea’s replica of the Neumann BV8.

The mic ships with a shockmount, power supply, cable, and a wooden storage case for the microphone.
