## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

Based on the design of the Mouse microphone, the OmniMouse is a small diaphragm condenser microphone featuring a rotating omnidirectional capsule enclosed within a spherical grille.

The capsule is the Blue B4, available separately in a bayonet-mount lollipop head for Blue’s Bottle and Bottle Rockets mics. The B4 uses a center-terminated, 18mm Mylar diaphragm sputtered with gold and covered with an aluminum grille, on a 24mm capsule. The voicing of the B4 is said to be modeled after the [Neumann M50](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/M-50).

Although the lollipop B4 head can be fitted to at least three other Blue mics, none of them provide a transformer-coupled FET amplifier, making the OmniMouse a unique combination within Blue’s product line.

The Omni Mouse was discontinued in 2009.
