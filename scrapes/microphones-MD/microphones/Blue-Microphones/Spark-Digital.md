## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Blue Spark Digital is a USB/iPad condenser mic based on the [Spark](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Blue-Microphones/Spark) FET condenser.

It uses an edge-terminated true condenser capsule with a 23mm diaphragm (25mm capsule). The mic features the “Focus” switch introduced on the original Spark; it incorporates a high-pass filter and subtle frequency-response changes by modifying the capsule’s polarization voltage. (See the Spark profile for more information.)

[![Spark Digital](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/blue/spark-digital-stand-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/blue/spark-digital-stand.jpg)The Spark Digital connects to PC or Mac via USB2, or to iPad via Apple 30-pin cable. According to Blue, it is the first microphone to offer both USB and iPad connectivity.

A subsequent version of the mic, the “Spark Digital Lightning,” includes an Apple Lightning cable, for direct connectivity to modern iPhones and iPads.

An onboard headphone jack provides zero-latency monitoring of the recorded signal. Headphone volume, mic gain, and mute are all controllable from the microphone.

The ADC (Analog-Digital converter) runs at 16 bits, with a maximum resolution of 44.1 kHz.

The [Blue Microphones Spark Digital](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Blue-Microphones/Spark-Digital) is also known as: Spark Digital Lightn.

The mic was released in 2012.
