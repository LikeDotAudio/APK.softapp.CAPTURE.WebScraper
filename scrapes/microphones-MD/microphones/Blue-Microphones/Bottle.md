## Detachable-Capsule Tube Condenser Microphone

The Bottle is Blue’s flagship tube condenser, combining low noise and excellent transient response with a system of eight optional interchangeable capsules (called the “Bottle Caps”).

These bayonet-mount capsules can be exchanged to provide different tonal characteristics and pickup patterns while the power supply remains on. The Class A discrete amplifier circuit of The Bottle is a thoroughly modern homage to the past, with a design utilizing a single hand-selected EF86 pentode vacuum tube in triode mode.

To avoid the use of extraneous circuits which may degrade signal integrity, no pads or filters are incorporated into the Bottle’s schematic.

* Hand-built, hand-tuned, hand-tested
* Class A discrete amplifier circuit with hand-selected EF86 pentode vacuum tube in triode mode
* Capsules can be swapped “on the fly” for extreme versatility
* Integrated capsule shockmounting
* Custom wound output transformer
* Includes B6 capsule, our renowned 9610 Tube Power Supply, Champagne tube mic cable, and ATA flight case

> Resolution Magazine

> By any measure, the Bottle is a stunning microphone — aesthetically, sonically and technically.
