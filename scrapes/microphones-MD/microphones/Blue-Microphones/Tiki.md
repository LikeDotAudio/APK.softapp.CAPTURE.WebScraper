## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Blue Tiki is a compact noise-cancelling USB microphone. Despite its diminutive size, it contains two condenser mic capsules.

> BLUE Microphones
>
> The world’s first USB microphone that mimics the intelligence of human hearing by focusing on desired sounds, minimizing background noise and knowing when to mute altogether.

The two capsules enable switching between two operational modes:

* **Intelligent Speech Mode** - minimizes background noise via DSP, focusing on the nearest/loudest voice.
* **Natural Recording Mode** - for vocal or instrument recording in environments where background noise is not present.

The Tiki connects to PC or Mac via USB2; no drivers or software are required. The onboard ADC operates at 16 bit, 44.1kHz.

The Tiki was announced at CES Winter 2012.
