## Blue Microphones Poseidon

## Condenser Microphone

Blue’s Poseidon is the world’s first underwater microphone.

According to Blue, it will “take your recording to new lows!”

The Poseidon was announced on April 1, 2013, with availability limited to Blue dealers located in Atlantis.
