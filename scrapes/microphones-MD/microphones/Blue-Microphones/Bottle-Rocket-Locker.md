## Blue Microphones Bottle Rocket Mic Locker

## 1-Mic Set

The Bottle Rocket Mic Locker is a bundled set of four of Blue’s interchangeable capsules with the Stage One FET condenser microphone body.

The four included capsules are:

* B0 Bottle Cap - silky, extended presence
* B6 Bottle Cap - classic warmth, larger than life sound
* B7 Bottle Cap - vintage detailed mids with airy presence
* B8 Bottle Cap - crystal-clear sound with incredible detail

The four capsules, shockmount, and head amp are packed into an SKB hardshell case with custom die-cut foam.
