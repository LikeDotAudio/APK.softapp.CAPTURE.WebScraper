## Telefunken Elektroakustik AR-51 Stereo Set

## Stereo Mic Set

The AR-51 Stereo Set includes two of Telefunken Elektroakustik’s AR-51 microphones, matched on the component level — capsule, tube, and circuit — for both gain and frequency response.

The mics are packed with a USA-made, fully regulated two-channel power supply that ramps up the heater voltage slowly to prevent damage to the tube in each microphone.

The kit also includes two shockmounts, two wooden storage cases for the mics, and all the necessary cables.
