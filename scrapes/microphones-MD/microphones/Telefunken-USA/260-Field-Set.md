## Telefunken Elektroakustik 260 Field Set

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The M 260 Field Set is a stereo pair of multipattern Ela M 260 tube condensers, designed for portability and location recording. It differs from the M 260 Stereo Set primarily in its use of a compact power supply and the inclusion of a lithium-ion battery pack that enables up to 20 hours of recording “off the grid.”

The Field Set includes: a matched pair of Ela M 260 microphones, a stereo mounting bar, two padded wooden storage boxes (p/n WB60), three pairs of matched capsules (TK60 Cardioid, TK61 Omni, TK62 Hypercardioid), two Accusound TX-7 Dual Shielded cables with right-angle female XLR jacks, two M 760 elastic shockmounts, two foam windscreens, a compact 2-channel power supply, and a Novuscell E Series rechargeable Lithium-Ion battery pack with charger and carrying case.

The kit has a 4 year warranty with product registration, aside from the tubes, which are covered for 90 days.
