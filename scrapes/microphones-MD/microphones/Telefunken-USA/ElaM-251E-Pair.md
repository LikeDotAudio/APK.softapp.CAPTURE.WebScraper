## Telefunken Elektroakustik ElaM 251E Stereo

## Stereo Mic Set

Telefunken Elektroakustik’s new ElaM 251E Stereo Set contains two of the company’s ElaM 251E replica tube microphones, handbuilt with 6072a vacuum tubes, and matched for consistent frequency response and sensitivity.

Because the ElaM 251E can be switched among Cardioid, Omnidirectional, and Figure-8, a pair of them is capable of recording in numerous stereo configurations: XY, AB, MS, ORTF, and Blumlein.

The stereo set includes twin wooden storage cases for the microphones, swivel mounts, cables, a two-channel power supply, and a locking leather suitcase.
