## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Telefunken Elektroakustik Ela M 250 E is a two-pattern tube condenser microphone, intended as a historically accurate reproduction of the AKG-built vintage mic of the same name.

The 250E is one of many similar microphones from the ElaM 250/251 family. It differs from the more-expensive 251E in exactly one respect: the 250E provides Cardioid and Omni patterns, whereas the 251E also provides Figure-of-8. (See the reference table below to distinguish the other mics from this series.) Pattern switching is effected by a control built into the microphone body, below the headbasket.

[![Telefunken USA Ela M 251E Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/telefunkenusa/elam_251e_circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/telefunkenusa/elam_251e_circuit.jpg)The 250E and 251E use Telefunken Elektroakustik’s reproduction of the AKG CK-12 capsule, a dual-backplate, dual-diaphragm, edge-terminated design with 25mm Mylar diaphragms. (See the [ElaM 251E](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Telefunken-USA/ElaM-251E) profile for further discussion.) Note that the photo at right shows the 251E’s 3-way pattern switch; the circuit and capsule match the 250E.

The 250E uses the same Haufe 14:1 transformer, and the same JAN-spec GE 6072a tube as the 251E.

Telefunken Elektroakustik manufactures numerous variations of the ElaM 250/251 microphone, including European and “Export” versions:
