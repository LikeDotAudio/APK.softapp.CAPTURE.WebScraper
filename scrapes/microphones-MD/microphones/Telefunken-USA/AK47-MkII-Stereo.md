## Telefunken Elektroakustik AK47 Mk II Stereo

## Stereo Mic Set

The AK47 Mk II Stereo Set incorporates two of Telefunken Elektroakustik’s AK-47 Mk II tube microphones, with matching wooden cases, shockmounts, cables, and a two-channel power supply.

The Mk II version of the AK-47 uses a Telefunken EF732 subminiature tube, and a recreation of the BV8 transformer from early Neumann designs.

The stereo kit is packed into a vintage tweed suitcase.
