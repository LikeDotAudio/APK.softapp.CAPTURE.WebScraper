## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

Telefunken USA’s Ela M 260 is a small-diaphragm tube condenser that ships with three detachable capsules (omni, cardioid, hypercardioid) and is compatible with dozens more capsules made by other vendors (see list below).

![Telefunken USA Ela M 260 Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/telefunkenusa/elam_260_circuit.jpg)This mic is *not* a historical reproduction of the vintage Telefunken of the same name. The original Ela M 260 was manufactured by AKG for Telefunken, and used a Telefunken AC701 tube and AKG’s [CK28](http://www.saturn-sound.com/Curio's/akg's_ck26_&_ck28_capsules.htm) capsule.

The amplifier circuit was designed by AMI, which also supplies the T60 output transformer. The tube is a NOS Telefunken EF-732.

The mic ships in a padded wooden box, and includes a dedicated power supply and Gotham Audio cable, a mic clip, a shock mount, Telefunken USA’s three stock capsules, the adapter for AKG/BLUE/JZ capsules, an owner’s manual, and a fully transferable 10-year warranty.

The Telefunken USA Ela M 260 won the 2008 TEC award for Outstanding Technical Achievement in the Microphone category.

Permalink: [Telefunken Elektroakustik ElaM-260](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Telefunken-USA/ElaM-260)

The mic was released in 2007.
