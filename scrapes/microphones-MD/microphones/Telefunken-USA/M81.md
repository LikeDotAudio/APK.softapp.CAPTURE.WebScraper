## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Theh Telefunken Electroacoustic ELA M 81 is a dynamic cardioid mic similar to the M 80, but intended for general studio use rather than for vocals.

The M81 has a flint-grey body and “tri-chrome” grille and is easily distinguishable from the black bodied M80.

The main difference between the M80 and M81 is the mics’ frequency response.

> Telefunken Electroacoustic Press Release, 2012
>
> Building off many of the strengths of the M80, the TELEFUNKEN M81 retains the same minimal proximity effect, superior feedback rejection, and articulate mid-range. However, with the M81, the top end is pulled back a bit, yielding a flatter overall frequency response. This combination results in a microphone with a wealth of body and clarity, making it a great tool for taming brighter vocals and guitar tones and for fattening up percussion, horns and thinner sounding sources.

The M81 ships with a wooden box, additional black head grille, and a mic clip.

The mic was introduced at Winter NAMM, January 2012.

We will update this profile as additional details become available.

The [Telefunken Elektroakustik Ela M 81](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Telefunken-USA/M81) is also known as: M81, ELAM81.

The mic was released in 2012.
