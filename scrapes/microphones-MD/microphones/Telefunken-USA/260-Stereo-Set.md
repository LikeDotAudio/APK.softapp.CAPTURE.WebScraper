## Telefunken Elektroakustik 260 Stereo Set

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The M 260 Stereo Set contains a matched pair of the Ela M 260 tube condenser microphones with a single two-channel power supply. The kit includes six capsules total: two TK60 Cardioid, two TK61 Omni, and two CK62 Hypercardioid.

The microphones in this set are finished in a “flint gray” to distinguish them from the vintage cream-colored M 260.

The kit ships with two mics in individual padded wooden storage cases (p/n WB60), a stereo mounting bar, a single vintage-style dual-channel power supply, two Accusound TX-7 Dual Shielded cables (with right-angle female XLR jacks to plug into the microphones), two M 760 elastic shockmounts, two foam windscreens, and three pairs of capsules, all packed into a vintage-style, leather-bound, locking suitcase.
