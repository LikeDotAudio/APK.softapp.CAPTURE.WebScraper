## Warm Audio

Warm Audio is a manufacturer based in Liberty Hill, Texas USA. Founded in 2011, they have specialized in making affordable versions of classic studio outboard gear such as the WA-76 compressor, EQP-WA Equalizer, and several preamp designs, among others. They have been quite successful with this: During Winter NAMM, The WA-2A Opto Compressor was awarded the 2017 TEC AWARD for Technical Achievement in the category of Signal Processing Hardware.

They began manufacturing microphones in 2016, with the WA-87 being their first model.
