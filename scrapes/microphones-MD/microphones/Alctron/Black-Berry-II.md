## CardioidNingbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. Black Berry II

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Black Berry and Black Berry (II) are Alctron’s two visual clones of the [![Violet Design Ltd. Amethyst Standard](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mics/00729/00729_150.jpg)Violet Design Amethyst Standard](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Violet/Amethyst-Standard "Violet Design Ltd. Amethyst Standard"). The II trades the Amethyst’s dual-post headbasket suspension for a traditional bottle-style head.

The capsule is a 34mm OD, large-diaphragm, center-terminated design with a 6-micron diaphragm. The rear diaphragm is probably not sputtered, as this is a Cardioid-only microphone.

The impedance conversion circuit is described as Class A and transformerless. It has very low self-noise of 14 dBA, and a high max SPL of 145dB (0.5% THD).

The mic includes a U-shaped bracket that wraps around the mic from behind; it attaches via elastic bands to the grommets fixed to the side of the microphone body. This appears to provide effective shockmounting of the microphone in a relatively low-profile package.

According to Alctron, the grille is a multilayer brass mesh, and the circuitry is built “using only the best quality selected components.” Oddly, the Alctron catalog also refers to this microphone as the “Black Strawberry.”
