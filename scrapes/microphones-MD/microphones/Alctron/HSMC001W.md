## CardioidNingbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HSMC001

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

An inexpensive side-address condenser, this mic has a cardioid pickup pattern and an internally-switched high pass filter.

Like all Alctron products, it is not typically available direct from the manufacturer, but rather through a number of European and North American “rebadgers.” For example, the HSMC001W is visually identical to, and believed to be the source of the Apex Electronics model 435 condenser.

The [Ningbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HSMC001](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HSMC001W) is also known as: HSMC001WA, HSMC001, MC001.
