## OmnidirectionalCardioidNingbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HST-01A

## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The HST-01A is a small-diaphragm “pencil” condenser microphone, sold with detachable cardioid and omnidirectional capsules.

Like all Alctron products, it is not typically available direct from the manufacturer, but rather through a number of European and North American rebranders. For example, the HST-01A is visually identical to Apex Electronics’ model 180 condenser.

Alctron’s HST-02A is an HST-01A with the addition of a -10dB pad and a HPF; switches for both appear on the 02A’s body.

The [Ningbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HST-01A](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HST-01A) is also known as: HST01A, T01A.
