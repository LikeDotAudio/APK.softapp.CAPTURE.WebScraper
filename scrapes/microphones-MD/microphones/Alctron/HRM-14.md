## Bidirectional Ribbon Microphone

The HRM-14 is a passive dual-ribbon microphone, most likely manufactured by ShuaiYin (see their SYR50).

The HRM-14 is available with a black grille (HRM-14B) or white grille (HRM-14W), and ships with a padded wooden storage box and shockmount.

The [Ningbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HRM-14](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HRM-14) is also known as: HRM14, HRM-14W, HRM-14B, RM-14.
