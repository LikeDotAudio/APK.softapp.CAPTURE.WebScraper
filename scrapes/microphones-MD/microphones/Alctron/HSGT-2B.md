## 9 polar patternsNingbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HSGT-2B

## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The HSGT-2B is a large-diaphragm, multipattern tube mic, made in China by Alctron and sold under a variety of familiar brand names.

The specs claim the tube is a selected 6072/12AT7, but is more commonly a 12AX7 which has different characteristics and is arguably a poor fit for the mic’s schematic — leading to the common and easy mod of replacing the tube (assuming it’s socketed) with a true 6072/12AY7.

The mic ships with a power supply, 7-pin cable, spider shockmount, foam windscreen, and an aluminum road case.

Note that Alctron’s published frequency-response graphs for this mic are identical to the HST-11A, and in fact to most of their large-diaphragm tube condensers.

The [Ningbo Alctron Electronics Co., Ltd. HSGT-2B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Alctron/HSGT-2B) is also known as: HST-2B, GT-2B, HSGT2B.
