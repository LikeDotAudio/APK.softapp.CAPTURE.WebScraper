## Audix Studio Elite 8

## 8-Mic Set

The Studio Elite 8 is Audix’ premier microphone kit for drum recording. It contains the company’s best choices in every category:

* One D6 for kick drum
* One i5 dynamic for snare, with DVICE
* Two D2 dynamics for high toms, with DVICEs
* One D4 dynamic for floor tom, with DVICE
* Two SCX25A large-diaphragm condensers for overheads, with SMT25 shockmounts
* One SCX1 with a hypercardioid capsule, for hi-hat

The SCX-1 is a pencil mic with detachable capsules. This kit includes the hypercardioid capsule, intended to reduce bleed from the snare and small tom when positioned near the hi-hat. Users can elect to purchase the omni or cardioid capsules separately, to expand the possible applications for this mic around the studio.

![Audix DVice gooseneck rim clamp](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audix/dvice.jpg)The DVICE is an adjustable rim mount, with a gooseneck arm to facilitate mic positioning. The DVICE includes the DCLIP, a rigid plastic clip engineered to tightly grip the end of the D2/D4/D6 body.
