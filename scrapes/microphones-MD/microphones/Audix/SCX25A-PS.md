## Audix SCX25A-PS

## Stereo Mic Set

The Audix SCX25A-PS Piano Miking System is a pair of SCX25A with DFLEX mounts, XLR cables and foam-lined aluminum case.

The SCX25A is a compact side-address condenser with smooth and transparent sound. The included DFLEX clamp mounts, which can also be used for rim-mounting drum mics, allow easy placement on the piano soundboard.
