## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The Audix TM1 is an omnidirectional condenser designed for test and measurement applications. It uses a 6mm electret element, much as is found in the [Avenson STO-2](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Avenson-Audio/STO-2) and the [Earthworks](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Earthworks) omnis.

The mic has a uniform omnidirectional polar pattern below 1kHz, with diminished sensitivity between 3–15kHz.

The TM1 is also available as the “TM1 Plus,” which includes a calibration data file, a threaded acoustic windscreen, and the CA4231 machined calibration adapter.

The [Audix TM1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/TM1) is also known as: TM1 Plus.
