## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

The UEM81S is a medium length shotgun mic with a pre-polarized (electret) condenser capsule, based on the Audix PS-81 modular head amplifier.

The PS-81 operates on (and requires) a single AA battery, rather than phantom power. Two switches on the mic body set on/off and high-pass filter enable.

The capsule is removable and can be replaced with the CPS-81C Cardioid module; that combination is the Audix UEM81c.

The UEM81S ships with:

* Modular preamp supply with on-off switch and bass roll-off filter (PS-81)
* Modular cardioid capsule (CPS81S)
* Tension-fit clip (MC81)
* External Foam Windscreen (WS81S)
* 20' XLR-XLR mic cable
* 1 AA Battery
* Vinyl carrying box

The [Audix UEM81S](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/UEM81S) is also known as: UEM81-S.
