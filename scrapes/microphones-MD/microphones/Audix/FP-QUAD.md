## Audix FP QUAD

## 4-Mic Set

The FP QUAD is a 4-mic kit comprised of the Audix’ lower-cost Fusion series of microphones.

The kit includes:

* One f6 dynamic for kick drum
* One f5 dynamic for snare drum, with DVICE rim mount
* Two f9 pencil condenser for overhead

The mics and clips are packed into a padded aluminum flightcase.
