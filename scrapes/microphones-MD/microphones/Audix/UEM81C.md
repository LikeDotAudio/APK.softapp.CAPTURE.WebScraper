## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Audix UEM81C is a battery powered Electret pencil condenser with cardioid pattern.

The mic operates on single AA battery. Two switches on the mic body set on/off and high-pass filter enable.

The capsule is removable and can be replaced with the CPS-81S shotgun module.

The UEM81C ships with:

* Modular preamp supply with on-off switch and bass roll-off filter (PS-81)
* Modular cardioid capsule (CPS81C)
* Tension-fit clip (MC81)
* External Foam Windscreen (WS81C)
* 20' XLR-XLR mic cable
* Vinyl carrying box

The [Audix UEM81C](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/UEM81C) is also known as: UEM81-C.
