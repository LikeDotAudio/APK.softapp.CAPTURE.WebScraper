## CardioidAudix M44

## Detachable-Capsule Condenser Microphone

The Audix M44 is a miniature condenser microphone for drum and percussion capture. It is a modular microphone, equipped with either a Cardioid or Hypercardioid capsule (model M44-HC).

The condenser elements are minature electrets, cited as 12mm although actually measuring 10mm in diameter. They have high SPL capability (150dB at 0.5% THD), low sensitivity (3mV/Pa), and, predictably, high self-noise (25dBA). Given these specs, the seem best suited to close-miking loud sources such as horns and percussion.

The mic is terminated with mini-XLR jack. A 25' mini-XLR-F to XLR-M cable is supplied.

It ships with:

* 25' cable with mini-XLRf - XLRm connectors (CBL-M25)
* Mic stand adapter (MC-MICRO)
* Lug mount adapted for hand percussion instruments with shockmount
* ring (DCLAMP-MICRO)
* Drum rim mount attachment with shockmount ring (DVICE-MICRO) External foam windscreen (WS10)
* Carrying pouch (P1)

The [Audix M44](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/M44) is also known as: M44-HC.
