## Audix DP7

## 7-Mic Set

The DP7 is a 7-piece drum mic package with aluminum case and mounts. It is essentially the DP5A, plus a pair of ADX51 pencil mics. It includes:

* One D6 for kick drum
* One i5 dynamic for snare
* Two D2 dynamics for high toms
* One D4 dynamic for floor tom
* Two ADX51 cardioid electret condenser for overheads

![Audix DVice gooseneck rim clamp](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/audix/dvice.jpg)DP7 includes 4 DVICE mounts. The DVICE is an adjustable rim mount, with a gooseneck arm to facilitate mic positioning. The DVICE includes the DCLIP, a rigid plastic clip engineered to tightly grip the end of the D2/D4/D6 body.

The ADX51 is an electret pencil condenser mic; two are included, with plastic clips.
