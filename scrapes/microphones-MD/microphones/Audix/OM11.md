## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Audix OM11 is a reissue of the OM1, originally in production in 1985. The mic is a handheld dynamic, with a hypercardioid polar pattern.

The OM11 uses the VLM (Very Low Mass) diaphragm, which Audix claims produces a superior transient response.

The mic’s frequency range is 50 Hz–18kHz, although the low-frequency -3dB point is closer to 80Hz. The published graph shows slightly increased sensitivity from 100-800Hz and 2k-12kHz, with highest peak at 12kHz; however, the measurement distance is not provided.

The OM11 is built in by Audix in the USA.

The [Audix OM11](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/OM11) is also known as: OM1.

The mic was released in 2003.
