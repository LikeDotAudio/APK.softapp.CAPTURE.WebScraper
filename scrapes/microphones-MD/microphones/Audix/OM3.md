## Hypercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Audix OM3 is a handheld vocal dynamic with a Hypercardioid polar pattern.

Like the other OM-series vocal mics, the OM3 uses a durable body made of zinc alloy, coated in black “E-coat” non-glare finish. The grille is a steel mesh, with internal blast filtering.

The published specs for the OM3 are very similar to the OM2; the OM3 has a subtly different frequency response, is slightly more directional at high frequencies, and is more sensitive than the OM2 (2.5 mV/Pa vs. 1.7 mV/Pa).

According to Audix, the OM3 has less proximity effect, a slightly tighter pickup pattern, and more clarity in the mids than the OM2, all of which combine to sound better than the OM2 on large PA systems.

The [Audix OM3](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/OM3) is also known as: OM3S.
