## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Audix F50 is a low cost handheld dynamic from the company’s Fusion series. It is billed as a general-purpose stage vocal microphone.

The mic has a cardioid pattern. Its frequency plot shows wide boosts centered at 150Hz and 4kHz, creating a smiley EQ shape.

Two versions are available; the **F50S** has a noiseless, magnetic on/off switch.

The mic body is cast zinc alloy. The grille is steel mesh.

The [Audix F50](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/F50) is also known as: F50S, F50CBL.
