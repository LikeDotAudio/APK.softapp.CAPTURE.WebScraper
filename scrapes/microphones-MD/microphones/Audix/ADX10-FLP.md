## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

Audix ADX10-FLP is a miniature electret condenser (lavalier) designed to be easily mounted to a flute. The ADX10 can also be mounted to acoustic guitar or violin.

There are a few model variations:

* ADX10: lavalier version, includes MC10L tie clip and windscreen. 3' attached cable. For use with RAD360 wireless system.
* ADX10-P: same as above with APS-911 phantom power module. 8' attached cable.
* ADX10-FLP: Flute clip (MC-FLUTE) replaces tie clip, includes APS-911. 8' attached cable.

The attached cable is terminated with a mini XLR-F connector.

The [Audix ADX10-FLP](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Audix/ADX10-FLP) is also known as: ADX10FL.
