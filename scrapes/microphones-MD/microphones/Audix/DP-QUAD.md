## Audix DP QUAD

## 4-Mic Set

The Audix DP QUAD is a 4-piece drum miking kit with two dynamics for kick and snare, and two pencil condensers for overheads.

This set includes:

* One D6 dynamic for kick drum, with DCLIP hardmount
* One i5 dynamic for snare drum, with DVICE gooseneck rim-mount
* Two ADX51 electret condensers, with DCLIP hardmount and foam windscreen.

The ADX51 is a prepolarized cardioid pencil condenser with wide cardioid pattern and bright character. The included foam wind covers will aid in reducing wind interference in outdoor use.
