## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The OC818 is Austrian Audio’s flagship large diaphragm condenser microphone with microprocessor-controlled analog technology to remotely control the pad and high-pass filter, and to finely tune the directionality of the pickup pattern. Designed and built completely in Austria, the OC818 ships as a ‘studio set’ that includes one OC818, a custom OCS shock mount, a mic clip, a foam windscreen and a mini 5-pin xlr cable (more on this later). A ‘live set’ is also available that includes two OC818s, and skips the shock mounts.

[![OC818 Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/austrian/aa-capsule-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/austrian/aa-capsule.jpg)At the heart of this microphone is the edge-terminated CKR12 capsule, newly engineered and hand-built by Austrian Audio but modelled after the famous CK12 capsules manufactured by AKG in Vienna through the 1950s and 1960s.

> Austrian Audio
>
> … the unique ceramic capsule design is so consistent, ANY OC818 is match-paired with ANY other OC818, and when in cardioid mode, it can also be match-paired with ANY OC18, its affordable smaller brother.

On the back of the OC818 is a mini 5-pin XLR connector that serves two purposes. When partnered with the optional OCR8 Bluetooth dongle (USD $149) the polar patterns, pads and high pass filters can be remotely controlled using the open-source Polar-Pilot app available for iOS and Android.

[![OC818](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/austrian/aa-breakout-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/austrian/aa-breakout.jpg)Alternatively, by using the supplied OCC8 mini 5-pin XLR to 3-pin XLR connector, the signal from both the front and back of the capsule can be recorded separately to a stereo track in a DAW. This allows for post-recording manipulation of the polar pattern, proximity and other attributes of the captured audio using AA’s PolarDesigner plugin.

The [Austrian Audio OC818](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Austrian-Audio/OC818) is also known as: OC 818, OC-818.

The mic was released in 2019.
