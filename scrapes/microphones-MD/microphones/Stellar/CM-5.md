## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The CM-5 is a multipattern tube condenser, based on the common Chinese “C12” platform; see, for example, the Apex 460, ShuaiYin SYT-1200, Nady TCM-1150, etc.

Unlike most such microphones, the CM-5 uses an edge-terminated capsule. The capsule is not a true copy of the AKG CK-12, but produces a similar frequency response despite its significantly simpler internal construction. The capsule is a 34mm design, with 27mm diaphragms made of 6-micron Mylar.

The tube is an Electro-Harmonix 6072a, imported from Russia.

The mic’s output transformer is based on the T14 design from the AKG C12.

The CM-5 is manufactured in China. It ships with a shockmount, power supply, cable, and foam windscreen, in an aluminum flightcase.
