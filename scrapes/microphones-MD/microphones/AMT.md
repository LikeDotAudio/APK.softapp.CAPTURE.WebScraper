## Applied Microphone Technology

AMT, Applied Microphone Technology, manufactures an array of miniature microphones and pickups intended primarily for acoustic instrument miking in live, stage applications.

The company’s products incorporate innovative mounting mechanisms tailored to specific instrumental applications, such as bell clamps for woodwinds, rim clamps for drums, and gooseneck extensions that facilitate putting the transducer in the instrument’s sweet spot.

Wireless transmitter options give performers mobility; the mic and transmitter attach directly to the instrument (or performer), untethering the artist while on stage.
