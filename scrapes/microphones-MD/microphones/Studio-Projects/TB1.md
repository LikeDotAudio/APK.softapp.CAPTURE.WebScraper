## CardioidStudio Projects TB1

## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The TB1 is an inexpensive fixed-cardioid tube microphone manufactured by 797 Audio in China, to Studio Projects’ specifications.

The capsule, as in the B1 FET, is a 34mm design with a single ~25mm, 3-micron diaphragm.

The tube amplifier circuit is relatively simple, lacking a pad, high-pass filter or pattern selection control. The tube is a 6072 dual-triode. A transformer is used for impedance conversion.

The TB1 includes an elastic shockmount, a dedicated power supply (110/220V), and a 7-pin power cable. It ships in an aluminum flight case.

![Studio Projects TB1, original branding, circa 2004](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/studioprojects/tb1_orig.gif)Like most of Studio Projects’ mics, the TB1 was redesigned in 2006; the published specs show a 2dB improvement in sensitivity. Apart from that, the branding changed (the 797 Audio logo was removed) and the retail price changed (which dropped from 2006 MSRPs of $389-$479 to $299.99).

> MIX
>
> side-by-side male vocal recordings through the TB1 and a $2,000 [RODE Classic II](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Rode/Classic-ll) — an undeniably great mic — were a real eye-opener. The RODE definitely sounded bigger (and in fact, it is bigger!) and is more midrange-forward, but I couldn’t say that the TB1 sounded worse in this application — just different.
>
> Normally, you can identify inexpensive mics right away by their steely high end, but the TB1’s high end is smooth and open, toe-to-toe with the RODE — very impressive.

> DigitalProSound
>
> The TB1 exhibited a kind of silkiness that smoothed out some of the rough edges of my voice; it got better and better the more I experimented. I brought Glenda back in to sing a little and though she sounded perfect to me through the [C1](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Studio-Projects/C1), I would have picked the TB1 in a comparative shootout. Her voice is already silky smooth, and this mic just took it to the next level.

The [Studio Projects TB1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Studio-Projects/TB1) is also known as: TB-1.
