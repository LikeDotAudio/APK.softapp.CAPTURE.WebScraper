## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

Studio Projects’ C4 is a detachable-capsule pencil mic built by 797 Audio in China. The C4 is sold only as a matched pair.

Each mic has two three-way switches built into the body. The first is a three-position pad, offering 0dB, -10dB, and -20dB options. The second is a high-pass filter, offering a 6dB/octave cut at either 75Hz or 150Hz.

![](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/studioprojects/c4-original.jpg)Like most of the mics in the Studio Projects line, the C4 was redesigned in 2006 by Brent Casey of PMI Audio. According to Casey, the revised C4 has a new amplifier circuit designed for high headroom; the mic’s circuit can withstand 139 dB SPL with no pad, or 159 dB SPL with the optional non-capacitive pad engaged.

The new version also has higher sensitivity and 2dB lower noise (from 18dBA to 16dBA). Finally, a hypercardioid capsule was introduced.

The C4 kit includes a matched pair of head amps, two omni capsules, two cardioid capsules, two mic clips (p/n C4SC), two foam windscreens, a stereo mounting bar (p/n SPSB), and a hard-shell carrying case.

The [Studio Projects C4](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Studio-Projects/C4) is also known as: C-4.

The mic was released in 2003.
