## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The MD 120 is an omnidirectional dynamic microphone in a classic handheld form factor, manufactured in conjunction with Mikrofontechnik Leipzig. It is part of the MD series of dynamic vocal mics.

Features:

* frequency response optimized for intelligibility
* internal elastic suspension reduces handling noise
* wire headgrill includes built-in pop filter
* dark bronze finish

The mic ships in a wooden case.

The [Microtech Gefell MD 120](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/MD-120) is also known as: MD120.
