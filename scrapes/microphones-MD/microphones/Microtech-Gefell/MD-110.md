## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The MD 110 is a supercardioid dynamic microphone following the classic handheld-vocal mic design. It is produced in conjunction with Mikrofontechnik Leipzig.

Features:

* frequency response optimized for intelligibility
* internal elastic suspension reduces handling noise
* wire headgrill includes pop filter
* dark bronze finish

The mic ships in a wooden case.

The [Microtech Gefell MD 110](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/MD-110) is also known as: MD110.
