## Detachable-Capsule Condenser Microphone

Gefell’s M200 is a small-diaphragm, cardioid-pattern condenser microphone. It consists of the SMS 2000 preamp body with the detachable M20 cardioid capsule.

Available separately are hypercardioid and omnidirectional capsules.

The Cardioid capsule contains a half-inch (~13mm) gold-plated diaphragm in a ceramic housing. (The housing diameter is about 18mm.) The preamp circuit utilizes Gefell’s low-noise, transformerless design. Two onboard switches provide access to a -10dB preattenuation pad and a high-pass filter.

The M200 mic ships in a padded wooden case in either of two finishes:

* Satin nickel (p/n 211116)
* matte black (p/n 211117)

Available separately: mic clips, suspension mounts, foam windscreens, pop filters, etc.

The [Microtech Gefell M 200](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-200) is also known as: M200.
