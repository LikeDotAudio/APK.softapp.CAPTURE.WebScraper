## CardioidMicrotech Gefell M 930 Ts

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The M 930 Ts is a fixed-cardioid, large-diaphragm condenser with an output transformer. It is essentially a transformer-coupled version of the company’s popular M 930, and is intended to provide the same excellent performance specifications as the M930 with the additional benefits of a transformer-coupled output.

The transformer is said to provide desirable coloration, as well as providing a very low-impedance balanced output to drive long cable runs without signal degradation.

The amplifier circuit is a new design, created with the two goals of incorporating a large output transformer and retaining the the excellent performance characteristics of the M 930 transformerless circuitry.

The M 930 Ts capsule can be found in the M 930, UM930, and UM930 Twin. It has a 32mm outside diameter, with a diaphragm diameter of 28mm. The capsule is shockmounted within the headbasket, reducing the microphone’s sensitivity to structure-born noise.

The Ts model is slightly longer and heavier than its transformerless sibling, but remarkably attains the same low self-noise rating. The following table highlights the differences between the two microphones:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | M 930 | M 930 Ts |
| **Length** | 118 mm | 130 mm |
| **Weight** | 210 g | 273 g |
| **Sensitivity** | 21 mV/Pa | 23 mV/Pa |
| **Self-Noise** | 7 dBA | 7 dBA |
| **Max SPL** | 142 dB | 142 dB |
| **Impedance** | 100 Ω | 40 Ω |

The M 930 Ts is available in either Satin Nickel or Dark Bronze, and ships with a mic clip in a wooden case.

Optional accessories include a foam windscreen (p/n W 76), a pop filter (p/n P 110.35), and an elastic shockmount (in either finish).

> Paul Strikwerda, 2012
>
> Based on my criteria, the Gefell M 930 Ts scored 7 out of 8 points, plus a bonus point for size. Apart from lacking a high-pass filter (not a deal breaker) and a shock mount, it has everything a voice talent could hope for. It’s neutral without being boring, and when I listen to my audio samples, I hear myself and not some boutique sound.

The [Microtech Gefell M 930 Ts](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-930-Ts) is also known as: M930Ts.

The mic was released in 2011.
