## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The M 930 art, like its relative the M930, is a transformerless FET condenser with a fixed cardioid pattern. It differs from the M930 in these respects:

* Like the M990 art, the M930 art has a handmade PVC diaphragm in its capsule. (But the capsule is not an M7 — rather it is an M930 capsule with a PVC diaphragm.)
* The microphone body is accented with a stripe of pearl gold.

Gefell celebrated its 80th anniversary in 2008 with the release of two custom microphones, the M 990 art and M 930 art. Both feature handmade PVC diaphragms and distinctive finishes. Both mics will be discontinued in March, 2009.

*Studio Magazin* describes both “art” mics as (forgive the rough translation) *unbelievably close and detailed, with irresistably warm, clear highs and disarmingly honest mids.* It concludes, “These microphones sound simply awesome!”

The “M 930 art” is shipped in a padded wooden storage case with the MH 93.1 mic clip (p/n 2111111). Accessories are available separately (including an elastic suspension mount).

The [Microtech Gefell M 930 art](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-930-art) is also known as: M930-art.

The mic was released in 2008.
