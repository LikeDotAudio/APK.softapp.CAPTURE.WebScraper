## Microtech Gefell M940 Stereo XY

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The M 940 XY Stereo kit includes a matched pair of M 940 (Supercardioid) large-diaphragm FET condenser microphones, and the SH93 XY adapter bar, which holds one microphone inverted above the other so that their capsules can be as near together as possible.

[![MTG TD-SH XY stereo bar](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/gefell/SH93-sm.jpg)](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/gefell/SH93.jpg)The set is available in either a Satin Nickel (p/n 211163) or Dark Bronze (p/n 211164) finish, both of which ship in a 334mm x 148mm x 76mm wooden case.

The EH 93 rubber isolation mount and EA 93 elastic shockmount for the M930/M940/M950 is available separately, in either Nickel or Bronze finishes.
