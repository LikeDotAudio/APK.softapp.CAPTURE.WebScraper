## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The MD 100 is a cardioid dynamic microphone in a classic handheld form factor, manufactured in conjunction with Mikrofontechnik Leipzig.

Features:

* frequency response optimized for intelligibility
* internal elastic suspension reduces handling noise
* wire headgrill includes built-in pop filter
* dark bronze finish

The mic ships in a wooden box.

The [Microtech Gefell MD 100](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/MD-100) is also known as: MD100.
