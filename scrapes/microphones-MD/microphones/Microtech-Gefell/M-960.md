## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The M960 is a large-diaphragm omnidirectional FET condenser, known for Gefell’s low noise, transformerless amplifier circuit. It was designed for ambient recording.

The mic’s frequency response is generally flat from 20Hz through 1kHz, with a sizeable on-axis presence peak that rises to +12dB at 10-11kHz. On-axis response is near -1dB at 20kHz. Off-axis response is smoother; the presence peak tops out at +4dB.

The M 960 is available in a single finish, dark bronze, and ships in a padded wooden storage case (p/n 211171).

The [Microtech Gefell M 960](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-960) is also known as: M960.
