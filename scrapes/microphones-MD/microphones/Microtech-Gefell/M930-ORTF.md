## Microtech Gefell M930 Stereo ORTF

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The M 930 ORTF Stereo kit includes a matched pair of the Cardioid M 930 large-diaphragm FET condenser microphones, and the TD93 ORTF adapter bar.

[![MTG TD-93 ORTF stereo bar](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/gefell/TD93-sm.jpg)](http://cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/gefell/TD93.jpg)The set is available in either a Satin Nickel (p/n 211165) or Dark Bronze (p/n 211165) finish, both of which ship in a 334mm x 148mm x 76mm wooden case.

The EH 93 rubber isolation mount and EA 93 elastic shockmount for the M930/M940/M950 is available separately, in either Nickel or Bronze finishes.

See also the M930 XY kit, and the M940 and M950 ORTF kits.
