## Omnidirectional Pencil Condenser Microphone

The M296 is the third mic in Gefell’s series of nickel-diaphragm pencil condensers. It uses a capsule design from the company’s measurement and test series, with a 22mm, 0.8-micron nickel diaphragm housed in ceramic for “temperature-stable performance” and uniform sensitivity across frequencies.

Some early reviews and literature describe the M29x series as using 1'' capsules, but this is only true of the M296, and then refers only to the outside diameter of the capsule assembly. The other microphones in this series employ smaller capsules, with 16mm diaphragms.

The response of the M 296 is flat from 20 Hz through about 2 kHz, where a gentle +2dB rise occurs. This voicing is intended to bring out detail when used for ambient recording.

The M 296 ships in a padded wooden case in either of two finishes:

* satin nickel (p/n 211134)
* black matt (p/n 211135)
