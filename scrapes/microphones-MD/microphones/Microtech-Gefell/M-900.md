## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The M 900 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser with an unusual shape, designed for uncolored reproduction of vocal and instrumental soloists.

It is equipped with a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter. See also the M910, a hypercardioid version of the same microphone.

The M900 ships in a wooden case, with a dark bronze finish (p/n 211140). Optional accessories include a foam windscreen, pop screen, stand clamp (p/n MKV 8), elastic suspension mount, and more.

The [Microtech Gefell M 900](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Microtech-Gefell/M-900) is also known as: M900.
