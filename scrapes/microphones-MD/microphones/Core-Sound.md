## Core Sound

Core Sound, LLC, specializes in portable high-fidelity audio devices, such as battery-powered mic pres and DACs, miniature/stealthy microphones, and their new “TetraMic” soundfield microphone.

The company was founded in 1990 by Len Moskowitz.
