## CardioidSontronics STC-20

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The STC-20 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser with a fixed Cardioid pattern. It was introduced as an inexpensive all-purpose studio mic.

It shares a large-diaphragm capsule design with the company’s STC-2, and appears to be presented as a lower-cost version of same — it lacks the pad, HPF, shockmount, and flightcase of the STC-2.

The STC-20 ships with a ring mount. An elastic shockmount is available separately.

(Additional details about this mic are forthcoming.)

The [Sontronics STC-20](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Sontronics/STC-20) is also known as: STC20.

The mic was released in 2010.
