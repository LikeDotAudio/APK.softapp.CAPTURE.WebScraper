## CardioidNingbo Shengke Electronic Co.,Ltd RM 6

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The RM 6 is a large-diaphragm FET condenser microphone with a fixed cardioid pickup pattern.

The mic uses a single-sided K47-style capsule, and a transformerless output circuit.

The mic ships in a padded flight case, with an elastic shockmount and a cloth-mesh pop filter.

Permalink: [Ningbo Shengke Electronic Co.,Ltd RM-6](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/iSK/RM-6)

The mic was released in 2010.
