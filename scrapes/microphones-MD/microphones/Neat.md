## Neat Microphones

Neat Microphones was founded by the cofounders of BLUE, Skipper Wise and Martins Saulespurens, with Martin Wolf and Ken Niles. The company’s mission is to create the “next generation of microphones.” As a part of Gibson Brands, Neat is able to take advantages of economies of scale to produce high-end products at consumer-level pricing.
