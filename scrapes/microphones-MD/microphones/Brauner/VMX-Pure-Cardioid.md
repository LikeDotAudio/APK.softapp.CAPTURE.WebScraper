## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The VMX Pure Cardioid is the fixed-cardioid version of the Brauner VMX multipattern tube condenser. Both microphones are part of Brauner’s “Charming” microphones, designed to enhance any source rather than simply capture it neutrally.

The VMX Pure Cardioid is designed to sound identical to the VMX in cardioid mode although with even lower self noise.

Although the power supply and internal circuitry are different, the body of the microphone is identical to the VMX.

The VMX Pure Cardioid ships with Aluminum case, power supply, VOVOX Tubelink cable, BMS1 suspension mount.
