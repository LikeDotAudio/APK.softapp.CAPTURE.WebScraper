## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The Phantom Classic is a large diaphragm FET condenser with wide cardioid polar pattern.

> Dirk Brauner
>
> The various Phantom types have the same circuitry design (beside minor changes which do not have an influence on the sonic character), and have the same capsule.

The Phantom Classic ships with: Aluminum case, VOVOX XLR Cable, and BMS-2 Suspension mount.

The Phantom Classic is also available in a “Basic Edition,” which includes a hard mount but no other accessories.

According to Dirk Brauner, the Phantom Classic and Phantom C are the same microphone. The Phantom AE is “a bit different,” although Brauner has not described what the differences are.
