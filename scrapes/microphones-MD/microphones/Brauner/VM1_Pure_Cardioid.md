## Cardioid Tube Condenser Microphone

The VM1 Pure Cardioid is a single polar pattern version of the VM1 hand-built tube condenser microphone. It is part of Brauner’s “Natural” product line, intended to capture a source without enhancement.

As with all Brauner microphones, the VM1 Pure Cardioid has exceptionally low noise and high sensitivity.

The VM1 Pure Cardioid is designed to sound identical to the VM1 in cardioid mode although with with 2dB lower noise.  
Although the power supply and internal circuitry are different, the body of the microphone is identical to the VM1.

The VM1 Pure Cardioid ships with Aluminum case, power supply, VOVOX Tubelink cable, BMS1 suspension mount.
