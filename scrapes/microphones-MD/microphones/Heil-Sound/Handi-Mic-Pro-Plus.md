## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Intended as a small, general-purpose dynamic mic, the Handi Mic Pro Plus is the smallest and least expensive of Heil’s PR series of dynamic microphones. Suggested uses include:

* interviewing
* drums (toms)
* Leslie cabinets

The Handi mic follows the model of the other PR dynamics: relatively large, low-mass dynamic element whose magnet is constructed of a proprietary blend of neodymium, iron, and boron; excellent rejection of off-axis sound; consistent cardioid pattern; reduced proximity effect; [high SPL capability](/2008/02/26/maximum-spl-of-dynamic-microphones/).

The [Heil Sound Handi Mic Pro Plus](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Heil-Sound/Handi-Mic-Pro-Plus) is also known as: handi, handimic.
