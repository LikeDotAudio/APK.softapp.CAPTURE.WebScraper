## Heil Sound HDK-7

## 7-Mic Set

The HDK-7 kit includes:

* Two PR-22 (snare, hi-hat)
* One PR-48 (kick drum)
* Two PR-28 with HH-1 rim mounts (toms)
* Two PR-30B (overheads)

The PR30B is identical to the PR-30, aside from the color. The ‘B’ model is finished in black with a red end grille, to match the other drum microphones. (The stock PR-30 is a matte silver color.)

The kit ships in a hard-shell road case.
