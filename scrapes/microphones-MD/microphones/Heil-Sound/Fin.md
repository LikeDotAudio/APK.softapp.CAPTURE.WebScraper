## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Fin is a phantom powered dynamic microphone with retro styling.

The capsule uses a 1 1/8'' aluminum low mass voice coil similar to the [PR 20](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Heil-Sound/PR-20).

The steel body is copper plated, then double chrome plated. The mic “head” angle is adjustable. The mic body is threaded for attachment to a mic stand, and contains the XLR output jack.

A brightly colored (blue, red or white) foam windscreen is illuminated by 4 LEDs inside. +48V is required to power the LED only.

The Fin was used as a prop in the 2012 film *The Hunger Games* (“May the odds be ever in your favor!”).
