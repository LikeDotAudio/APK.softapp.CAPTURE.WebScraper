## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Heil PR-20 UT (utility) is the same as the PR-20 with fewer included accessories.

The PR-20 UT includes foam windscreen, stand mount, and zippered pouch.

The [Heil Sound PR-20 UT](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Heil-Sound/PR-20UT) is also known as: PR20UT.
