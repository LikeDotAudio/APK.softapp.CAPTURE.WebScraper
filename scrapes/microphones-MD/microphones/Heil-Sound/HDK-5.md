## Heil Sound HDK-5

## 5-Mic Set

The Heil HDK-5 is a 5-mic set of dynamic mics for drum kit miking. It includes four of Heil’s Handi Mic Pro Plus, a small dynamic, and the PR-48 kick drum mic. Two of the Handi Mic Pro Plus can be mounted to toms or snare via the included two HH-1 rim mounts; the other two can be mounted on stands (not included) for overhead use.

The kit is covered by a 1-year warranty (parts only; 90 days on labor), and ships in a padded vinyl carrying case.
