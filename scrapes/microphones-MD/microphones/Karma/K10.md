## CardioidKarma Audio K10

## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

[![K10](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/karma/k10-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/karma/k10-circuit.jpg)

The K-10 is a cardioid pencil condenser microphone with detachable capsules and a transformer-coupled circuit. No alternative capsules have been made available, but likely will be in the future, as it is a simple screw-off design that lends itself easily to expansion.

[![K10 schematic](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/karma/k10-schematic.png)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/karma/k10-schematic.png)The amplifier circuit is built with surface-mount components. It uses the 2SK170 JFET that is common to most Chinese-made microphones.

The mics are sold in matched pairs, and ship in a padded hard-shell case with plastic mic clips.

[![Karma K-10](http://farm4.static.flickr.com/3293/3130191494_5a761e3579_m.jpg)](http://www.flickr.com/photos/24455487@N05/3130191494/ "Karma K-10")All Karma mics carry a 2-year warranty.
