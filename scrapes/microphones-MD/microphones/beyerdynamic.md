## beyerdynamic

beyerdynamic was founded by Eugen Beyer in Berlin in 1924, and has grown to become an international leader in microphone and headphone technology.

The company was among the the first, if not the first to introduce Neodymium magnets in its microphones, with the TG-X 180 (a handheld vocal mic replaced in the beyer product line by the TG-X 20 around 1995).
