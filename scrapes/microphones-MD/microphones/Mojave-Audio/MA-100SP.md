## Mojave Audio MA-100SP

## Stereo Mic Set with Stereo Bar

The MA-100SP is a stereo pair of the Mojave MA-100 tube condenser microphones, bundled with a two-channel power supply and a stereo bar for ORTF and XY stereo applications. The kit includes both Cardioid and Omnidirectional capsules.

It ships in a padded aluminum flightcase with stereo power supply, cables, mics, and capsules. The included mounts are basic plastic clips; shockmounts are available separately (from a variety of manufacturers).
