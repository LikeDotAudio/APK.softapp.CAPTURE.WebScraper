## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The MC49 is a small-diaphragm pencil condenser microphone. It has a fixed cardioid pickup pattern.

![Nevaton MC49 grille](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/nevaton/MC49-grille.jpg)The original version of this microphone had a unique dual-diaphragm capsule; each membrane was connected to its own amplifier circuit. This architecture raised the mic’s sensitivity to 20 mV/Pa.

The capsule designed changed around 2010, to a more-traditional single-diaphragm design.

The capsule is shockmounted to reduce handling noise.

The body is brass, finished in a dark gray epoxy paint.

![Nevaton MC49 case](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/nevaton/MC49-case-sm.jpg)The mic ships in a padded hardwood box. A shockmount is included, as is a frequency-response plot for the individual microphone.

All Nevaton microphones are manufactured in St. Petersburg, Russia.

The [Nevaton MC49-C](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nevaton/MC49) is also known as: MK49, MC49-C.

The mic was released in 1987.
