## ShotgunNevaton MC401

## Shotgun Condenser Microphone

The MC401 is a shotgun condenser microphone.

Its pickup pattern is described as “supercardioid (hypercardioid below 2kHz).”

The mic’s frequency response is generally flat (±1dB) from 40Hz - 5kHz. It has a +5dB presence peak centered at 10kHz. Its HF -3dB point is above 20kHz.

![Nevaton MC401 case](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/nevaton/MC401-case-sm.jpg)The mic ships in a padded hardwood box with a mic clip.

All Nevaton microphones are manufactured in St. Petersburg, Russia.

The [Nevaton MC401](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Nevaton/MC401) is also known as: MK401.

The mic was released in 2002.
