## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The Regensburg Dom is a large-diaphragm tube mic, presented as a “vintage German design with bite.”

[![M7 Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/barecapsules/front/sm/cathedral-pipes-m7.jpg)](/capsules/#M7)The capsule is an American-made M7, a 32mm capsule with glued-on diaphragms of 6-micron Mylar. The tube is a PF86 pentode (similar to an EF86), sourced from Amperex or Valvo Holland. The output transformer is a Cinemag CM-2461NiCo. High-quality components are used throughout the circuit, including Wima, Solen, and Multicap capacitors.

The mic ships in a metal flightcase with its power supply, a shockmount, foam windscreen, and cables.
