## Bidirectional Active Ribbon Microphone

The Seville is an active ribbon microphone made in the style of the RCA 77 and AEA R84, with a pill-shaped body on a swiveling yoke mount. It uses a single 2.5-micron ribbon element, a Cinemag CM-9887 output transformer, and a JFET-based transformerless active output circuit.
