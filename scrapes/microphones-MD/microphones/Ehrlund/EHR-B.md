## CardioidEhrlund Microphones EHR-B

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The EHR-B is the “broadcast” version of Göran Ehrlund’s EHR-M condenser mic. Both are premium, fixed-Cardioid, transformerless condenser microphones with the company’s unique (and patented) triangular capsule design.

The only difference between the two mics is the inclusion of a fixed high-pass filter (at 80Hz) on the B version, which is built specifically for broadcast use.

[![EHR-M Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/ehrlund/EHR-M-capsule-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/ehrlund/EHR-M-capsule.jpg)The mic’s triangular capsule is said to be less subject to resonance than a circular diaphragm would be. Read more in the patent, linked from the sidebar, or in the [EHR-M profile](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Ehrlund/EHR-M).
