## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The design goals of the AMT350 were to capture natural sound with a high degree of transparency, definition and detail. It follows the classic large-diaphragm cardioid model, resulting in a utility mic than can be used for vocals or on a variety of instruments.

The [Applied Microphone Technology 350](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/AMT/350) is also known as: AMT-350, AMT350.
