## Mercenary Audio Mfg

Mercenary Audio Manufacturing is a division of Mercenary Industries, which includes the well-known boutique pro audio gear dealership formerly run by Fletcher.

The company’s only product, as of this writing, is the Mercenary KM-69 small-diaphragm FET condenser microphone.
