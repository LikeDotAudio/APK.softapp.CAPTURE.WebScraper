## JZ Microphones DMK1

## 3-Mic Set

The JZ DMK1 is a set of three BT 201 condensers, two with Wide Cardioid capsules and one with the -20dB padded Wide Cardioid capsule (for kick drum).

The three mics and capsules ship in a padded aluminum flightcase.
