## CardioidJZ Microphones HH1

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

The HH1 is a dynamic stage microphone. It is JZ’s first dynamic microphone.

The mic uses a Neodymium magnet within its cartridge. This suggests that the published sensitivity figure (-74dB / 0.119mV/Pa) is likely incorrect, because dynamic mics with rare earth magnets tend to have much higher sensitivity (~10x higher).

The HH1 ships with a hard mount and a storage pouch.

Permalink: [JZ Microphones HH1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/JZ/HH1)

The mic was released in 2014.
