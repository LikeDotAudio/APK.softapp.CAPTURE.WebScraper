## Avantone Pro CDMK-7

## 7-Mic Set

The CDMK7 is a 7-mic set for drum kit. It is the Avantone Pro CDMK5, plus two additional overhead microphones:

* One MONDO for kick drum
* One ADM for snare drum
* Three ATOMs for toms
* Two CK-1 condensers for overheads

The kit includes the company’s metal-body SSM elastic shockmount, one for every microphone. Also included are four PK-1 Pro-Klamp rim mounts for the snare and tom mics.
