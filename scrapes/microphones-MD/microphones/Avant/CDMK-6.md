## Avantone Pro CDMK-6

## 6-Mic Set

The CDMK-6 is a six-mic drum kit set consisting of:

* One MONDO for kick drum
* One ADM for snare drum
* Two ATOMs for toms
* Two CK-1 small-diaphragm condensers for overheads

It is essentially the CDMK-4 plus a pair of overhead mics.

The CK-1 condensers have detachable capsules, and include a pair of the CK-1 Cardioid capsule. Users of the CDMK-6 have the option of purchasing the compatible Hypercardioid or Omni capsules to expand the applications of this kit.

All six microphones include the company’s SSM metal shockmount. In addition, three PK-1 Pro-Klamp rim mounts are included for the snare and tom microphones.

The kit ships in an oversized tweed-covered vintage style suitcase, whose foam insert includes extra cut-outs for additional microphones to be purchased separately.
