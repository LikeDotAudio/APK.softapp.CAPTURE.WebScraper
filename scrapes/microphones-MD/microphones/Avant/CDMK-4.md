## Avantone Pro CDMK-4

## 4-Mic Set

The CDMK4 is Avantone Pro’s smallest drum mic kit. It contains four microphones:

* One MONDO for kick drum
* One ADM for snare drum
* Two ATOMs for toms

All four mics include the company’s SSM elastic shockmount. Three PK-1 Pro Klamp rim mounts are included for the snare and tom mics.

The kit ships in an oversized, padded, velvet-lined, vintage-style tweed suitcase, which has foam cutouts for additional microphones should the owner decide to expand the kit.

If you need a third tom mic, see the CDMK5 or CDMK7 (which includes overhead microphones too).
