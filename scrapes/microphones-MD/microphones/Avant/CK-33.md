## X/Y StereoAvantone Pro CK-33

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Stereo Microphone

The CK-33 is a stereo FET condenser microphone. Two large-diaphragm 32mm capsules are mounted vertically; the top one swivels 90 degrees relative to the bottom.

Both are fixed-cardioid capsules. The mic can therefore record two cardioid signals with up to 90° of separation — this is single-point, XY-stereo. (For additional stereo options, see the CK-40, which offers two multipattern capsules.)

As are all the mics in Avant’s “C” series, the CK-33 body is painted metallic red (“Cabernet”), with polished nickel trim and plated steel grilles.

[![Avant CK-33 Package](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/avant/ck-33_kit_sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/avant/ck-33_kit.jpg)The CK-33 includes a padded wooden storage box for the mic, a signal-splitter box (5-pin XLRF to two 3-pin XLRM), an elastic shockmount, and a 5-pin (stereo) mic cable, all packaged into a foam-lined flight case. It is covered by a 5-year warranty.

The [Avantone Pro CK-33](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Avant/CK-33) is also known as: CK33.

The mic was released in 2008.
