## OmnidirectionalCardioidHypercardioidAvantone Pro CV-28

## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Tube Condenser Microphone

The CV-28 is a small-diaphragm tube condenser with three interchangeable capsules providing three pickup patterns: cardioid, omni, and hypercardioid. Unlike related multipattern SDCs, only the internal capsule is changed — the external grille assembly must be removed first, to expose the threaded capsule head.

The diaphragm in these capsules measures 6 microns in thickness, with an approximate diaphragm diameter (measured by us within the inside diameter of the trim ring at the top of the capsule assembly) of 17mm.

[![CV-28 Circuit](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/avant/CV28-circuit-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/avant/CV28-circuit.jpg)The microphone shares exterior metalwork with a few other models, such as the Advanced Audio CM-28, the Groove Tubes GT44, and the Sterling ST44. However, the GT44 and ST44 circuits differ considerably from the CV-28. Further, the capsule mounting platform on the ST44/GT44 is slightly different than the CV-28.

The tube is a 6J1B, a Chinese-made tube sourced by the manufacturer. The transformer is also of Chinese origin.

The mic includes a padded wooden box, suspension shockmount, dedicated power supply, power cable, 3 interchangeable capsules (omni, cardioid, hypercardioid) within a plastic pillbox, an aluminum flightcase, and a 5-year warranty.

The [Avantone Pro CV-28](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Avant/CV-28) is also known as: CV28.

The mic was released in 2006.
