## CardioidAvantone Pro AMK Mondo

## Cardioid Dynamic Microphone

Following the model of competing kick-drum dynamics, the AMK has a pronounced EQ curve, described as:

> Large bass boost between 50-90hz   
> Large attack/presence peak between 3500-7000kz

It ships in a padded plastic clamshell case and carries a 5-year warranty.

The [Avantone Pro AMK Mondo](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Avant/AMK) is also known as: Mondo.

The mic was released in 2007.
