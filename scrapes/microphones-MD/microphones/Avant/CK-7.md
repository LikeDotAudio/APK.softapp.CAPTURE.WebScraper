## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

Avant’s CK-7 is a transformer-coupled large-diaphragm FET condenser with three pickup patterns: cardioid, omni, figure-8.

Onboard switches provide access to a -10dB pad and a high-pass filter (-6dB/octave below 80Hz).

The mic’s capsule is a dual-diaphragm, multipattern design. The capsule diameter is 35mm; the Mylar diaphragms measure approximately 30mm across, and are center-terminated.

The CK-7 ships with an elastic shockmount, a wooden storage case, and an aluminum flightcase.

The [Avantone Pro CK-7](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Avant/CK-7) is also known as: CK7.

The mic was released in 2006.
