## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Schoeps “Colette” series of detachable-capsule microphones was introduced in 1973. The CMC 6 is the latest analog head amplifier in the series. It is a high-performance transformerless pencil condenser microphone with 20 compatible capsules.

Schoeps produces four variations of the CMC 6 head amplifier:

* **CMC 6** (standard) - includes a fixed -12dB/octave rolloff below 20Hz to prevent pickup of subsonic and mechanical vibration.
* **CMC 6 Uxt** - extends the high-frequency response of all “axially addressed” (end-address) capsules to ~40kHz.
* **CMC “linear”** - maintains a flat low-end response below 20Hz. By special order, the LF response can be extended (flat) al low as 3Hz.
* **CMC “+5 dB”** - raises the sensitivity of the attached capsule by 5dB, and lowers the max SPL by the same amount.

The CMC 6 head amp, like all the products in Schoeps’ Colette series, is available in either a matte grey or nickel finish, with other finishes available by request. The mic ships in a wooden case with room for two microphones, and includes a mic clip (p/n SG 20).

*How is the CMC 6 different from the CMC 5?*

A partial listing of the differences follows:

* The CMC 6 is more immune to RFI (radio frequency interference).
* The CMC 5 requires +48V, whereas the CMC 6 can run on either +12V or +48V (with automatic detection and switching).
* The CMC 6 HPF attenuates low frequencies below 20Hz at a slope of 12dB/octave; in the CMC 5, the “corner” frequency is 30Hz, and the slope is 6dB/octave.

At least two revisions of the CMC 6 exist. The newer one, released in 2004, incorporates improved RFI shielding, and can be distinguished by the gold-colored “shield plate” on its XLR3M output jack.

The [Schoeps Mikrofone CMC 6](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Schoeps/CMC-6) is also known as: CMC-6U.

The mic was released in 1992.
