## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The CMC 5 is a transformerless, balanced head amplifier for the Schoeps “Colette” series of modular microphones. Although a newer version has been released (see the CMC 6), the CMC 5 remains in production.

Any of 20 different capsules can be used with the CMC head amps, including both end- and side-address versions of most polar patterns. Special-purpose capsules for speech, for distant miking, and for very close miking are available.

*How is the CMC 5 different from the CMC 6?*

These two head amps differ in their powering modes and the shape of their built-in high-pass filters.

The CMC 5 required +48V phantom power, but runs at a low 4mA current draw. The CMC 6 has an additional powering mode (+12V, 8mA) with an internal detection and switching circuit.

The CMC 5 has a fixed low-frequency filter, intended to reduce rumble and mechanical noise. It is a -6dB/octave slope, rolling off below 30Hz. In contrast, the CMC 6 filter is twice as steep, but begins at a lower frequency.

This head amp is produced in two finishes: matte grey and satin nickel. It can be special-ordered with a different low-frequency filter slope or cutoff for special applications.

The [Schoeps Mikrofone CMC 5](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Schoeps/CMC-5) is also known as: CMC-5U.
