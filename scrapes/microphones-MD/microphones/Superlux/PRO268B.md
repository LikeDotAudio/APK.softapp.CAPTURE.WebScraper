## Cardioid Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Superlux PRO268B is a back-electret pencil condenser with detachable cardioid capsule. This is a low-cost mic, part of Superlux’ “performance” series, within which it is recommended for overhead applications.

The mic has a fairly high noise floor of 25dB, making it unsuitable for quiet acoustic instruments.

Its frequency response chart shows reduced low frequency response below 200Hz and a broad +5dB peak centered at 8500 Hz.

It ships with the HM30S clip with screw knob, YA2 threaded adapter, S20 windscreen, and a storage pouch.

The [Superlux PRO268B](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRO268B) is also known as: PRO-268B.
