## CardioidSuperlux PRO238MKII

## Cardioid Condenser Microphone

The PRO238 Mk II is a handheld vocal condenser with a large-diaphragm capsule and a fixed Cardioid pattern.

The capsule is described as having a 1-inch diaphragm made of 3-micron Mylar.

Removing the capsule basket reveals the switchable 100Hz high-pass filter.

The mic’s frequency response is nominally flat with multiple presence peaks at 2.5kHz, 7kHz and 10kHz.

The MKII improves upon the original in frequency response handling noise isolation.

The [Superlux PRO238MKII](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRO238) is also known as: PRO238.
