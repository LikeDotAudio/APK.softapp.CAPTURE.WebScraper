## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Superlux TOP248 is a handheld supercardioid dynamic intended for stage vocal applications.

The mic capsule is described as a “double membrane” design, which is not something we’ve seen before. It uses neodymium magnets for higher output, and an aluminum voice coil. The output is transformer-coupled.

The mic body is zinc alloy and topped with strong metal mesh ball windscreen.

This mic is also available with a lockable, magnetic on/off switch, under model TOP248S.

The [Superlux TOP248](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/TOP248) is also known as: TOP-248, TOP248S.
