## Supercardioid Condenser Microphone

The Superlux PRO258 is a dynamic supercardioid mic intended for instrument or vocal applications, both on stage or in the studio.

The dynamic cartridge uses a neodymium magnet and aluminum voice coil. The body style is similar to Shure Beta 57A.

The mic is the same basic design as the PRO248 but with a more compact grille. It is possible the capsule resonator varies slightly from the 248.

It ships with stand mount and foam-lined plastic case.

The [Superlux PRO258](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRO258) is also known as: PRO-258.
