## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The Superlux D112/C is a handheld omnidirectional harmonica mic.

[![D 112/C schematic, appears to be a clone of Shure's diagram](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/superlux/harmonica-mic-copy-sm.png)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/superlux/harmonica-mic-copy.png)The mic body design and internal circuitry are similar to the Shure 520DX “Green Bullet”, a classic blues harmonica mic. The D112/C’s published wiring diagram is in fact identical to the 520DX, down to the wire colors.

The mic has a contours to fit the hand while playing. A volume control allows the player to adjust output level.

The mic has a 6-meter cable terminated with 1/4'' TS male plug; it was designed to plug into a guitar amplifier. The CP838 impedance converter is available separately.

The [Superlux D 112/C](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/D112-C) is also known as: D112/C.
