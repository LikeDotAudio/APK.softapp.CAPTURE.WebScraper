## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

Superlux PRO248 is a handheld supercardioid stage dynamic. The mic is intended for live vocal use.

The mic transducer uses a neodymium magnet and aluminum voice coil.

The published frequency response plot shows significant proximity effect centered at 200Hz. A strong presence peak is seen at 6kHz.

The mic is also available with silent on/off switch (248S).

It ships with stand mount and foam-lined plastic case.

The [Superlux PRO248](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRO248) is also known as: PRO248S.
