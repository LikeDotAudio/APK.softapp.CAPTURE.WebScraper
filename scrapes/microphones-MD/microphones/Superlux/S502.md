## Cardioid Condenser Stereo Microphone

The Superlux S502 is a T-shaped stereo microphone with two small-diaphragm cardioid capsules in fixed-ORTF array.

The S502 copies the form of the Schoeps MSTC 64 U.

![S502 Capsule](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/superlux/S502-capsule.jpg)The two 1/2-inch true-condenser capsules output to 5-pin XLR jack. 5-pin to dual 3-pin XLR splitter is included. The output circuit appears to be transformerless.

The published frequency-response graph shows a flat response from 120Hz–5kHz, with a gentle +5dB presence peak centered at 10kHz.

The mic ships with a shockmount (p/n HM30) and a 0.6m Y-Splitter Cable (5-pin female XLRF to twin 3-pin XLRM).
