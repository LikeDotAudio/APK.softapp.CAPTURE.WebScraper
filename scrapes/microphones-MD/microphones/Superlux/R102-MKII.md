## Bidirectional Active Ribbon Microphone

The Superlux R102 MKII is an active ribbon microphone. It is a modified R102, with the primary difference being the extended high-frequency response in the MK II.

Whereas the original R102 rolls off sharply above 5kHz, the Mk II has its -3dB point around 13kHz. The difference is in the design of the ribbon transducer; the Mk II’s electronics are identical to the original R102. In other words, the extended HF is *not* due to electronic equalization.

The body design for both microphones is the same, save that the Mk II uses a gold, rather than silver grille.

The R102 Mk II ships with the HM31 Shock mount, HM63 metal windscreen, aluminum case, a 6M mic cable, cloth pouch, and U-bracket mount.
