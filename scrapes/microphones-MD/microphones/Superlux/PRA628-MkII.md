## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PRA628 Mk II is an inexpensive dynamic instrument microphone. Its flat, side-address design allows for close positioning in tight spaces.

This flat-front side-address design bears a strong resemblance to the Sennheiser e606/609 Silver/906 mics.

With the MKII version of the 628, Superlux made the response flatter and changed the head shape from lollipop to something resembling an icecream treat.

The [Superlux PRA628MKII](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRA628-MkII) is also known as: PRA628.
