## Detachable-Capsule Pencil Condenser Microphone

The Superlux S241/U3 is a medium-diaphragm FET pencil condenser with detachable capsule.

The mic uses a 1/2'' gold-evaporated diaphragm. The mic ships with a cardioid capsule; an omni capsule is sold separately.

It has a low self-noise of only 16dB.

Switches on the mic body provide -10 dB or -20 dB attenuation and HPF at 75 Hz or 150 Hz.

Accessories Furnished: HM40 shock mount;YA2 threaded adapter;S09F windscreen;HM43 metal mesh pop screen

The [Superlux S241/U3](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/S241-U3) is also known as: S241 U3.
