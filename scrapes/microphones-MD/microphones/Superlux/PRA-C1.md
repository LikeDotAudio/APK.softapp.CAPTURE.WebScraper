## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The PRA C1 is a handheld stage dynamic.

The mic has a supercardioid pattern and frequency response of 50 - 16,000 Hz, Frequency plot shows smooth tapering of lows from 200 Hz down. An 8dB peak appears at 3.5kHz.

Th mic capsule uses a neodymium magnet.

An On/Off switch is on the mic side.

The mic is also sold in 3x and 5x packages as PRA-C3 and PRA-C5 respectively.

The [Superlux PRA C1](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRA-C1) is also known as: PRAC, PRA-C3, PRA-C5.
