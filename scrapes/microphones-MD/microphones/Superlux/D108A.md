## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The D108 is a supercardioid handheld dynamic with locking on/off switch.

The D108 series comes in two versions. Both are supercardioid vocal mics; they differ in frequency response. The D108A rolls the low end off at a higher frequency (~150Hz), and has a higher-amplitude presence peak (+5dB at 5.5kHz), whereas the D108B has a fuller low end below 500Hz, and a slightly flatter high-frequency response (+3.5dB at 5.5kHz).

The Superlux D107 series is the same as the D108A/B but with more compact construction.

The mic ships with hard clip and carrying pouch.

The [Superlux D108A](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/D108A) is also known as: D108B, D108.
