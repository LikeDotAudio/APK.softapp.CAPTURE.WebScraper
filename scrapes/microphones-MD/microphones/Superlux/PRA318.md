## Omnidirectional Dynamic Microphone

The Superlux PRA-318S is an omnidirectional dynamic for handheld interview use.

Its frequency response curve is shaped to enhance clarity in speech range: it rolls off the lows below 250Hz, and enhances presence with a wide +8dB boost at 3.5kHz. The -3dB points are 60Hz and 12kHz.

The PRA318 is available in two lengths:

* **PRA318L**, 23cm
* **PRA318S**, 18cm

Both versions include a hard mount (p/n HM-30) and foam windscreen (p/n S-07).

The [Superlux PRA318](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/PRA318) is also known as: PRA318S, PRA318L.
