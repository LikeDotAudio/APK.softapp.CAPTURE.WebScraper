## Supercardioid Dynamic Microphone

The Superlux TOP258 is a dynamic supercardoid intended for stage vocal and instrument use. It appears to be identical to the TOP248 vocal mic, with a flattened grille.

The published frequency response and polar charts differ from the TOP248. Whether this is an effect of the grille shape, or capsule design, or capsule mounting, is unknown.

The [Superlux TOP258](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/TOP258) is also known as: TOP-258.
