## Multi-Pattern Tube Condenser Microphone

The CMH8G is a 9-pattern large-diaphragm vacuum tube condenser.

The capsule is a K67 derivative, likely with a 32mm outside diameter. The diaphragms are made of 3-micron Mylar.

The mic has a transformer-coupled output. The tube model is not specified.

The mic’s self-noise is excellent for a large-diaphragm tube mic, at 14dBA in Cardioid (18dBA Omni/Figure 8).

The CMH8G ships with its power supply, cables, shockmount, hard mount, and foam windscreen in a padded flightcase.

The [Superlux CMH8G](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Superlux/CMH8G) is also known as: CM-H8G.

The mic was released in 2004.
