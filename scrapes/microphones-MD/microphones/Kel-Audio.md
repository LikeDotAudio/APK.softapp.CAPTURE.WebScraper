## Kel Audio

Kel Audio is the first brand of Kelly Dueck; the company manufactured a small line of low-cost microphones, designed in Winnipeg, Canada, and manufactured in China.

The line was discontinued in 2014, following the introduction of a premium line under the Vertical Microphones brand.
