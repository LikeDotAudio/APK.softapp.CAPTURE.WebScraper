## InnerTUBE Audio

InnerTUBE Audio is a small, enigmatic manufacturer of unique studio electronics and microphones located midway between San Luis Obispo and Los Angeles near the California coast.

The company’s product line includes a tube compressor, a tube summing box, the “MagMic” — a tube condenser built into a Maglite case — and tube retrofit/upgrade amplifier kits for the [Neumann U87](http://recordinghacks.com/microphones/Neumann/U-87-Ai) and Gefell UM70 / UM71.
