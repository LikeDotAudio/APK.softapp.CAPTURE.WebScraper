## Multi-Pattern Condenser Microphone

The RA-23 is a dual-pattern FET condenser that was sold as a DIY microphone kit. It has since been repackaged and renamed for the type of capsule included; the new versions (as of 2015) are called the S-47, S-12, and S-87.

[![RA-23 Cutaway View](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mparts/RA23-xray-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mparts/RA23-xray.jpg)The kit includes a transformerless circuit based on the Schoeps CMC-5. The two PCBs are made in the USA. The components include matched capacitors, specially selected and pre-biased Siliconix J305 JFET, low-noise Hitachi output transistors (also matched), and precision resistors.

The PCBs were designed to fit easily with the enclosed body, which is aluminum (anodized in black), with a tapered grille made of 2 layers of brass mesh. The use of two layers of different-gauge mesh offers a good compromise between plosive protection (in which more layers are beneficial), resonance reduction (ditto), and minimization of sonic reflections (in which fewer layers, and a more open weave would be preferred).

Any of 5 large-diaphragm capsules can be selected, including K47, K67, and CK-12 types. The capsule choice affects sensitivity and frequency response, but in all cases the sensitivity would be at least 27mV/Pa (and possibly as high as 45mV/Pa), and self-noise would be below 13dBA.

The polar pattern switch on the PCB removes the capsule’s rear diaphragm from the circuit entirely when in “True Cardioid” mode; this raises the sensitivity of the mic by ~4dB in Cardioid as compared to Omni.

The kit includes: microphone chassis and body, capsule mount, capsule, PCBs (unpopulated), all components necessary to build the circuit, US-made silver/teflon hookup wire, and a zippered storage pouch.
