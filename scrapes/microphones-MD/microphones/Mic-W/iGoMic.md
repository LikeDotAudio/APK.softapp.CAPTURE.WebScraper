## X/Y StereoMicW iGoMic

## X/Y Stereo Condenser Microphone

The iGoMic is a miniature stereo condenser microphone with a 3.5mm stereo output, designed for the GoPro camera and other audio-for-video applications.

Two miniature electret Cardioid condenser elements are mounted in a 90° array, producing an XY stereo image.

The iGoMic ships with a windscreen (p/n WS022), a mounting arm (PP011) with spare rubber mount (CL020), and two output cables:

* 3.5mm stereo male to mini USB, 30cm (p/n CB031U)
* 3.5mm stereo male to 3.5mm stereo male, 30cm (p/n CB030M)

[![iGoMic with GoPro](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images/mic_extras/mic-w/igomic-mounted-sm.jpg)](//cdn.recordinghacks.com/images//mic_extras/mic-w/igomic-mounted.jpg)The mounting arm is designed to facilitate connection of the iGoMic to the GoPro camera mount.

The iGoMic housing weighs 16g, and measure 51mm in length.
