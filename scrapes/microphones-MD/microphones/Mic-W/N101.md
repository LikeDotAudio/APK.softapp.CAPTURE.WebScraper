## Omnidirectional Pencil Condenser Microphone

The N101 is a medium-diaphragm FET condenser with an omnidirectional polar pattern.

The 23.8mm capsule uses a 5-micron nickel diaphragm. The capsule housing is made of stainless steel.

Like all the N-series microphones from Mic-W, the N101 uses a head amplifier design described as “transparent.”

Additional details are forthcoming.

Permalink: [MicW N101](//recordinghacks.com/microphones/Mic-W/N101)

The mic was released in 2009.
