## OmnidirectionalMicW CN201

## Omnidirectional Condenser Microphone

The MicW CN201 is a compact omnidirectional microphone that uses a half-inch, pre-polarized (electret) nickel-diaphragm capsule similar to the n201.

The CN201 has a nominally flat frequency response, with a soft lift (+2.5dB) centered at 10kHz. The mic’s self-noise is relatively high at 18dBA. Its sensitivity, 40mV/Pa, is very high.

The CN201 is similar in design to the cardioid CN255.

The CN201 is sold individually or in pairs. A lapel clip (CL012) is sold separately.
