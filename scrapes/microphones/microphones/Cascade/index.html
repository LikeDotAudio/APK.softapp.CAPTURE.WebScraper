Cascade Microphones

Cascade Microphones is the house brand of pro audio retailer Bayview Pro Audio of Olympia, Washington. The company began by selling imported Chinese microphones, then began offering upgraded versions of same, and is now constructing new microphones locally using parts from China, Sweden, and the USA.

All mics from Cascade carry a 5-year warranty. Ribbon elements and condenser capsules have a 1-year free-repair warranty, provided no modification attempts have been made.
