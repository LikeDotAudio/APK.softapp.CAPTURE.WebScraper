Ear Trumpet Labs

Combining high-quality transducer components with plumbing hardware, kitchen gadgets, and bicycle parts, Ear Trumpet Labs produces a small line of visually distinctive microphones for live and studio applications. Company founder Philip Graham designs and builds the Ear Trumpet products by hand in his Portland, OR based laboratory.
