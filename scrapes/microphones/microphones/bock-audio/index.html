Bock Audio

Bock Audio is David Bock’s pseudonymous microphone manufacturing company. It was founded in 2007, when Bock parted ways with Soundelux, where he’d run the microphone division. He retained all the rights to the microphones he had designed under the Soundelux name.

The company’s initial product lineup is comprised of mics designed under the Soundelux name: the U195, the E251C, and the Elux251.
